VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant market catalyst today, with reports of strong app demand driving AI stocks broadly higher. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Meta Connect Conference Wednesday night is being eagerly anticipated. Meta is described as one of the top-performing names in the entire market today.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging alongside other CPU-linked names on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This structural demand shift is seen as a major tailwind for CPU chipmakers. AMD is among the top outperformers in the tech sector today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis sparked by Meta Muse momentum. The market is repricing CPU-exposed names higher on the view that mainstream agentic AI will require substantially more compute power. Intel is explicitly named as a beneficiary of this theme.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding for PC unit volumes to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the PC supply chain. This is a notable negative data point for the broader PC market. HPQ is one of the standout underperformers on the session.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general to resolve the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, clearing a major legal hurdle. The stock is jumping sharply on the news alongside WBD. The deal reportedly involves preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the Paramount-Warner merger to proceed. This removes the biggest outstanding legal risk to the deal closing. WBD is among the top performers in the media sector today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for more specific guidance. The lack of detail overshadowed what was otherwise a forward-looking presentation on pipeline launches. This is a negative sentiment read for the stock near-term.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, driven by price promotions and package deals that are pulling in consumers. A Wall Street Journal report highlights Disney as a rare bright spot in the theme park sector. This is a modest positive data point for the stock.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times report highlighting poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility having hit a wall. This raises questions about the health of Oracle's hyperscale infrastructure buildout. The report is a near-term negative overhang for the stock.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and other potash-linked names including Mosaic and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below current Canadian potash rates. This is a direct pricing threat to North American fertilizer producers. The group is among the worst performers in the materials sector today.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with notable Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. Two macro themes are dominating: an AI-driven surge led by Meta Muse optimism and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off planned airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is running high ahead of the UN General Assembly in New York, where a potential Trump-Iran meeting is being floated.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve. The dollar backdrop is relatively calm. Treasury plans to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly relies on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt and above the Treasury's own 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is generating strong demand signals and could be a mainstream breakthrough for agentic AI, lifting the entire AI ecosystem including CPU-intensive chip names like AMD, ARM, and Intel. Oil is falling sharply on Houthi ceasefire hopes and potential diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly, removing a significant macro overhang. Treasuries are catching a modest bid alongside equities, suggesting a risk-on but not reckless environment. The Paramount-Warner merger clearing its legal hurdle adds a positive M&A sentiment boost to media.

BEAR CASE

The rally is narrow — Nasdaq is up over 150 basis points while the equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points, pointing to thin breadth underneath the surface. The US-China trade truce extension was not reached over the weekend, and the existing truce expires November 10, leaving a live tariff risk on the horizon. Geopolitical risks remain elevated — the Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend, Iran has warned of fierce retaliation if the US resumes strikes, and the Middle East situation is described by the State Department as having potential for unforeseen escalation. Apartment landlords face roughly $1.8 trillion in debt refinancing over the next decade at significantly higher rates, a slow-burning financial stress.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean for today's session, driven by dual tailwinds of AI enthusiasm and geopolitical de-escalation in oil. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Meta Connect Conference Wednesday night, which is pulling forward buying in AI and CPU-linked names today.

Sector Watch

Tech and AI names are sharply outperforming, led by CPU-linked stocks on agentic AI demand optimism. Energy is underperforming as Brent crude slumps roughly 400 basis points below $100 on Houthi ceasefire hopes. Potash and materials are weak on Trump's Belarus fertilizer supply deal commentary. Transports are lagging. REITs and data center industrials are outperforming alongside the broader AI theme. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points. Asset managers, travel and leisure, and select consumer discretionary names are also in the green.
