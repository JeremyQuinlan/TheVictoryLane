Morning Game Plan
Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 11:16 AM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant market catalyst today, with reports of strong early consumer demand for the agentic AI app. Investors are pre-positioning ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. Meta is described as one of the top-performing names in the entire market today.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging on the thesis that agentic AI applications, which Meta Muse could help push into the mainstream, are significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This creates a structural demand tailwind for CPU manufacturers. AMD is among the explicit outperformers cited in today's session.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis. The logic is straightforward: more compute-intensive AI workloads mean more CPU cycles, which is structurally bullish for traditional CPU players who have been under pressure. Intel is named explicitly as an outperformer today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. provided a cautious outlook, saying it anticipates PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar 2027. This is a negative demand signal for the PC supply chain and is driving HPQ lower today, making it a notable underperformer.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The settlement reportedly involves preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Shares are jumping sharply on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears a major legal obstacle to the Paramount-Warner merger proceeding. The deal had been in advanced settlement talks over the weekend and the formal agreement removes significant deal-break risk.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting. Investors were disappointed by the lack of specific guidance despite the company touting future blockbuster drug launches. The stock is underperforming on unmet expectations.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming its theme park peers, many of whom are seeing traffic headwinds. Disney is bucking the industry slump through price promotions and package deals that are driving attendance. This is a relative positive for Disney versus leisure peers.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article over the weekend focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about the company's aggressive data center financing strategy.

▼ MOS Mosaic Company Other

Potash-linked names including Mosaic are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below current Canadian potash rates. This is a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers and is hitting the entire fertilizer complex today.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher on Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points, and Russell 2000 +50 basis points. The two dominant themes are an AI/agentic stock surge tied to Meta Muse optimism and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism heading into the UN General Assembly in New York is also supporting risk sentiment, with hopes for progress on Middle East tensions including a potential Trump-Iran meeting.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the flight toward risk-off in energy and geopolitical relief rather than any Fed catalyst. Treasury is expected to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the roughly 20% target. No major Fed speakers or data are scheduled for today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is generating strong early demand signals and could be the product that breaks agentic AI into the mainstream consumer market, a structural positive for the entire AI ecosystem. CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel are catching a specific bid because agentic AI is significantly more compute-intensive than prior generations. Oil falling roughly 400 basis points on Houthi de-escalation relieves an inflationary pressure and improves the macro backdrop. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes an overhang and signals M&A activity can still get done.

BEAR CASE

The Nasdaq-heavy rally masks broad weakness — equal-weight S&P is only up 30 basis points, transports, energy, insurance, and materials are all underperforming meaningfully. Oil tanker costs are surging, the Middle East conflict continues to metastasize with new fronts opening weekly, and the US-China trade truce extension was not secured over the weekend. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech have risen roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, and HPQ's warning of a 5% decline in PC unit volumes in calendar 2027 signals consumer hardware demand remains fragile.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean on the session, driven by the dual tailwinds of AI/agentic enthusiasm and oil price relief. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night — Meta Muse demand signals are front-running that event and pulling the entire AI complex higher. Lingering geopolitical risk and the unresolved US-China trade truce extension are the main overhangs.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI/agentic names across semiconductors, software, and internet. CPU-linked stocks (AMD, ARM, INTC) are getting a specific structural re-rate on the agentic AI compute intensity thesis. Energy is a notable underperformer as Brent crude slumps roughly 400 basis points on Houthi de-escalation and diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly. Potash/fertilizers are being hit by Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. Transports are soft. REITs and data center-linked industrials are catching a bid. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points.
