Company Coverage
▲ CBRL Cracker Barrel Earnings beat + positive forward guidance

Cracker Barrel reported strong FQ4 upside across all key metrics: sales came in at $849.3MM vs. the Street at $834MM, adjusted EPS of 99c crushed the 16c consensus, and EBITDA of $62.1MM topped the $41.5MM estimate. Notably, even excluding tariff refunds, EBITDA of $53MM still beat expectations. Looking into F27, the EBITDA guidance midpoint of $190MM is ahead of the Street's $177MM forecast.

◆ GIS General Mills Earnings beat + guidance reaffirmed, but structural sector headwinds

General Mills posted FQ1 results that were better than feared: EPS of 75c (down 13% on a foreign-exchange neutral basis) beat the 72c consensus, organic revenue was roughly flat vs. the Street's -1.8% estimate, and all four business segments outperformed on revenue. Gross margins declined 0.90% and operating margins fell 1.30%, but both beat estimates, and prior full-year guidance was reaffirmed. Despite the solid execution, sentiment toward food staples is likely to remain weak given persistent cyclical and secular headwinds.

◆ MANU Manchester United Quarterly earnings beat offset by weak forward guidance

Manchester United posted modest FQ4 upside on sales of GBP 157.5MM vs. the Street's GBP 145MM, and EBITDA of GBP 28.9MM vs. the GBP 22MM estimate. However, the F27 EBITDA guidance midpoint of GBP 215MM fell well short of the Street's GBP 245MM expectation, which is a meaningful negative offset to the quarterly beat.

▲ MCD McDonald's Analyst Day — 2030 operating margin and strategy targets

McDonald's unveiled its 'McDonald's > NEXT' strategy at today's analyst meeting, headlined by an operating margin target of low-to-mid 50% by 2030 — solidly above the current consensus forecast of 49.5%. The company plans to increase capital spending to drive growth, which shifts free cash flow conversion lower to the mid-to-high 80% range by 2030, down from the prior low-to-mid 90% goal set in 2023. The margin target is the key bullish takeaway, though the higher capex and lower FCF conversion are modest offsets.
