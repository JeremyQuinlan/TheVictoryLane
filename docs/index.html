VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street at $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point gross margin tailwind from tariff refunds and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive comp linearity through the quarter. The opex ratio rose 100 basis points year-over-year to 33.4% of sales.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street at 36 cents, and crucially the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind still leaves EPS at 42 cents, well above consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales guidance trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged. Stock is an earnings-linked outperformer on the day.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street at 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% estimate. Revenue came in at $2.311 billion, down 8.4% year-over-year but slightly above the Street's $2.17 billion estimate. Management cited a difficult macro landscape as the primary headwind.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's Connect keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET, and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform — a meaningful commerce integration win. Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform, creating a competitive divide. Investor expectations into the keynote are building and Meta shares are outperforming on the day.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, positioning it as the preferred agentic commerce partner versus Amazon, which has blocked Muse. This is a meaningful distribution and monetization opportunity as agentic AI shopping scales. The partnership directly contrasts with Amazon's more closed approach.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The cuts are pressuring the entire lab testing space. Both Quest and Lab Corp are underperforming meaningfully on the day.

▼ LH Lab Corp Other

Lab Corp shares fell alongside Quest Diagnostics after CMS announced Medicare lab service rate cuts that were steeper than expected. The broader managed care and lab testing sectors are both under pressure today. The rate cut magnitude caught the market off guard.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's capabilities into the rapidly growing Physical AI and robotics segment. No earnings impact details were provided.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the study. The results reinforce Viking's position as a credible competitor in the obesity drug space. The data is a meaningful positive catalyst for the stock.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst day and outlined plans to achieve 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street at CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21% estimate. The company also adopted a $1 billion share buyback authorization. Both the revenue and margin targets came in above consensus expectations.
