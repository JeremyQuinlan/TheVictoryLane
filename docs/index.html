VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

Reported FQ4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point tariff refund tailwind and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit to gross margins. Domestic comps came in at plus 1.6% versus the Street's plus 3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened by 100 basis points year-over-year. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final eight weeks and expressed confidence in fiscal 2027 growth.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Announced the acquisition of RealSense, the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. RealSense is expected to generate $80-90 million in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth. The deal positions Cognex directly in the fast-growing physical AI and robotics ecosystem.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

Reported FQ1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and critically the beat holds even excluding an 11 cent tariff refund tailwind — clean EPS of 42 cents still topped consensus. Sales missed, declining 3.3% organically to $923.4 million versus the Street's $943.2 million, though orders rose 3.5% year-over-year. Fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed slightly while EPS guidance was left unchanged, and FQ2 EPS guidance midpoint is in line with consensus.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Named Bob Hau as its new CFO effective November 1, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman remaining through the transition date. Hau brings prior CFO experience at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International. The announcement is a routine leadership transition with no financial guidance update.

◆ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

Reported FQ4 EPS of 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, missing on the bottom line as gross margins fell 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% estimate. Revenue of $2.311 billion was actually slightly above the Street's $2.17 billion estimate but management's commentary was cautious, citing stubborn interest rates, fuel costs, and inflation pressuring household budgets. Bright spots include healthy dealer inventory levels, structural cost actions underway, and a roughly 75% year-over-year surge in North American towable RV order backlog.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Shares are spiking in pre-market trading after the company reported positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in trial participants. The result is a major positive data readout that validates the drug's efficacy profile and strengthens Viking's competitive position in the obesity treatment market. This is a significant binary catalyst event driving the pre-market move.
