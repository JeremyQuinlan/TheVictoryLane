VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — tariff refunds provided a 145 basis point gross margin tailwind and a non-cash LIFO impact added another 105 basis points. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive sales linearity in the quarter. Operating expenses worsened 100 basis points year-over-year, raising quality concerns despite the stock rallying.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — ex-refunds EPS was 42 cents, still comfortably above consensus. Guidance was mixed: fiscal 2027 sales range was trimmed but the EPS outlook was left unchanged. Stock is an earnings-linked outperformer on the day.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — margins fell 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited a difficult macro landscape. The miss was driven by margin compression rather than a volume shortfall.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET. Shopify announced it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform, a meaningful e-commerce integration win — though Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform. Investor expectations are growing increasingly bullish into the event.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, making it a key early partner in the agentic commerce ecosystem. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a differentiation advantage. The partnership could drive meaningful transaction volume if Muse adoption accelerates.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services representing a larger reduction than the market anticipated. The cuts are a direct headwind to revenue and margins for lab testing firms. Both Quest and LabCorp are underperforming on the news.

▼ LH LabCorp Other

LabCorp is getting hit alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS Medicare lab service rate cuts that came in worse than expected. The rate reductions represent a meaningful earnings headwind for the sector. Shares are underperforming on the day.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's capabilities into the fast-growing physical AI and robotics space. This is a meaningful strategic acquisition for the machine vision company.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the trial. This is a strong clinical readout that reinforces Viking's position in the competitive obesity drug market. The result is a meaningful positive catalyst for the stock.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst day and outlined targets for 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21%. The company also adopted a $1 billion share buyback authorization. The targets came in above consensus across the board.

Macro · Rates

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

AI-linked names are broadly outperforming as investor anticipation builds into a string of major catalysts. Iran apparently offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the US takes initial steps to ease military pressure, which could meaningfully reduce energy risk. Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline appears to be partially coming back online, easing crude supply concerns. Consumer staples, housing, and travel/leisure sectors are showing resilience, suggesting underlying demand holds up.

BEAR CASE

Financials — banks, brokers, insurers, and asset managers — are broadly underperforming, which is a concerning signal for market breadth. CMS rate cuts are hitting lab testing firms harder than anticipated, and managed care is also under pressure. Trump's approval rating has sunk to a career-low 32%, raising political uncertainty around policy. A delayed data center IPO and Texas freezing new data center permits signal that sentiment around the AI infrastructure buildout may be cooling at the margin.

Market Outlook

Neutral to cautiously optimistic lean. The market is treading water ahead of a dense AI catalyst window over the next 10 days — Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night, OpenAI's DevDay on September 29, and Micron earnings on September 30. Resolution of the Iran/Hormuz situation is the single biggest macro wildcard.
