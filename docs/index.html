VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO was pushed to November from October, which the WSJ described as 'later than many expected.' Year-end ARR projections came in at greater than $100-110 billion, below some bull expectations of $120 billion or higher. The company may launch a powerful new model ahead of the IPO to recapture momentum from OpenAI's Astra, and plans to have 5GW of compute by year-end doubling to 10GW by end of 2027.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times report focused on poor price action in debt tied to its massive 'Project Jupiter' data center facility in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have 'hit a wall,' raising concerns about Oracle's data center financing exposure.

◆ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its Warner Bros. Discovery merger deal. As part of the settlement, Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

◆ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

The Paramount-Warner deal faces a potential settlement with California's attorney general that could require divestitures of certain cable channels including Comedy Central and commitments around CNN's editorial independence. The outcome of these advanced talks will be a key overhang for the merger's completion.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure on Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The franchisee failure raises concerns about the health of Wendy's franchise network and consumer spending in the QSR segment.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk Activist Entry

Novo Nordisk is being pressured by shareholders to diversify away from GLP-1 exposure through M&A activity. The pressure reflects investor concern about over-concentration in the weight loss and diabetes drug category.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on 10 or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, a potential recovery catalyst for the troubled program after its earlier setbacks. This would represent meaningful new business for Boeing's space division and a competitive dynamic with SpaceX.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture will embed a team of evaluators to monitor safety and alignment at Anthropic, a notable enterprise AI governance contract. The arrangement reflects growing institutional demand for AI oversight services and positions Accenture as a key player in the AI safety ecosystem.

▼ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores as the grocery giant pushes back against supplier price hikes. The move signals intensifying pressure on consumer staples brands as large retailers leverage their purchasing power in an inflationary environment.

▲ NSCL Nscale Other

London-based neocloud firm Nscale filed its S1 publicly on Friday afternoon and aims to list on the NYSE under the ticker NSCL. The filing adds to a growing pipeline of AI infrastructure IPOs and will be closely watched as a read on investor appetite for neocloud compute plays.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by escalating Middle East tensions and a cluster of negative AI industry headlines. The Houthis dramatically ratcheted up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities, worsening the geopolitical backdrop and threatening energy infrastructure. Ukraine also launched a massive drone attack on Russia over the weekend, adding further instability to global risk tone.

Global monetary policy remains pointed in a hawkish direction, with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month. The BOJ's latest hike was slightly more dovish than anticipated, but it placed downward pressure on the yen and rekindled fears about Japan selling Treasuries to defend its currency. Markets are pricing greater than 50% odds of additional hikes from multiple central banks including the RBA, RBNZ, BoC, FOMC, ECB, and BOE over the coming weeks. Kansas City Fed President Schmid warned Friday that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Any geopolitical improvement in the Middle East — even incremental — could trigger an outsized drop in energy prices with favorable knock-on effects including lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The UN General Assembly this week brings together officials from major Middle Eastern countries including Iran, creating a window for diplomatic progress. The upcoming Trump/Xi summit could produce select tariff reductions on roughly $30 billion worth of goods, providing a modest positive catalyst. China and Pakistan are reportedly urging Iran to rein in the Houthis, suggesting growing international pressure toward de-escalation.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical landscape is deteriorating rapidly, with Iran tightening its grip on global energy supply via Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and the Houthis now escalating attacks on Saudi Arabia. Energy prices continue to spike, with diesel shortages and motor oil rationing already appearing in the US, and the economic damage will increasingly work through the system. The AI industry faces a fresh wave of negative sentiment — Anthropic's IPO was pushed to November, OpenAI projects negative $280 billion in free cash flow through 2030, and Big Tech's off-balance sheet AI liability exposure rose roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months. Monetary policy globally remains hawkish with multiple central banks still hiking, leaving little room for a dovish pivot to rescue risk assets.
