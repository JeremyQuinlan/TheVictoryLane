▲ C Citigroup Earnings Beat

Citigroup posted Q3 EPS of $2.85, up 27% year-over-year and ahead of the Street estimate of $2.65. Revenue came in at $23.85 billion versus the $23.72 billion estimate, with expense discipline pushing the efficiency ratio to 59% versus the expected 60.7%. The company raised full-year NII guidance to mid-to-high 6% growth (from 5-6%) and lifted ROTCE guidance to 11% (from 10-11%), while equity underwriting surged 77% year-over-year.
