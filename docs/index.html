VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street at $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point tariff refund tailwind and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit to gross margins. Domestic comps came in at plus 1.6% versus the Street's plus 3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100 basis points year-over-year. Management noted that comp linearity improved through the quarter, which is providing some relief and lifting shares.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at the Meta Connect conference Wednesday night at 7pm ET. Shopify announced it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful distribution win as Amazon has blocked Muse from its own platform. Investor expectations are building bullishly into the event, and the stock is outperforming on the day.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases directly on its e-commerce platform, positioning Shopify as a key partner in the agentic commerce ecosystem. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a competitive edge in agentic checkout adoption.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street at 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind, EPS was 42 cents, still well above consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales guidance trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged. The stock is an earnings-linked outperformer today.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness of 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management explicitly cited a difficult macro landscape, raising concerns about the consumer discretionary environment. The stock is an earnings-linked underperformer.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics and Lab Corp shares were hit following new Medicare payment rates from CMS for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. Both stocks are underperforming meaningfully on the day as investors reprice the earnings impact of the rate cuts.

▼ LH Lab Corp Sector Reprice

Lab Corp is selling off alongside Quest Diagnostics after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that came in more negative than expected. The sector reprice is broader than just one name, reflecting a systemic headwind for lab testing companies.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the study. This is a strong clinical readout that keeps Viking competitive in the increasingly crowded but high-growth obesity drug space.

▲ BABA Alibaba Product Launch

Alibaba unveiled what it called China's most powerful AI chip and hinted at plans to train a model with up to 10 trillion parameters, while management outlined plans to operate more than 20 gigawatts of data center capacity by 2032. The announcement positions Alibaba as a serious domestic AI infrastructure competitor and is a positive catalyst for the stock.

▲ CPRI Capri Holdings M&A

A WWD article published Tuesday speculates that Capri Holdings, the parent of Michael Kors and Jimmy Choo, may be exploring a sale. No deal has been confirmed, but the report is generating M&A speculation around the stock.
