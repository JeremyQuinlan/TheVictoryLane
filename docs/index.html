VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top performers in the entire market Monday as enthusiasm builds around Meta Muse, with reports of strong demand for the agentic AI app. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is being closely anticipated as a potential catalyst for further upside. OpenAI is reportedly considering launching a competing product, validating the significance of the Muse launch.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is rallying sharply on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the consumer mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This positions CPU makers as direct beneficiaries of the agentic AI wave. AMD is explicitly called out as a top outperformer in the session.

▲ ARM Arm Holdings Sector Reprice

ARM is benefiting from the same agentic AI CPU intensity thesis driving AMD and Intel higher. The market is repricing CPU-linked semiconductor names broadly on the view that agentic AI workloads require far more processing power than prior AI generations. ARM is among the named outperformers in the tech complex.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD and ARM as the agentic AI CPU intensity narrative gains traction Monday. The Meta Muse catalyst is acting as a rising tide for the CPU ecosystem broadly. Intel's inclusion as a named outperformer is notable given its recent underperformance relative to peers.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The settlement reportedly involves preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Shares are jumping sharply on the news as the merger path is now significantly clearer.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears a major legal obstacle to their merger. The deal had been in advanced settlement talks over the weekend and the agreement removes a key overhang that had weighed on both stocks. This is one of the bigger single-stock moves in media Monday.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. The cautious outlook is weighing on the broader PC ecosystem and contributing to underperformance in the hardware segment. This contrasts sharply with the AI and CPU enthusiasm driving the rest of tech higher.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company's Monday analyst meeting disappointed investors by failing to provide more granular financial targets. The lack of specificity at what was expected to be a catalyst event is being treated as a miss by the market. The stock underperformed European peers meaningfully.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT report Friday focused on poor price action in debt tied to its massive data center in New Mexico, known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about the health of Oracle's data center financing. This is a meaningful negative overhang for a stock that has traded heavily on AI infrastructure optimism.

▼ MOS Mosaic Company Other

Potash names including Mosaic, CF Industries, and Nutrien slumped after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus with pricing substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct competitive threat to North American potash producers and hit the group hard. The group is among the worst performers in the session.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with a strong tech-led rally — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points, signaling a narrow, AI-driven move. Oil is tumbling roughly 400 basis points to below $100 a barrel after Trump called off planned airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is running high ahead of the UN General Assembly in New York, with hopes for Middle East de-escalation and a potential Trump-Iran meeting.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting mild risk-off in rates even as equities surge. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the 20% target. No major Fed commentary is driving the session.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse agentic AI narrative is generating genuine excitement and pulling up the entire AI ecosystem, from semiconductors to cloud infrastructure. Trump backing off Houthi airstrikes and diplomatic hopes at the UN General Assembly are driving a sharp oil selloff, which acts as a tax cut for the consumer and economy broadly. Treasuries are bid, yields are slipping, and crypto is surging — risk appetite is broadly constructive even if the equity rally is narrow.

BEAR CASE

The rally is extremely concentrated — the equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points versus Nasdaq at 153 basis points, a sign that breadth is weak and the move is vulnerable to rotation or disappointment. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured over the weekend despite 'very successful' talks, leaving a November 10 cliff unresolved. Geopolitical risk remains elevated — Houthi escalation against Saudi Arabia, Iran warnings, and oil tanker shortages all represent tail risks that could reprice quickly.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously bullish on a narrow, AI-theme basis — the Meta Muse catalyst and agentic AI narrative are clearly the dominant force today, but the rally is highly concentrated in mega-cap tech and CPU-linked names while energy, transports, and materials drag. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night and any further development on the US-China trade truce, which expires November 10.

Sector Watch

AI and CPU-linked semiconductors are the standout outperformers driven by the agentic AI narrative around Meta Muse. Energy is underperforming broadly as oil drops nearly 400 basis points on Houthi de-escalation hopes. Potash and materials are under pressure from Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. Transports are lagging with rails and delivery names notably weak. REITs and data center industrials are catching a bid alongside the AI theme. Travel and leisure names are outperforming as geopolitical tension eases.
