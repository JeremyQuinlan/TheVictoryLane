VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO was pushed from October to November, seen as later than many expected. Year-end ARR projections came in at greater than 100 to 110 billion dollars, slightly below the most bullish expectations of 120 billion dollars or higher. The company is considering launching a powerful new model ahead of the IPO to recapture momentum lost to OpenAI's Astra.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising concerns about Oracle's data center financing exposure.

▲ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California to resolve a state AG lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. A settlement could involve Paramount agreeing to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Progress here removes a key regulatory overhang on the deal.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery's deal with Paramount moves closer to resolution as Paramount enters advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general. A settlement requiring divestitures of certain cable assets and CNN editorial independence protections could clear the path for deal completion.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. This raises concerns about franchisee financial health and could signal stress in the broader quick-service restaurant consumer environment.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk M&A

Novo Nordisk is facing shareholder pressure to diversify away from its concentrated GLP-1 exposure through M&A. While this reflects confidence in the core business's cash generation, it also signals investor concern about single-product concentration risk.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on 10 or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, a meaningful potential contract win that could rehabilitate Starliner's reputation after prior setbacks. This puts Boeing in renewed competition with SpaceX for NASA commercial crew missions.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture announced it will embed a team of evaluators at Anthropic to monitor safety and alignment. This positions Accenture as a key enterprise partner in the AI safety infrastructure buildout and reinforces its deepening ties with frontier AI labs.

◆ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores nationwide as the grocery giant pushes back against supplier price hikes. This signals Kroger is willing to use shelf space as leverage against premium-priced consumer brands and could pressure other suppliers.

▼ VOWG Volkswagen Guidance Cut

Volkswagen issued a large downside preannouncement for 2026 operating margins, now guiding to up to 1 percent versus the Street's roughly 4.1 percent estimate and prior guidance of 4 to 5.5 percent. The miss is driven by a goodwill impairment at Porsche, a challenging auto market especially in China, and additional restructuring charges.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by escalating Middle East conflict and a cluster of negative AI industry headlines. The Houthis dramatically ratcheted up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities, raising fears of further energy supply disruption. Ukraine also launched a massive drone strike on Russia including Moscow, adding to geopolitical risk heading into the week.

Global monetary policy remains pointed in a hawkish direction, with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month. The BOJ's latest hike was slightly more dovish than expected but triggered concern about yen weakness and the potential for Japan to sell Treasuries to defend its currency. Kansas City Fed President Schmid issued hawkish remarks Friday, warning that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil prices.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Even a small improvement in Middle East geopolitics could trigger an outsized drop in energy prices with favorable knock-on effects — lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The UN General Assembly brings together key Middle East players including Iran, creating at least an opportunity for diplomatic progress. The Trump/Xi summit could deliver modest tariff reductions on roughly 30 billion dollars of goods, providing a tailwind for trade-sensitive names. Saudi Arabia is also increasing ship-to-ship oil transfers to help compensate for supply shortages, showing some adaptive capacity.

BEAR CASE

Iran continues to tighten its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and the Houthis in Yemen, with attacks on Saudi Arabia escalating sharply over the weekend. The economy and corporate earnings have held up over the summer, but the latest spike in energy prices will begin working through the system and could trigger meaningful demand destruction. AI sentiment took multiple hits after the Friday close — a delayed Anthropic IPO, disappointing ARR projections, OpenAI's staggering negative free cash flow forecast of 280 billion dollars through 2030, and rising Big Tech off-balance sheet AI liability exposure. Global central banks remain hawkish with rate hike odds above 50 percent at multiple upcoming meetings.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautious-to-bearish heading into the week. The combination of worsening Middle East geopolitics, negative AI sentiment, and a globally hawkish central bank backdrop creates headwinds. The key catalysts to watch are the Trump/Xi summit Thursday and the UN General Assembly, where any diplomatic progress on Iran could be an outsized positive for risk assets, but a breakthrough is hard to imagine.

Sector Watch

AI and Big Tech face a cluster of negative sentiment drivers including rising off-balance sheet liability exposure up roughly 300 billion dollars in the last 12 months, OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of 280 billion dollars through 2030, and regulatory noise around safety and alignment. Energy remains the swing factor for the entire market — geopolitical risk from the Iran conflict is the dominant macro driver, with diesel and motor oil prices surging and supplies being rationed. Auto sector faces pressure from Volkswagen's major guidance cut and a challenging China market. Media stocks are in focus around the Paramount-Warner deal regulatory progress.
