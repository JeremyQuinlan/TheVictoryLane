VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone beat fiscal Q4 EPS at $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but quality was low — the beat was driven by tariff refunds adding 145 basis points to gross margins and a non-cash LIFO impact adding 105 basis points. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive sales linearity through the quarter, which is providing some reassurance to investors.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported strong fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — adjusting for the 11-cent tailwind, EPS was still 42 cents versus 36 cents expected. Guidance was mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, with gross margin weakness of 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% more than offsetting slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management explicitly flagged a difficult macro landscape, which is a cautious signal for the RV industry.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect Wednesday night at 7pm ET, with investor expectations building. Shopify confirmed it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform — in contrast to Amazon, which blocked Muse — giving Meta a meaningful e-commerce distribution win.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a notable partnership win that differentiates Shopify from Amazon, which blocked Muse. This positions Shopify as a preferred destination for agentic AI commerce.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit following new Medicare payment rates from CMS for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The negative rate surprise is pressuring the entire lab testing group.

▼ LH Lab Corp Other

Lab Corp shares fell alongside Quest Diagnostics after CMS released new Medicare lab service payment rates with cuts deeper than expected. Both names are under meaningful selling pressure on the reimbursement news.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. This is a direct bet on the physical AI and robotics buildout.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, including 22% weight loss, a strong efficacy readout that keeps the company competitive in the increasingly crowded obesity drug space.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst day and outlined a plan to reach 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins above 22% versus the Street's 21% estimate. The company also authorized a $1 billion share buyback, adding a capital return element to the growth story.
