MARKET OUTLOOK

The macro backdrop is net negative, with Middle East escalation pushing oil higher and acting as a tax on the consumer — Axios pegs the extra energy burden at ~$100B for American households. The real intraday story is the violent rotation out of software/services and into AI infrastructure hardware; that pivot feels durable given the capex cycle still has legs (Foxconn's +52% August sales growth is not an anomaly). Defense names are catching a bid on legitimate geopolitical risk, not just momentum, and that bid deserves respect. Watch the Apple event Wednesday for a sentiment read on consumer tech — if the foldable lands well, it reframes the hardware upgrade cycle heading into year-end.

COMPANY NEWS

ABM reported Q2 EPS of $1.04 vs. Street $1.01, with free cash flow of $128.4MM crushing the $43MM estimate. Full-year EPS guidance raised to $3.95–$4.10; organic revenue growth of +2.1% missed the +2.3% consensus slightly on macro timing headwinds.

BSX (Boston Scientific) warned it will not meet prior Q3 and full-year 2026 guidance due to a cybersecurity incident, though systems restoration is underway.

GE (GE Aerospace) is acquiring Consolidated Precision Products from Warburg Pincus and Berkshire Partners for $11.75B (26x headline, 18x including synergies), which is weighing on supplier HWM.

GLW (Corning) signed a multi-billion dollar agreement to supply 80+ million miles of high-density optical fiber to Verizon from 2027–2032.

QCOM (Qualcomm) announced a multi-generation AI chip collaboration with Amazon for large-scale data centers; Amazon receives warrants for up to 25M shares at $161.26, fully vesting on $60B in cumulative purchases.

GME (GameStop) confirmed Q2 results in line with its August 31 preannouncement and raised full-year EBITDA guidance to >$650MM from >$600MM.

NVS (Novartis) shares fell sharply after a second consecutive negative data readout — this one for myotonic dystrophy type 1 (acquired via the $12B Avidity deal) — on top of the failed cholesterol study with IONS (Ionis) announced Friday (WSJ, FT). AMGN is also under pressure from the negative Novartis cholesterol data.

CRCL (Circle) is acquiring Singapore-based B2B payments firm Tazapay for $400MM in stock.

WAFD will acquire EverBank in a $3.9B deal; the combined company will trade as "EVBK" with ~29% EPS accretion expected in 2027.

PM (Philip Morris) nudged full-year EPS guidance higher on a larger FX tailwind.

GIS (General Mills) reaffirmed F27 guidance.

SYK (Stryker) cautioned at the Wells Fargo conference that supply headwinds in peripheral vascular persist and hip replacement seasonality is running above expectations.

UNFI reported FQ4 EPS of 69c vs. Street 61c, but EBITDA of $172MM was inline and sales of $7.64B missed the $7.7B estimate; F27 guidance mid-points disappoint on both EPS and revenue.

GOOGL (Google) received strong marks from SemiAnalysis, which found its TPU v7/Ironwood delivers 50% better price-performance than Nvidia's B200/B300.

TSLA (Tesla) gained regulatory approval for FSD in Slovenia, its 6th European market (Reuters).

HOOD (Robinhood) designated OG.com as infrastructure and clearing provider for its Prediction Markets offering.

JEF (Jefferies): its Point Bonita fund holds nearly $500MM in exposure to troubled Radiant World, more than previously disclosed (FT).
