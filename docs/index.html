VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top performers in the entire market today as Meta Muse demand signals are strong and investors anticipate Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. OpenAI is reportedly considering a Muse-like product, validating Meta's first-mover positioning in agentic AI for consumers. The stock is functioning as the primary catalyst for the broader AI/tech rally.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging alongside ARM and Intel on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring into the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This creates a structural demand uplift for CPU makers beyond the GPU-centric narrative that has dominated. The agentic computing thesis is acting as a sector-wide re-rating catalyst.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD and ARM as the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis gains traction on the back of Meta Muse momentum. This is a meaningful narrative shift that could provide Intel with a more credible AI demand story after years of market share erosion. The stock is catching a bid it has struggled to sustain in recent cycles.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with the settlement reportedly including preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The deal clears a major regulatory hurdle and is sending both Paramount and WBD shares sharply higher. This removes a significant overhang on the merger timeline.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are jumping alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the merger to proceed. The removal of this legal block is the most concrete merger progress in some time and is being received enthusiastically by the market. Both names are among the top performers in the media sector today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after warning that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that weighs on the broader PC supply chain. This is a headwind for component makers and undercuts any near-term recovery narrative in consumer PC demand. The stock is one of the notable underperformers in tech today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for greater visibility on the GLP-1 growth runway. The lack of specificity on forward targets is being read as a sign of uncertainty in the business outlook. This is a negative relative to elevated investor expectations.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article highlighting poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility having hit a wall. This raises questions about the financial engineering underpinning Oracle's aggressive data center expansion strategy. It is a negative read for sentiment around the stock heading into the week.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, with the company using price promotions and package deals to sustain attendance. This is a constructive data point for Disney's highest-margin segment and differentiates it positively from travel and leisure peers facing consumer softness. The news adds support to the bull case on Disney's parks recovery narrative.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and fellow potash names Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially less than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct demand and pricing threat to North American potash producers and is hitting the entire fertilizer complex hard. The geopolitical dimension — engaging Belarus — adds further complexity to the trade.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. Two dominant themes are driving the session: an AI stock explosion led by Meta Muse optimism ahead of Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night, and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday. Diplomatic optimism is running high into the UN General Assembly this week, with hopes Trump could meet Iran's president to cool Middle East tensions.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on but not euphoric tone. The dollar is benefiting from geopolitical de-escalation hopes rather than any fresh Fed catalyst. Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly leans on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the Treasury's 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is generating genuine demand signals and dragging the entire AI ecosystem higher, with CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel getting a second-order boost from the thesis that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior generations. Oil falling sharply reduces inflation risk and acts as a tax cut for consumers and industrials. Diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly, including a potential Trump-Iran meeting, could further compress the geopolitical risk premium. Treasuries rallying alongside equities suggests this is a clean risk-on move rather than a flight to safety.

BEAR CASE

The Nasdaq's massive outperformance versus the equal-weight S&P — 153 basis points versus 30 basis points — signals this is a narrow, momentum-driven rally rather than broad market strength. The US-China one-year trade truce expires November 10 and no extension was agreed to over the weekend, leaving a significant overhang. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech have risen roughly 300 billion dollars in just the last 12 months, a growing structural risk. HPQ's warning that PC unit volumes could fall 5% in calendar 2027 is a reminder that consumer tech demand outside of AI remains fragile.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean today, driven by a powerful combination of AI enthusiasm around agentic computing and geopolitical de-escalation in the Middle East. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night — any details on Meta Muse adoption or agentic AI capabilities could extend or deflate the current tech rally. The US-China trade truce extension not being confirmed yet is a latent risk.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU-linked names on Meta Muse optimism. Energy is a notable underperformer as oil falls roughly 400 basis points below $100 on Houthi de-escalation hopes and diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly. Potash and fertilizer names are getting hit hard on a potential Trump-Belarus supply deal. REITs and data center industrials are outperforming, partly benefiting from lower rates and AI infrastructure demand. Transports are lagging across the board. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points.
