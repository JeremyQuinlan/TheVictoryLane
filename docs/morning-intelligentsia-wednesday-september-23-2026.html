Macro

US equity futures are essentially flat Wednesday morning — S&P +0.05%, Dow +0.02%, Nasdaq -0.04%, and Russell 2000 -0.27%. Asian markets were mixed overnight with Hong Kong notably weak (Hang Seng -1.01%) dragged by Alibaba (-4.4%) and Tencent (-2.4%), while Korea and Taiwan outperformed. Treasuries are flat across the curve, Brent crude is flat near $99.50 after recent selling pressure, and gold is down 1.0% while silver drops 2.5% as markets await developments on US-Iran negotiations and tonight's Meta Connect keynote.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously mixed — the market is in a holding pattern waiting on two binary catalysts: Iran diplomacy progress and Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote at 7pmET. Muse/AI enthusiasm has been a powerful bullish driver for semis and pick-and-shovels names in recent days, but it has simultaneously pressured software, financials, and select consumer names perceived as AI disruption targets. If Zuckerberg delivers bullish Muse and Watermelon commentary tonight and Iran diplomacy continues to progress, that's the bull case; stalling on either front keeps the market rangebound-to-weak. The Trump/Xi summit Thursday is viewed as low-impact (status quo most likely outcome).

Sector Watch

AI/Semis (pick-and-shovels) have been surging on Muse enthusiasm, but the same AI wave is pressuring software/services names (ADBE, BKNG, INTU flagged as disruption risks) as well as financials — specifically retail brokers and wealth managers. Refinery stocks saw selling pressure Tuesday after Trump signaled support for a diesel export ban. Homebuilders face macro headwinds from elevated mortgage rates and weakening buyer sentiment per KBH commentary, though LEN outperformed Tuesday after Berkshire boosted its stake above 10%.

Company Coverage
▲ META Meta Platforms Earnings/Event — Meta Connect keynote tonight 7pmET

Zuckerberg delivers the Meta Connect keynote tonight at 7pmET, with investors expecting bullish commentary on Muse adoption (500K+ users and 250K+ DAUs in its first week) and the forthcoming frontier model Watermelon. The Connect conference historically focused on smart glasses, but AI narrative is what truly moves the stock. There is some risk that OpenAI uses its upcoming Dev Day to launch a competing personal agent and steal momentum.

▼ AVGO Broadcom Geopolitical/Regulatory — Chinese government guidance on domestic chip sourcing

China may issue formal guidance ordering data centers to reduce use of Broadcom hardware as Beijing pushes the tech industry toward domestic products. This is a meaningful demand headwind for AVGO's China business. The news adds geopolitical risk on top of an already complex macro backdrop for US semiconductor suppliers operating in China.

◆ KBH KB Home Earnings — FQ3 results and FQ4 guidance

KB Home reported FQ3 EPS of $1.05 vs. the Street's $0.89, with the beat driven largely by cost controls (SG&A ratio beat by ~0.30%) and to a lesser extent slightly higher gross margins and modestly better deliveries. However, the FQ4 guide is mixed-to-underwhelming — deliveries roughly inline but gross margin and SG&A ratio both a bit worse. Management flagged a challenging housing market with conditions weakening since June, citing higher mortgage rates, geopolitical uncertainty, and broader economic headwinds.

▲ RCL Royal Caribbean M&A — $3B acquisition of 50% stake in Sandals

Royal Caribbean confirmed it will acquire a 50% stake in Sandals Resorts for $3B, representing a forward EBITDA multiple of approximately 10x. Management said the deal will be accretive to earnings next year. The acquisition expands RCL's all-inclusive resort footprint and diversifies its hospitality revenue stream.

▲ BHF Brighthouse Financial M&A — Sixth Street takeover interest reported

Brighthouse shares rallied in the final minutes of Tuesday's session after Bloomberg reported that Sixth Street remains interested in acquiring BHF should the Acquarian deal fall apart. This provides a potential M&A floor under the stock. The situation adds optionality for shareholders if the primary deal collapses.

▼ NFLX Netflix Analyst Action — second downgrade in one week

Netflix shares were hit Tuesday after receiving its second downgrade in a week — HSBC cut to Hold, following Wells Fargo's downgrade to Underweight on September 18. Investor sentiment is deteriorating amid concerns about engagement and a weak content lineup. Netflix reports earnings on October 20.

▼ LH Labcorp Regulatory — CMS Medicare payment rate cuts

Labcorp shares fell 3% Tuesday on concerns about reduced CMS Medicare payment rates, which management says 'would impose steep annual payment cuts that threaten patient access to laboratory services.' The company is reaffirming its 2026-2029 long-term guidance from the September 10 analyst meeting, though the 2027 guidance impact will not be quantified until February. The CMS rate risk creates a meaningful overhang.

▲ FUN Six Flags Entertainment Activist — Jana Partners pushing for a sale

Jana Partners is pressuring Six Flags' board to consider a sale of the company. Activist involvement from a credible hedge fund typically provides a near-term floor and potential upside if a deal materializes. The situation puts strategic alternatives squarely in focus.

▲ WOR Worthington Enterprises Earnings — FQ1 EPS, EBITDA, and sales beat

Worthington reported modest FQ1 upside with EPS of $0.82 vs. the Street's $0.75 and EBITDA of $74M vs. $69.5M expected, while sales grew 13% to $344M vs. the Street's $332M. Management highlighted rapidly growing demand for engineered ASME tanks used in liquid cooling systems for data centers as a key growth driver. A clean beat with a positive AI infrastructure tailwind narrative.

◆ MSFT Microsoft Product — Copilot 'super app' launch imminent, bulk discount ramp

Microsoft is ramping up bulk discounts for corporate Copilot buyers and is preparing to launch a refreshed 'super app' version of the product — potentially as soon as this week — that will combine coding tools and personal AI agents. The move signals urgency to drive Copilot adoption in a competitive AI agent landscape. Increased discounting could pressure near-term monetization but is strategically aimed at capturing enterprise share before OpenAI and others.
