MARKET OUTLOOK

This is a bounce after three straight down sessions, not a breakout — don't confuse the two. The absence of a clear catalyst is telling, and the fact that CRDO, MDB, and PANW are selling off despite beating Street estimates suggests expectations embedded in many AI-linked names are simply too elevated to clear. The bond market's global vote of no-confidence (WSJ) is a structural headwind that doesn't resolve in a single session. Watch the August jobs report Friday and next week's CPI — those two prints will determine whether the Fed hikes on 9/16 or holds.

GEOPOLITICAL

- The US blockade of Iran is working where sanctions never could — for the first time in history, Iran has gone seven weeks without shipping material amounts of crude through the Strait of Hormuz, effectively strangling its energy sector (Reuters). Russia has been helping Iran develop advanced supersonic cruise missiles since 2023, and Putin has vowed continued support for Tehran (FT, Washington Post).

- Pakistan says it remains engaged with both the US and Iran on ceasefire talks and is hopeful both sides "will return to the table for dialogue" (Al Arabiya).

- G20 nations are openly grumbling that US tariff policy and the Iran war are among the biggest obstacles facing the global economy (NYT). The US-Canada trade war shows no signs of resolution and could drag past the midterms (Politico).

- New Zealand's central bank hiked 25 basis points to 2.75%, as expected, citing elevated inflation risks (WSJ). BOJ Governor Ueda signaled a strong likelihood of a rate hike this month, while hawk Hajime Takata floated the possibility of hikes exceeding 25 basis points or at consecutive meetings (Reuters, Bloomberg).

COMPANY NEWS

DELL reported blowout FQ2 results and raised full-year guidance, citing robust demand across both AI and non-AI markets. The stock is higher on earnings but has faded from overnight highs.

GTLB (GitLab) posted EPS of 24c vs. the Street's 18c, with sales up 21% to $286.3MM against expectations of $273.3MM. Operating margins of 15% beat the Street's 11.2% estimate, and full-year guidance was nudged higher.

MDB (MongoDB) delivered FQ2 EPS of $2.03 vs. $1.61 expected, with revenue up 30% to $772MM vs. $735MM estimated and operating margins roughly 300 basis points above plan. Full-year guidance moves higher — yet the stock is selling off, which says everything about positioning.

CRDO (Credo) posted FQ1 EPS of $1.20 vs. $1.16 expected, with sales up 114.7% to $479MM vs. $474MM estimated and gross margins in-line at 68%. FQ2 guidance midpoints are slightly ahead of consensus. Selling off anyway — the AI expectations bar is brutally high.

PANW (Palo Alto Networks) beat on all FQ4 KPIs: Next-Gen Security ARR surged 64% to $9.1B vs. $8.8B expected. F27 guide is slightly ahead on both ARR ($11.125B vs. $10.9B) and EPS ($4.17 vs. $4.11). Also selling off on earnings.

NVDA (Nvidia) is in advanced talks to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $14B (Bloomberg).

OLLI (Ollie's Bargain Outlet) reported FQ2 EPS of $1.42 vs. $1.12 expected. Gross margins expanded 360 basis points, aided by a 380 basis point tailwind from IEEPA tariff refunds, offsetting a 1.8% comp decline. Full-year EPS guidance moves higher; comp guidance trimmed to 0–0.5% from +2%.

BF/B (Brown-Forman) posted FQ1 EPS of 38c vs. 37c expected; revenue fell 1% to $911MM vs. $913.5MM estimated. Whiskey was flat, tequila down 13%, Ready-to-Drink up 11%. Full-year guidance reaffirmed.

GIII (G-III Apparel) earned 26c vs. 23c expected as gross margin expansion of 440 basis points offset a 10% revenue decline to $554MM vs. $570MM. Full-year EPS midpoint raised to $2.25 from $2.20; Q3 guide disappoints.

UBER will cut approximately 3,000 jobs (~10% of global headcount) in a restructuring aimed at slashing management layers by 20% (Bloomberg).

VRT (Vertiv) is acquiring UtilityInnovation Group, a microgrid and data center power solutions firm, for up to $2.6B including $1.45B cash at close, representing 13x EBITDA.

CVX (Chevron) formally announced a $7B investment plan in Venezuela over five years, targeting production of ~600K barrels per day — though industry skepticism about the deal's viability runs high (Politico).

F (Ford) reported August sales down 10.3% year-over-year to 170,680 units: EVs -79%, hybrids -19.9%, ICE -4.6%.

GOOGL (Google) could release Gemini 3.8 Flash as soon as Wednesday, with notably improved coding benchmarks in internal testing. Separately, Google is now releasing two TPU generations per year, up from one every two years (WSJ, Nikkei).

PCG (PG&E) plans to defer ~$2B of California investment while still committing ~$11.4B in 2027, and will launch a strategic review covering regulatory, financial, and operational alternatives.
