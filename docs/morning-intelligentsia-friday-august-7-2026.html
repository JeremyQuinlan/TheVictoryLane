MARKET OUTLOOK

The setup into Friday is constructive: after two days of disappointing tech prints, Thursday night's earnings slate was broadly solid, and TEAM's blowout is giving software stocks a much-needed lifeline. We think a Fed rate hike could ultimately be *positive* for equities — it would help cap long-end yields that have drifted higher on credibility concerns. The jobs report is the key near-term catalyst; a soft number helps Treasuries in the immediate term, but a robust 175K+ print that locks in a September hike may paradoxically be the better outcome for 10s and 30s and therefore equity sentiment.

COMPANY NEWS

TEAM (Atlassian) delivered a blowout FQ4 with EPS of $1.87 vs. the Street's $1.50, driven by robust margins and strong sales. RPOs surged 44% to $4.8B vs. the Street $4.0B. The CEO plans to personally buy $250M of stock, pushing back hard on "SaaSpocalypse" fears (FT).

TTD (Trade Desk) was the ugly exception Thursday night — Q2 EBITDA came in at $241MM vs. $261MM expected, revenue missed at $715MM vs. $752MM, and EBITDA margins compressed 500 basis points year-over-year. Q3 guidance is deeply soft: sales/EBITDA of $650MM/$160MM vs. the Street's $808MM/$342MM.

NET (Cloudflare) posted revenue of $696MM (+36% Y/Y) vs. the Street's $666MM, with full-year sales guidance raised to $2.864–2.87B from $2.805–2.813B.

MCHP (Microchip) beat FQ1 EPS at 76c vs. 70c expected and guided Q2 EPS to 91–95c vs. the Street's 80c, with sales guidance of $1.589–1.618B vs. $1.56B.

TWLO (Twilio) earned $1.47 vs. $1.32 expected on revenue of $1.5B (+22%), raising full-year organic sales growth guidance to +13–13.5% from +9.5–10.5%.

ABNB (Airbnb) beat on EBITDA with bookings of $27.2B vs. $26.4B expected. Q3 sales guided to $4.69–4.77B vs. the Street's $4.6B.

DKNG (DraftKings) missed Q2 EBITDA badly — $115MM vs. $160MM — on customer-friendly sports outcomes and elevated promos, though MUPs rose 9% to 3.6M vs. 3.2M expected. Full-year EBITDA guidance held.

MNST (Monster) beat Q2 EPS/revenue at 60c/$2.54B vs. 58c/$2.43B, contrasting sharply with the CELH miss earlier that day.

GOOGL (Google) is consolidating AI model work under co-founder Sergey Brin after executives grew frustrated with Demis Hassabis's commercial focus on Gemini. Separately, Google Cloud won an internal resource war over Gemini, likely permanently capping the latter's frontier ambitions while supercharging Cloud growth (FT, SemiAnalysis).

META (Meta) was ordered by a New Mexico judge to pay $942MM and restrict minors' app usage time — the latest in a string of child-safety legal setbacks (WSJ).

BA (Boeing) faces an FAA directive requiring airlines to inspect hundreds of 737 MAX jets for potential fuselage cracks (Bloomberg).

DV (DoubleVerify) agreed to be acquired by Nielsen for $13.60/share in cash, valuing the deal at $2.15B.

SK Hynix announced ~$38B in chipmaking facility expansion in South Korea and signaled a formal shareholder capital return announcement in Q3 (Bloomberg, Reuters).
