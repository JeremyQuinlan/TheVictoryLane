RATES & FED

Treasuries are rallying, with yields down 2–4 basis points across the curve — though notably they barely caught a bid Monday despite the sharp oil selloff, signaling persistent inflation anxiety. Markets are pricing ~35% odds of a rate hike tomorrow and ~43 basis points of total tightening for the year. The WSJ describes this week's outcome as "unusually uncertain" with a "surprise rate increase in play" under Chair Warsh.

MARKET OUTLOOK

The next three sessions are among the busiest of the summer — peak earnings, a Fed decision, macro data, and a potential Iran deal all colliding at once. The best-case Fed scenario is a hike paired with a "probably it" message; the worst case is a hold plus another opaque Warsh presser invoking "task force" at record frequency. Chip stocks are getting crushed globally (Samsung -13.5%, SK Hynix -14.7%, TSMC -3%) while the rest of the market holds in surprisingly well — that bifurcation is the key tell right now. AI credit risk is rising fast (FT), capex is swamping free cash flow, and financing structures are growing increasingly circular — the boom isn't over, but the market's tolerance for AI paper is clearly thinning.
