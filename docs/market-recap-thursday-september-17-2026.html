Market Recap · Thursday September 17, 2026

Sep 17, 2026 · 04:05 PM ET

MARKETS

SPX +1.14%, Nasdaq +1.69%. Dow +318 points, R2K +15.8 points. Brent -1.80% (~$104). Gold +2.00%, Silver +3.70%, BTC +0.60%. DXY mixed — down 19 basis points vs. JPY, up 15 basis points vs. GBP.

RATES & FED

Treasuries rallied hard, with yields dropping 8–10 basis points across the curve, supported by lower oil, the Fed's credibility-reinforcing hike on 9/16, and a dovish BOE QT tweak (the BOE committed to holding £120B of the longest-dated gilts permanently). Markets are pricing roughly 32 basis points of additional hikes across the Fed's final two 2026 meetings and another ~33 basis points into 2027. Trump, notably restrained post-hike, continues to back Warsh — blaming the board, not the chair (WSJ).

MARKET OUTLOOK

Thursday's rally was real but the bear case hasn't gone away — oil remains the load-bearing wall of the entire macro problem, and there's no durable fix in sight. Inflation missed in August and September numbers will be worse given energy, meaning the Fed tightening path stays intact even as growth holds. The AI capex boom is alive and well (CoreWeave's ~$6B raise confirms the market can absorb paper), but the open vs. closed-weight battle is the more important debate investors are sleeping on — China and Nvidia are gaining token share while Anthropic and OpenAI burn cash fighting each other. Any stumble in those two franchises before IPO puts the entire AI trade at risk. A Hormuz resolution — even an ugly one involving tacit tolls — would trigger a cascade of positive outcomes; without it, margin pressure and Fed tightening will increasingly dominate Q3 earnings season.

COMPANY NEWS

CRWV (CoreWeave) launched a ~$6B capital raise — a $3B convertible note plus a 35M share ATM offering worth another ~$3B at Wednesday's close. The sheer size of the deal and its apparent market receptivity is a strong signal that appetite for AI-linked paper remains healthy, which is critical given capex spending far exceeds operating cash flow across the sector.

GNRC (Generac) surged in after-hours Wednesday after announcing a backup power supply agreement with AMZN (Amazon), underscoring that hyperscaler data center buildout remains firmly on track.

LEN (Lennar) missed on FQ3 EPS with weakness across deliveries, margins, and orders; the FQ4 outlook was similarly soft. Homebuilder stocks (DHI, NVR, PHM) still rallied Thursday as the rate drop more than offset the weak print.
