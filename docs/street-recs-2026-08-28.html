Street Recs 2026-08-28

Aug 28, 2026 · 07:45 AM ET

MACRO / FED

No macro or Fed data in this alert. Sector-level read-throughs from retailer earnings (Dollar General, Burlington, Best Buy) suggest a bifurcated consumer: low-income under pressure, but broader discretionary demand holding.

---

SECTOR

Consumer/Retail: Dollar General posted a strong Q2 with +3.5% comps and raised FY26 guidance, but low-income consumer pressure and promotional pricing remain 2H margin risks. Burlington's cautious comp guidance into 2H is overshadowing an otherwise solid print — new store cannibalization and low-income weakness are the culprits.

Cybersecurity: Multiple firms raising targets on S (SentinelOne) and CRWD (CrowdStrike) post-earnings, signaling broad sector demand strength. AI-agent identity demand is also emerging as a meaningful catalyst for OKTA.

BNPL/Fintech: AFRM beat across the board with management framing FY27 GMV guidance as a "floor" — broadly bullish read for the BNPL space.

AI Infrastructure: MRVL guidance raise and growing Google custom silicon opportunity reinforcing the custom ASIC buildout theme.

STOCKS

AFRM — Shares up ~10% after-hours following a fiscal Q4 top-line beat and above-consensus FY27 outlook. Multiple firms raising targets: Piper to $115, BofA to $104, Needham to $100, Barclays to $105. Management called FY27 GMV guide a "floor," not a base case.

WDAY — Initially down 7% on a light Q3 cRPO guide before recovering after management provided an initial FY28 outlook on the call. Price targets raised broadly (Needham $230, Wells $225, Bernstein $238, KeyBanc $215), though Morgan Stanley stays Underweight at $180 and Stifel holds at $160.

OKTA — Goldman raises target to $203 from $126, maintains Buy. Strong Q2 with revenue, cRPO, and margins all above expectations; 14% cRPO growth and accelerating AI-agent identity demand are the key catalysts.

ULTA — Beat Q2 comps, took share, and raised guidance. Goldman raises to $667 (Buy), BofA cuts to $650 (Buy) on peer multiple compression, UBS cuts to $710 (Buy). Mixed price target direction but underlying business momentum is solid.

MRVL — Needham raises to $300 (Buy), Goldman to $220 (Neutral). FY28 revenue growth guided at +50% y/y; custom silicon gross margin mix improving. Goldman flags premium valuation could keep shares range-bound near-term.

S — SentinelOne posted first clean beat-and-raise in ~2 years. Targets raised across the board: Jefferies $26, Stephens $26, Citi $23, Morgan Stanley $23. Piper stays Neutral at $20 citing balanced risk/reward at ~5x 2027 revenues.

ESTC — Elastic reported strong fiscal Q1; Piper raises to $130 (Overweight), BofA to $108 (Neutral), Stifel to $107 (Buy). 20% cRPO growth and AI search/security momentum underpinning confidence in 2H acceleration.

BBY — Fell 4.4% despite Q2 beat and raised FY27 guidance. Goldman notes tariff refunds drove much of the upside while incremental margins disappointed; keeps Sell, raises to $71. UBS (Neutral, $90) and Barclays (EW, $85) more constructive on broadening consumer electronics recovery.

MRNA — Argus upgrades to Buy from Hold with a $180 price target. No specific earnings catalyst cited.

ALL — Argus downgrades to Hold from Buy; premium growth slowing to low-single digits as P&C peers cut prices for share. Stock recently hit prior $238 target, viewed as fully valued.

PANW — Jefferies raises target to $450 from $335, maintains Buy ahead of fiscal Q4 earnings. Expects a sales beat and FY27 revenue growth guide above consensus 21% y/y.

CRWD — Wells Fargo raises to $235 from $230, Overweight. Q2 showed acceleration in NNARR and strong Mythos-related momentum; tone was notably positive.

VCTR — Goldman raises to $117 from $100, Neutral. First Eagle acquisition ($7B AUM) expands pro forma AUM to ~$571B with meaningful EPS accretion potential. (Note: This is our name — flagging for awareness.)

BURL — Multiple firms trimming targets (Morgan Stanley to $432, BofA to $365, Wells to $370, Bernstein to $355) on cautious 2H comp guidance and lower-income consumer weakness, though most maintain Buy/Overweight on margin story and 20%+ earnings growth potential.

DG — Strong Q2 (+3.5% comp, earnings beat, raised FY26 guide). Bernstein raises to $160 (Outperform), Goldman to $141 (Neutral), Piper to $123 (Neutral). Key risk: 2H margin expansion challenged by promotions and shrink comparisons.

WULF — William Blair initiates TeraWulf at Outperform with $31 fair value. Company has repositioned as a power provider to Anthropic, Google, and other hyperscalers; Blair sees multiple expansion as backlog converts to contracted revenue.

NTNX — Barclays raises to $75 from $53, holds Equal Weight. Good demand with traction in external storage and ARR acceleration post fiscal Q4.

ADSK — Guggenheim raises to $283 (Buy); Piper cuts to $338 from $369 but keeps Overweight. Clean quarter with beats across the board; next debate centers on FY28 growth with a large EBA renewal cohort upcoming.

RNG — Needham raises to $85 from $55, Buy, post CFO meetings. Path to accelerating revenue growth from ~4-5
