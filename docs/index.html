VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top-performing stocks in the market today as Meta Muse demand signals are strong and traders are positioning ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing agentic AI consumer product in direct response to Muse, validating the product's significance. The agentic AI theme is radiating bullish sentiment across the entire tech complex.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is outperforming sharply as the market reprices CPU-linked stocks higher on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could bring to the mainstream — is far more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This creates a meaningful incremental demand signal for AMD's processor business. The Meta Muse tailwind is being treated as a sector-level re-rating event for CPU makers.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis, as the market views Meta Muse as a potential mainstream breakthrough for the technology. This is a meaningful positive for Intel given its CPU market presence and ongoing turnaround narrative. The stock is among the outperformers in tech today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the stock in a session where the rest of tech is rallying. The warning signals continued softness in the consumer and commercial PC replacement cycle. This is a company-specific headwind in an otherwise strong tech tape.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, clearing a major regulatory hurdle. Reports indicate Paramount may agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The stock is jumping sharply on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the two companies to merge. The deal resolution removes the most significant remaining legal overhang on the transaction. Both stocks are among the top performers in the media space today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were expecting clearer long-term guidance. The lack of specificity at a high-profile capital markets event is being read as a negative signal about management's visibility. This is a company-specific setback in an otherwise constructive risk environment.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday flagging poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans having hit a wall. This raises questions about the financial structuring of Oracle's aggressive infrastructure expansion. The story adds to concerns about AI off-balance sheet liability growth across Big Tech.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, driven by price promotions and package deals according to a Wall Street Journal report. While peers are struggling with demand softness, Disney's strategic pricing flexibility is helping sustain visitation. This is a modest positive data point for the parks segment.

▼ CF CF Industries Sector Reprice

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below current Canadian potash rates. The announcement signals a potential significant disruption to North American potash pricing dynamics. The news is being treated as a sector-wide bearish repricing event for potash producers.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday midday with notable Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The dominant macro themes are AI enthusiasm around Meta Muse and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism heading into the UN General Assembly is lifting risk sentiment, with hopes for potential US-Iran dialogue and broader Middle East de-escalation.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on but not euphoric tone. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the 20% target. No major Fed commentary is driving the session.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse agentic AI narrative is a genuine demand-side catalyst pulling forward expectations for CPU-intensive compute, lifting AMD, ARM, and Intel alongside the broader AI stack. Oil falling sharply on Houthi de-escalation hopes reduces inflation risk and lifts consumer and travel stocks simultaneously. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing the state AG hurdle removes a major regulatory overhang, adding a deal-flow tailwind. Treasuries are catching a mild bid, suggesting the rate environment is not fighting the equity rally.

BEAR CASE

The US-China trade truce extension was not reached Sunday despite optimistic rhetoric, leaving a major uncertainty hanging with the November 10 expiration date approaching. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech are up roughly $300 billion in just the past 12 months, a rising risk that markets are ignoring. Oil tanker costs are surging and Middle East tensions remain complex — Trump's last-minute pullback on Houthi strikes does not resolve the underlying conflict. The equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points versus Nasdaq's 153 basis points, signaling narrow, concentrated leadership rather than a broad healthy rally.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean intraday, driven by two powerful catalysts: the Meta Muse/agentic AI wave lifting the entire tech complex, and falling oil prices easing geopolitical risk premiums. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could either sustain or deflate the AI momentum trade.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear leader with AI and CPU-linked names surging on the Meta Muse agentic AI catalyst. Energy is underperforming broadly as oil falls roughly 400 basis points below $100 per barrel on Houthi de-escalation hopes. Potash names are being hit hard on the Belarus supply deal news. REITs and data center industrials are outperforming. Transports are lagging. Crypto-linked stocks are rallying with bitcoin up 585 basis points. Insurance and payments names are also underperforming.
