◆ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO has been pushed to November from October, and year-end ARR projections of greater than 100 billion dollars per the NYT and greater than 110 billion dollars per the WSJ disappointed bulls hoping for 120 billion dollars or higher. The company is considering launching a powerful new model ahead of the IPO to recapture momentum lost to OpenAI's Astra, and plans to have 5 gigawatts of compute by year-end doubling to 10 gigawatts by end of 2027.

▼ OPENAI OpenAI Other

The FT reported OpenAI expects negative free cash flow of approximately 280 billion dollars during the 2026 through 2030 period as spending dramatically outpaces revenue. OpenAI's Astra launch has driven a sharp inflection higher in growth versus Anthropic over recent weeks, giving it renewed competitive momentum despite the alarming cash burn figures.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article focused on poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center facility in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have hit a wall, raising concerns about Oracle's off-balance sheet data center financing exposure.

◆ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. A settlement could require Paramount to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central, potentially clearing a key regulatory hurdle for the deal to close.

Wendy's saw pressure on Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The franchisee bankruptcy raises concerns about the financial health of Wendy's franchise network and potential store closures or disruptions.

▼ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores as the grocery giant pushes back against supplier price hikes. The move signals increasing retailer pricing power and willingness to delist premium-priced brands, a potential negative for consumer staples suppliers relying on major grocery chains.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk M&A

Novo Nordisk is being pressured by shareholders to diversify away from its GLP-1 concentration through mergers and acquisitions. The pressure reflects investor concern about single-asset dependency risk even as GLP-1 demand remains strong.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner capsule to be used on 10 or more new flights into low-Earth orbit. This represents a meaningful potential contract win for Boeing's beleaguered Starliner program following its earlier high-profile difficulties.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture will embed a team of evaluators to monitor safety and alignment at Anthropic. The arrangement positions Accenture as a key partner in AI governance and oversight at one of the most prominent frontier AI labs ahead of its IPO.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by escalating Middle East tensions and a wave of negative AI industry headlines. The Houthis dramatically ratcheted up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities, raising fears of further energy supply disruption. Ukraine also launched a massive drone attack on Russia including Moscow, adding to global geopolitical risk heading into the week.

Markets are pricing greater than 50% odds of additional hikes from the RBA, RBNZ, Bank of Canada, FOMC, ECB, and BOE over coming weeks, and Kansas City Fed President Schmid warned on Friday that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil.

The economy and corporate earnings have held up through the summer, but the latest spike in energy prices will begin working through the system rapidly and could trigger meaningful demand destruction. AI industry sentiment took multiple hits over the weekend including a huge OpenAI cash burn projection of negative 280 billion dollars in free cash flow through 2030, a delay in the Anthropic IPO, and rising off-balance sheet AI liability exposure for Big Tech of roughly 300 billion dollars in just the last 12 months. Monetary policy globally remains hawkish with multiple central banks expected to hike in coming weeks.
