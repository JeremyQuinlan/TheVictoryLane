MARKET CLOSE Sep 22, 2026 4:04:57 PM EST Vital Market Recap for Tuesday September 22, 2026 Market update

* Market levels – stocks saw mixed price action on Tuesday: the SPX and equal-weight S&P were both flat while the Dow fell 185

points/36bp, the Nasdaq rose 122 points/45bp, and the R2K advanced 14.5 points/51bp.Treasuries didn’t move much, with yields

flat across the curve.Fed expectations held steady (the market is still pricing in ~30bp worth of hikes this year and ~40bp in

’27).Brent sank 250bp to <$98 amid hope for diplomatic progress in the Iran war at this week’s UN GA.Gold rose 50bp while

silver climbed 190bp and bitcoin dropped 85bp.

* What happened Tuesday – from a macro perspective, it was a fairly quiet day, with investors focused on the UN GA and whether

the event will yield any diplomatic progress with regards to the Iran war.Media reports have been hopeful over the last several

sessions, with a handful of articles Tues morning indicating that Iran had a floated a compromise whereby it would have Hormuz

reopened within 7 days in exchange for some American concessions while Gulf countries lobby the White House to avoid military

escalation (Trump at 1:50pmET Tues afternoon revealed that the US team met with Iran’s delegation for three hours earlier in

the day and that the meeting “went very well” and was “very productive”, with further gatherings possible). Whether Middle East

temperatures can actually cool remains to be seen, but it still seems like Trump, despite all the talk of “annihilation”,

remains very unwilling (and incapable) of ratcheting kinetic activity to the level required to alter conditions on the ground,

especially since this is no longer simply a war between the US and Iran (Tehran’s proxies are activated in Yemen, Iraq, and

elsewhere, making it a multi-front conflagration). On the AI front, chip stocks traded well thanks to ongoing Muse optimism,

although software/services were hit hard as all the personal AI agent hype rekindles concerns about the technology disrupting

legacy SaaS and online services (which is why stocks like ADBE, BKNG, INTU, etc., were all hit hard).

be that important for the overall macro narrative.
