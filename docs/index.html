Macro · Rates

Fed hawks are vocal — Kansas City Fed President Schmid and Minneapolis Fed President Kashkari both warned over the weekend that inflation is running hot well beyond just energy, and the Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt financing.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The single most powerful bullish catalyst available right now is a reduction in Middle East tensions — even a modest diplomatic signal from the UN General Assembly could send oil prices sharply lower, which would ease inflation, reduce yields, improve margins, and boost consumer confidence simultaneously. Trump backed off planned Houthi air strikes at the last minute Sunday night and signaled openness to meeting Iran's president, suggesting some diplomatic flexibility. China and Pakistan are reportedly urging Iran to rein in the Houthis, adding international pressure for de-escalation. The upcoming Trump-Xi summit on Thursday could also deliver modest tariff relief and extend the trade truce, providing an additional tailwind.

BEAR CASE

The weekend news flow was almost entirely negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest-ever drone strike on Russia, Iran warned of fierce retaliation if the US resumes military action, and the State Department issued warnings about unforeseen escalation risk across the region. AI sector headlines were deeply troubling, including a delayed Anthropic IPO, underwhelming Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI projecting roughly $280 billion in negative free cash flow through 2030, and Oracle facing serious problems offloading debt tied to its New Mexico data center. Global monetary policy remains firmly hawkish with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month, and inflation is broadening beyond energy according to multiple Fed officials.

Market Outlook

Adam leans cautiously mixed with a short-term bullish tilt driven entirely by geopolitical optionality at the UN General Assembly — if any diplomatic progress on Iran materializes, it could trigger an outsized drop in oil with a cascade of bullish knock-on effects. The key catalyst to watch today is any headline out of the UN General Assembly around US-Iran diplomacy, including whether Trump meets with Iran's president, as well as any further Middle East escalation that could send oil spiking back higher.

Sector Watch

AI and data center infrastructure is under significant pressure from multiple angles — delayed Anthropic IPO, poor OpenAI cash flow projections ($280 billion negative free cash flow through 2030), and Oracle data center debt problems all point to growing risk in the hyperscaler pick-and-shovel trade, even as European data center stocks outperform this morning. Energy sector faces conflicting forces: Brent crude sliding 170 basis points to around $102.10 on diplomatic optimism, but underlying supply conditions remain extremely tight with surging tanker costs, motor oil shortages in the US, and the Iran war driving diesel price spikes. Financials in Europe got a boost from SocGen's bullish analyst day targets including ROTE of 13-14% in 2029 and above 15% beyond 2030.
