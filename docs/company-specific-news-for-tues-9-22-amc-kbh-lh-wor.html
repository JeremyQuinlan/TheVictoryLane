EARNINGS Sep 22, 2026 5:25:53 PM EST Company-specific news for Tues 9/22 (AMC) - KBH, LH, WOR * KBH (KB Home) reported decent EPS upside in FQ3 ($1.05 vs. the Street $0.89), w/the beat driven in large part by cost controls

(the SG&A ratio outperformed expectations by ~30bp) and to a lesser extent slightly higher GMs and modestly better deliveries,

although the FQ4 guide is mixed-to-underwhelming (the deliveries range is about inline, but the gross margin and SG&A ratio are

both a bit worse). The macro commentary is cautious (as one would expect it to be given the environment): “We are operating in

a housing market that continues to be challenging, with conditions weakening since our June earnings report. Higher mortgage

interest rates have further pressured affordability and, together with geopolitical uncertainty and broader economic headwinds,

have caused many prospective buyers to be more cautious on purchasing a home”

* LH (Labcorp) shares were hit during the Tues session (-3%) amid concerns about reduced CMS Medicare payment rates and while

mgmt. thinks the new rates “would impose steep annual payment cuts that threaten patient access to laboratory services”, the

company is reaffirming the 2026-2029 long-term guidance provided back at the Sept 10 analyst meeting (although the impact on

2027 guidance specifically will be provided to investors in Feb 2027 alongside Q4 earnings).

* WOR (Worthington Enterprises) reported modest upside on FQ1 EPS/EBITDA (82c/$74MM vs. the Street 75c/$69.5MM) and sales (+13%

to $344MM vs. the Street $332MM). “We're very encouraged by our start to fiscal 2027 and the opportunities we see across our

businesses, including rapidly growing demand for our engineered ASME tanks used in liquid cooling systems for data centers”.
