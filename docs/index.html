VK · Key Names
▲ KMX CarMax Earnings Beat

CarMax reported a massive FQ2 beat with EPS of $1.16 versus the Street at $0.73, an 81.3% upside surprise, on revenue of $7.9 billion versus estimates of $7.05 billion, up 19.5% year-over-year. The beat was broad-based: used vehicle units rose 13.8%, ASPs up 6.3%, extended protection plan revenue up 23%, and CarMax Auto Finance income rose 32.1% to $135.6 million on lower provisions. Management is resuming share repurchases this quarter and has scheduled an analyst day for November 3rd.

▼ OURA Oura Other

Oura announced this morning it is postponing its IPO citing uncertainty in the IPO market, becoming the latest in a string of delayed offerings alongside SB Energy and Holtec. This follows the implosion in Shein shares after its Hong Kong debut and adds to growing concern that Anthropic and OpenAI may struggle to go public on their anticipated timelines and at expected valuations. The potential delay of those two marquee AI IPOs is significant because they are viewed as critical catalysts for continued data center capital expenditure.
