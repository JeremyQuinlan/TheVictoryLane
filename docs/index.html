MORNING
Sep 30, 2026 · 05:20 AM ET
Morning Intelligentsia · Wednesday September 30, 2026
Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Wednesday morning brought a batch of key US economic data that is broadly dovish in tone. The August PCE came in cooler than expected on core, Q2 GDP was revised sharply higher to +2.2% annualized, and the ADP jobs report for September beat modestly. Markets are digesting a mixed but net-positive data set ahead of Friday's BLS jobs report.

The cooler core PCE print at +0.2% month-over-month versus the Street's +0.3% estimate is dovish and supportive of rate relief, especially following hawkish-leaning Williams remarks Tuesday. However, methodological changes in the PCE calculation starting with the August reading may have artificially dampened price momentum, which could limit the market's enthusiasm. The next major inflation catalyst is the September CPI due October 14th.

Market Outlook

Slight bull lean on the session given the softer PCE data and resilient ADP print, but conviction is tempered by the PCE methodology caveat and the market's forward focus on September inflation data. The key catalyst to watch is Friday's BLS jobs report, where a print in the 50-75K range would be considered ideal for yield relief.

Sector Watch

Construction, manufacturing, education/health services, and leisure/hospitality showed employment strength in September per ADP. Financial activities and professional/business services saw job losses.
