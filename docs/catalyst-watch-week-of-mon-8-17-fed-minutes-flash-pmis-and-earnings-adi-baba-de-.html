RATES & FED

The FOMC minutes (Wed 8/19) are expected to read hawkish, reflecting the tone of the July 29th meeting — but a lot has shifted since then, with a soft jobs report and cooler CPI/PPI prints giving the Fed cover to pivot. The market will be parsing every line for signals ahead of Warsh's Jackson Hole speech on Friday 8/28, which is the real event traders are positioning for.

MARKET OUTLOOK

The week of 8/17 is a warm-up act — the trio of catalysts starting Wed 8/26 (July PCE, Nvidia earnings, Jackson Hole) is what actually moves the needle. Housing-exposed names like HD and LOW face a stubbornly difficult macro backdrop with rates showing little sign of easing, and that's not a short-term problem. Walmart's FQ2 will be noisy thanks to IEEPA tariff refund distortions, making the underlying consumer read harder to parse. On the bullish side, AI-linked demand remains genuinely robust across semis and instrumentation — ADI and KEYS both carry strong tailwinds into their prints. Overall, the tape feels like it's in a holding pattern until Jackson Hole gives direction.

COMPANY NEWS

FN (Fabrinet) reports FQ4 Monday after the close. The Street models revenue +40% to $1.274B, gross margins of 12.2% (down 30 basis points), and EPS of $3.82. Sentiment is bullish on AI-driven demand, though margin pressure and a rich valuation are real risks.

HD (Home Depot) reports FQ2 Tuesday pre-market. Consensus looks for EPS of $4.73, total revenue +4.5% to $47.32B, comps of +1%, gross margins of 33.05%, and operating margins of 13.33%. The Pro/contractor segment M&A adds structural support, but the housing macro is a genuine drag.

KEYS (Keysight) reports FQ3 Tuesday after the close. Street models revenue +29.1% to $1.745B — Communication Solutions +33.5% to $1.254B, Electronic Industrial Solutions +19.5% to $492.5MM — with EPS of $2.48. AI tailwinds are real, but valuation leaves limited margin for error.

TOL (Toll Brothers) reports FQ3 Tuesday after the close. Consensus: revenue -10.8% to $2.627B, deliveries -9.75% to 2,670 units, gross margins ex-items down 220 basis points to 25.3%, EPS of $2.92. TOL's affluent buyer base — nearly 25% all-cash in FQ2, LTV ~70% — is a relative shelter from rate sensitivity.

ADI (Analog Devices) reports FQ3 Wednesday pre-market. Street models total sales +36% to $3.91B (Industrial +52% to $1.95B, Communication +71% to $636MM, Auto +10.5% to $940MM), gross margins of 72.5%, operating margins of 49.2%, and EPS of $3.34. The Empower acquisition deepens AI data center exposure.

EL (Estee Lauder) reports FQ4 Wednesday pre-market. Consensus: organic revenue growth of 3.1%, gross margins of 73.1%, operating margins of 6.5%, EPS of $0.32. Watch FY27 guidance closely — Street models EPS ~$3.18 with ~4% organic growth.

LOW (Lowe's) reports FQ2 Wednesday pre-market. Street expects comps +0.9%, gross margins of 32.6%, operating margins of 13.6%, and EPS of $4.25. Same housing headwind story as HD — management execution helps, but macro is the ceiling.

TJX (TJX Companies) reports FQ2 Wednesday pre-market. Consensus: overall comps +3.4% (Marmaxx +3.3%, HomeGoods +4.4%), gross margins of 31%, EPS of $1.18. Off-price secular tailwinds are intact; the premium valuation is the only real risk.

DE (Deere) reports FQ3 Thursday pre-market. Street models total sales +3.84% to $10.75B — Production & Precision Ag -7.8% to $3.94B, Small Ag & Turf +11% to $3.356B, Construction & Forestry +15.2% to $3.523B — with EPS of $4.70. Ag peers have been mixed: AGCO disappointed, CNH delivered solid results.

WMT (Walmart) reports FQ2 Thursday pre-market. Street models revenue +5.25% to $186.7B, US comps +3.7%, gross margins of 24.67%, EPS of $0.74. IEEPA tariff refunds will distort the headline read. The Albertsons blow-up is a watch item for grocery health broadly.
