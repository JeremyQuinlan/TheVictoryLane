VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point gross margin tailwind from tariff refunds and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100 basis points year-over-year. Management noted positive comp linearity during the quarter, which is providing some reassurance to the market.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported a solid fiscal Q1 EPS beat at 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents. Critically, even stripping out an 11-cent tailwind from tariff refunds, adjusted EPS of 42 cents still beat consensus — making this a higher-quality beat than AutoZone's. Guidance is mixed with the fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% against the Street's 13.85% estimate. Revenue came in at $2.311 billion, down 8.4% year-over-year, though slightly above the Street's $2.17 billion estimate. Management cited a difficult macro landscape as the primary headwind.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect Wednesday night, with investor expectations growing increasingly bullish. Shopify announced it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a significant commerce integration win — though Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform. OpenAI is reportedly now considering developing a competing product, validating the strategic importance of the agentic AI consumer push.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, positioning Shopify as a key partner in the agentic commerce ecosystem. This is a meaningful differentiation versus Amazon, which has blocked Muse. The partnership enhances Shopify's relevance as agentic AI shopping becomes more mainstream.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday following new Medicare payment rates from CMS for lab services, with the rate cuts representing a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The negative read-through also weighed on LabCorp. This is a regulatory pricing headwind with direct earnings implications for the lab testing sector.

▼ LH Laboratory Corporation Other

LabCorp shares declined alongside Quest Diagnostics after CMS announced new Medicare payment rates for lab services that were worse than expected. The rate cuts are a sector-wide headwind for clinical lab testing firms. Both names are underperforming meaningfully on the news.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, with patients achieving 22% weight loss. This is a strong efficacy readout that reinforces Viking's position as a credible competitor in the GLP-1 obesity drug market. The data keeps Viking relevant in a high-stakes race against Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's capabilities in robotics and Physical AI at a time when industrial automation demand is accelerating. Strategic fit is strong but the acquisition adds integration risk.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On held its analyst day and outlined a target of at least CHF 5.6 billion in 2029 sales versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or above versus the Street's 21% estimate. The company also authorized a $1 billion share buyback program. Both the above-consensus long-term targets and the buyback are incremental positives.
