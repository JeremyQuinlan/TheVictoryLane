VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AZO beat FQ4 EPS at $56.05 vs. the Street's $54.03, but the quality was low — the upside was driven by tariff refunds (145bp tailwind to gross margins) and a non-cash LIFO benefit (105bp tailwind) rather than operational strength. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% vs. the Street's +3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100bp year-over-year to 33.4% of sales. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final 8 weeks of the quarter and expressed confidence heading into fiscal 2027.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500MM upfront plus an additional ~$106.5MM. RealSense is expected to generate $80-90MM in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth. The deal positions Cognex deeper into the Physical AI and robotics perception space.

◆ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid FQ1 EPS of 53 cents vs. the Street's 36 cents, and notably would have beaten even without the tariff refund tailwind of 11 cents, implying 42 cents ex-refunds. Sales missed, declining 3.3% organically to $923.4MM vs. the Street's $943.2MM, though orders rose 3.5% year-over-year. Fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed to $3.88-4.03B from $3.93-4.13B, while the EPS range was held unchanged at $1.85-2.15.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Pentair named Bob Hau as its new CFO, effective November 1st, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman staying through that date to ensure a smooth transition. Hau brings prior CFO experience at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International. This is a leadership change with no financial results attached.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR missed FQ4 EPS at 78 cents vs. the Street's 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% vs. the Street's 13.85%, despite revenue coming in slightly above at $2.311B vs. the Street's $2.17B. Management cited stubborn interest rates, elevated fuel costs, inflationary pressures, and rising material costs as headwinds, and expects a relatively flat retail sales environment in fiscal 2027 without providing formal guidance. Bright spots included healthy dealer inventory levels, structural cost reduction initiatives, and a roughly 75% year-over-year surge in the North American towable RV order backlog.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking shares spiked in pre-market trading after reporting positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the trial. The result is a meaningful clinical milestone in the competitive GLP-1 obesity drug space. This positions Viking as a credible competitor in one of the most closely watched therapeutic categories in biopharma.
