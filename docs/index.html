VK · Key Names
▲ FICO Fair Isaac Corporation Partnership

United Wholesale Mortgage, the country's number-one mortgage lender, reaffirmed its commitment to using FICO Scores on all credit pulls across its broker lending operations. This is a notable vote of confidence in FICO's product amid ongoing debate about alternative credit scoring models in the mortgage industry. The news reinforces FICO's entrenched position in the mortgage origination ecosystem.

▲ PENG Penguin Solutions Earnings Beat

Penguin Solutions reported a strong fiscal Q4 beat with EPS of $1.00 versus the Street's $0.77 and revenue of $567 million versus $520 million expected. The company raised its fiscal 2027 guidance, now seeing sales up approximately 40% versus the prior outlook of 30%, and EPS up roughly 55% versus the prior 30% guide. Management cited accelerating momentum in AI Infrastructure as the key driver of the raised outlook.

◆ STZ Constellation Brands Earnings Beat

Constellation Brands beat fiscal Q2 EPS estimates at $3.74 versus $3.53 expected, with beer sales up 5% and volumes up 5.5% — approximately 340 basis points above consensus — and beer margins declining 160 basis points year over year, which was better than feared. However, beer depletions came in only inline at down 0.6% year over year, and the company left its full-year EPS, sales, margin, and cash flow guidance unchanged despite the outperformance. Additionally, the company announced the acquisition of SpikedAde for $75 million at close plus up to $278 million in contingent payments over five years.

▲ ZIM ZIM Integrated Shipping Guidance Raise

ZIM raised its full-year EBITDA guidance to $2.7 to $3 billion, up sharply from the prior range of $2 to $2.4 billion. This marks the second major upside preannouncement from a large shipping firm following Maersk, reflecting a powerful combination of resilient demand and geopolitical disruption driving freight rates and volumes higher. The trend signals a broad and durable tailwind for container shipping operators.
