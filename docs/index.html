Macro · Rates

The newsletter focuses on the US midterm elections and their market implications. Democrats are seen as highly likely to take the House, already priced in by markets, while the Senate flip is closer to 50/50 despite prediction markets showing ~65% odds. The broader risk tone is shaped by political uncertainty around the debt ceiling and potential government shutdown brinksmanship over the next two years.

Market Outlook

No strong directional bull or bear call is made for the session. The key catalyst to watch is the midterm election outcome, particularly the Senate race, though Adam argues the real longer-term market risk is the debt ceiling, which won't become binding until H2 2027 but could surface during the lame duck session.

Sector Watch

AI sector faces regulatory risk at the state level rather than federal — Texas is a key focus area. The midterms are not seen as a primary driver of AI's recent poor trading performance.

Adam argues the midterms are not a meaningful driver for AI stocks in either direction. State-level regulation, particularly in Texas under Governor Abbott, is the more immediate legislative risk for the sector.
