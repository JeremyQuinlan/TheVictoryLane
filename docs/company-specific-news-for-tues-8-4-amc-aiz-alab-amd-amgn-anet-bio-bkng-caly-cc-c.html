MARKET OUTLOOK

The earnings tape for Tuesday AMC is broadly constructive — beats are outnumbering misses by a wide margin, and guidance is moving higher across consumer, healthcare, and tech. AI infrastructure spending remains the dominant theme, with ALAB, ANET, and AMD all printing strong numbers and issuing bullish forward commentary. The one wrinkle worth watching is free cash flow — SPCX's deeply negative FCF is a reminder that AI capex enthusiasm has real costs that the income statement doesn't fully reveal.

COMPANY NEWS

BKNG (Booking Holdings) posted Q2 bookings of $51B vs. the Street's $49.7B and EPS of $2.54 vs. $2.43 expected, with management citing resilient travel demand into Q3 despite macro and geopolitical uncertainty. Full-year guidance is largely reiterated with cost-cut savings estimates raised.

CALY (Callaway) beat Q2 EPS by a nickel (40c vs. 35c expected), driven by higher sales and gross margin expansion of 420 basis points year-over-year. Full-year EBITDA guidance was raised meaningfully to $246–260MM from $211–233MM.

MAT (Mattel) missed slightly on EPS (1c vs. 4c expected) but beat on sales (+10% to $1.125B vs. $1.1B est.), operating income, and gross margins (48.6% vs. 47.8% est.). Full-year guidance is reaffirmed, with ~$100MM in remaining buybacks planned for H2.

WYNN beat on revenue ($1.86B vs. $1.83B) and property EBITDAR ($568.3MM vs. $556MM), with Macau driving the upside. Wynn Al Marjan Island is set to open September 2027.

AIZ (Assurant) delivered a strong Q2 EPS beat ($6.41 vs. $5.18 est.) across both Lifestyle and Housing segments, lifting full-year EPS growth guidance to ~5% from 1–2%.

AMGN (Amgen) crushed Q2 estimates with EPS of $6.29 vs. $5.62 and sales of $10.05B vs. $9.43B, raising full-year EPS guidance to $22.30–$23.50 from $21.70–$23.10.

BIO (Bio-Rad) beat Q2 EPS handily ($2.62 vs. $1.75 est.) on better sales and margins, though full-year guidance was only reiterated. Revenue was flat year-over-year with Life Science softness offset by Clinical Diagnostics and 20%+ growth in digital PCR instruments.

DVA (DaVita) posted modest Q2 upside with treatments per day up 1.09% and revenue per treatment down 1.7%, reiterating full-year guidance.

GILD (Gilead) reported sales of $7.8B vs. $7.4B expected, beating on the top line despite EPS being depressed by M&A and IPR&D charges. Full-year guidance was raised.

CC (Chemours) disappointed, guiding Q3 EBITDA to $175–205MM vs. the Street's $250MM and full-year EBITDA to ~$800MM vs. ~$848MM expected.

CE (Celanese) beat Q2 EPS ($2.45 vs. $2.23 est.) on resilient volumes (+4%) and strong pricing (+14%), but Q3 EPS guidance of $1.55 fell short of the $1.77 consensus.

ECG (Everus) was the standout in industrials, crushing EPS ($1.64 vs. $1.15 est.) and EBITDA ($128.6MM vs. $88MM est.) while raising full-year EBITDA guidance to $410–425MM from $345–360MM, citing strength in data centers, hospitality, and high tech.

EMR (Emerson) beat FQ3 EPS ($1.71 vs. $1.68 est.) with organic revenue growth of +6% vs. +4.75% expected, and Q4 guidance is essentially inline. Underlying orders were up 7%.

FBIN (Fortune Brands) posted a large headline EPS beat, but tariff refunds were the driver — on an apples-to-apples basis, the full-year EPS guide is being trimmed as management steps up investment spending.

J (Jacobs) came in essentially inline with adjusted EPS of $1.84 vs. $1.82 est. and reiterated full-year EPS guidance at $7.20–$7.30. Infrastructure & Advanced Facilities segment gross revenue grew 39% year-over-year.

MOS (Mosaic) posted modest EPS/EBITDA upside but missed on sales, with management citing sulfur availability and affordability headwinds.

ALAB (Astera Labs) was the TMT highlight beyond the megacaps, beating Q2 and guiding Q3 sales to $550MM vs. the Street's $416MM — an enormous beat — as optical interconnects and new design wins broaden the growth story into 2027.

AMD posted a largely inline Q2 but guided Q3 sales to $13B vs. $12.5B expected. Gross margin guidance of 56% was about 25 basis points light. Management's tone was bullish, calling out accelerating EPYC demand and Instinct deployments scaling.

ANET (Arista) delivered a strong Q2 with EPS of $1.02 vs. 89c expected and sales up 37.7% year-over-year to $3.03B vs. $2.82B est. Q3 guidance of $1.07 EPS and $3.3B in revenue also came in ahead of the $0.92/$2.95B consensus.

LSCC (Lattice Semiconductor) beat Q2 EPS (53c vs. 45c est.) and guided Q3 sales — including a recent acquisition — to $255MM vs. the Street's $204MM.

MTCH (Match Group) beat on EBITDA and guided Q3 EBITDA to $330–335MM vs. $315MM expected, but paying users missed at 13.25MM vs. 13.38MM est., down 6%.

PINS (Pinterest) beat across the board on EBITDA, sales, MAUs, and ARPU in Q2, with MAUs particularly impressive. Q3 EBITDA guidance of $345MM edges out the $335MM consensus.
