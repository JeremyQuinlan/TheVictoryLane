VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ ANTHROPIC Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO has been pushed to November from October, with year-end ARR projections coming in at greater than $100-110 billion versus some bull expectations of $120 billion or higher. The company is targeting a raise of over $100 billion at a greater than $2 trillion valuation, and is considering launching a powerful new model before the IPO to recapture momentum lost to OpenAI's Astra. Key overhangs include fierce competition with OpenAI, growing open-weight model token share, and questions about whether ARR is temporarily inflated by Meta usage that could shift to in-house tools.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter, with efforts to offload the loans reportedly having hit a wall. This raises concerns about the financial structure underlying Oracle's aggressive AI infrastructure buildout and its exposure to the broader theme of Big Tech off-balance sheet AI liabilities rising approximately $300 billion in just the last 12 months.

▲ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. Under the potential settlement, Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central, clearing a key regulatory hurdle for the deal to close.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery stands to benefit from Paramount's advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general, which could resolve a key regulatory obstacle to the Paramount-Warner merger. A settlement requiring CNN editorial independence preservation and select cable channel divestitures would likely clear the path for deal completion.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. This is a meaningful negative signal for Wendy's franchisee health and system-wide unit economics at a time when the consumer environment is already under pressure from elevated energy prices.

▼ VW Volkswagen Guidance Cut

Volkswagen issued a large downside preannouncement Friday, cutting its 2026 operating margin guidance to up to 1% from prior guidance of 4 to 5.5%, versus the Street's estimate of approximately 4.1%. The miss was driven by a goodwill impairment charge at Porsche, a challenging global auto market especially in China, and additional restructuring expenses, though adjusted margins of roughly 4% are essentially inline with consensus.

◆ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores nationwide as the grocery giant pushes back against supplier price hikes. This reflects broader retailer leverage dynamics and puts pressure on branded consumer staples companies attempting to maintain elevated pricing.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk Activist Entry

Novo Nordisk is facing shareholder pressure to diversify away from its heavy GLP-1 concentration by pursuing M&A. The pressure highlights growing investor concern about single-product dependency even as GLP-1 demand remains robust.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on 10 or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, a potential significant contract win that would help rehabilitate Starliner's reputation following its troubled crewed mission earlier in the program. This represents a meaningful competitive development against SpaceX in the commercial crew market.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture announced it will embed a team of evaluators at Anthropic to monitor AI safety and alignment, positioning the firm as a key enterprise partner in the growing AI governance and oversight space. This reinforces Accenture's strategy of monetizing the AI infrastructure buildout beyond pure technology implementation.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by escalating Middle East conflict and a cluster of negative AI headlines. The Houthis dramatically ramped up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities over the weekend, raising fears of further energy supply disruption. Ukraine also launched a massive drone attack on Russia including Moscow, while the US/China Trump-Xi summit set for Thursday offers a potential modest positive offset via select tariff reductions.

Global monetary policy remains pointed in a hawkish direction, with the world's three biggest central banks — the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC — all hiking this month. The BOJ's latest hike was slightly more dovish than expected but paradoxically sparked macro concern by pressuring the yen lower and rekindling fears of Japan selling Treasuries to defend its currency. Markets are pricing greater than 50% odds of additional hikes from multiple central banks over the coming weeks, and Kansas City Fed President Schmid warned Friday that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil prices.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Even a small improvement in Middle East geopolitics could trigger an outsized drop in energy prices with favorable knock-on effects including lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The Trump-Xi summit on Thursday could produce select tariff cuts on roughly $30 billion worth of goods and movement on Chinese LNG tariffs, providing a tangible trade positive. China and Pakistan are reportedly urging Iran to rein in the Houthis, suggesting growing global pressure for de-escalation. Saudi Arabia is also increasing ship-to-ship oil transfers to help compensate for supply shortages, providing some near-term energy market relief.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical landscape is deteriorating rapidly, with Iran tightening its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and the Houthis now escalating attacks directly on Saudi Arabian cities. Corporate earnings and the economy have held up over the summer but an energy price spike of this magnitude will inevitably trigger demand destruction and margin compression as it works through the system. The weekend AI news dump — including OpenAI's projected negative $280 billion in free cash flow through 2030, rising Big Tech off-balance sheet AI liabilities, and a modest Anthropic IPO delay — adds a sector-specific headwind to the broader risk-off tone. Global monetary policy remains hawkish with multiple central bank hikes likely in coming weeks, leaving little rate cushion to absorb further shocks.

Market Outlook

Adam leans bearish into the week open given net negative weekend newsflow — escalating Middle East conflict, a cluster of AI disappointments, and persistent hawkish central bank signals. The key catalyst to watch is the Trump-Xi summit on Thursday September 24th, which could deliver modest tariff relief and a brief risk-on lift, while the UN General Assembly running all week offers a low-probability but high-impact diplomatic wildcard on Iran and energy prices.
