Mid-Day Update · Wednesday September 23, 2026

Sep 23, 2026 · 11:04 AM ET
Macro

Major indices are in the red as of mid-morning: SPX is down 33 points or 0.43%, Nasdaq off 205 points or 0.75%, and the Russell 2000 is the biggest laggard down 1.25%. Treasuries are selling off hard with yields up 0.07% to 0.10% across the curve, driven by very hot flash PMIs that are stoking inflation fears and hawkish Fed repricing — the market is now pricing roughly 0.38% in hikes this year and ~0.50% in 2028. Brent crude is surging 2.60% back above $101.90 as US-Iran diplomatic hopes fade, while gold falls 1.55%, silver drops 3.30%, and bitcoin slides 2.05%.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautious and bearish-leaning on the session. The twin headwinds of hot PMI-driven yield surges and stalling Iran diplomacy are pressuring risk assets broadly, with tech and consumer discretionary leading losses. The key catalyst to watch today is the Zuckerberg keynote at Meta Connect at 7pm ET, plus any further developments on US-Iran diplomacy that could move oil.

Sector Watch

Healthcare, energy, software, select hardware, exchanges, insurance, aerospace/defense, chemicals, consumer staples, off-price retail, and hotels are all outperforming on the session. Underperforming sectors include travel/leisure, most of tech (semis and large-cap growth in particular), select consumer discretionary, housing, payments, asset managers, and materials. The energy outperformance is tied to Brent crude climbing back above $100 on fading Iran deal hopes. Semiconductor and broad tech weakness is being driven by rising yields and China hardware-ban headlines.

Company Coverage
▲ CBRL Cracker Barrel Earnings beat

Cracker Barrel delivered strong upside in FQ4 across the board: sales came in at $849.3MM vs. the Street's $834MM, adjusted EPS of 99 cents crushed the 16-cent consensus, and EBITDA of $62.1MM beat the $41.5MM estimate. The F27 EBITDA guidance mid-point is also ahead of expectations. Shares are among today's earnings-linked outperformers.

◆ GIS General Mills Earnings — mixed results

General Mills posted FQ1 results that were not as bad as feared: EPS of 75 cents on an FX-neutral basis beat the 72-cent estimate, organic revenue was roughly flat vs. the Street's -1.8% forecast, and all four business segments outperformed on revenue. However, gross margins fell 0.90% to 33.3% and op. margins dropped 1.30% to 14.4%, and shares are among today's earnings-linked underperformers despite reaffirming prior FY guidance.

◆ MCD McDonald's Analyst meeting / financial targets

McDonald's held an analyst meeting today and provided 2030 financial targets, headlined by an operating margin goal in the low-to-mid 50% range — solidly above the consensus 2030 forecast of 49.5%. However, free cash flow conversion guidance was trimmed due to higher capex, and shares are sliding on the day. The headline margin target is positive but the capex disappointment is tempering enthusiasm.

▼ MANU Manchester United Earnings — guidance miss

Manchester United reported modest FQ4 upside on sales (GBP 157.5MM vs. Street GBP 145MM) and EBITDA (GBP 28.9MM vs. Street GBP 22MM), but the F27 EBITDA guidance mid-point fell well short at GBP 215MM vs. the Street's GBP 245MM. Shares are among today's earnings-linked underperformers given the disappointing forward guidance.

▼ PAYX Paychex Earnings — modest beat, soft revenue

Paychex delivered very modest EPS upside in FQ1 at $1.34 vs. the Street's $1.32, with the beat driven by operating margin expansion of 1.30% year-over-year to 42%. Sales were slightly light due to softness in the Management Solutions segment, which grew only 4%, while PEO/Insurance Solutions did better at +12% year-over-year. Prior F27 guidance is mostly unchanged, and shares are among today's underperformers.

◆ KBH KB Home Earnings — beat but weak guidance

KB Home reported decent FQ3 EPS upside at $1.05 vs. the Street's $0.89, driven largely by cost controls — the SG&A ratio beat expectations by ~0.30% — and modestly better deliveries. However, FQ4 guidance is mixed-to-underwhelming, with deliveries roughly in line but gross margin and SG&A ratio both coming in a bit worse than expected.

▼ AVGO Broadcom Geopolitical / regulatory risk — China hardware ban

Reports indicate China could issue formal guidance ordering data centers to reduce their use of Broadcom hardware as Beijing pushes the tech industry toward domestic products. This is a meaningful geopolitical risk overhang for the stock and is contributing to broader pressure across semiconductor names today.

◆ RCL Royal Caribbean M&A — acquisition announced

Royal Caribbean confirmed it will acquire a 50% stake in Sandals Resorts for $3 billion, representing a forward EBITDA multiple of approximately 10x. Management says the deal will be accretive to earnings next year, though shares are under pressure on the day amid broader weakness in travel names.

▲ BHF Brighthouse Financial M&A speculation / takeover interest

Brighthouse shares rallied in the final minutes of Tuesday's session after Bloomberg reported that Sixth Street remains interested in acquiring BHF if the existing Acquarian deal falls apart. This keeps a potential M&A floor under the stock and is being viewed positively by the market.

▲ WOR Worthington Enterprises Earnings beat

Worthington Enterprises posted modest FQ1 upside on EPS ($0.82 vs. Street $0.75), EBITDA ($74MM vs. Street $69.5MM), and sales, which climbed 13% to $344MM vs. the Street's $332MM. Shares are among today's earnings-linked outperformers on the strength of the across-the-board beat.
