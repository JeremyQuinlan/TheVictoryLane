MARKET OUTLOOK

The SPX is impressively extending its rally even as the equal-weight version sits in the red — a classic sign of mega-cap concentration doing the heavy lifting. Nvidia is the story of the day: SpaceX is standardizing its AI compute on Nvidia and Elon laid out an aggressive buildout from 1.4GW at end of Q2 to 10GW by end of 2027, which is a demand signal that towers over any macro noise. The ADP miss (+44K vs. +65K expected) and the ISM's employment and pricing deterioration are genuine soft spots that the market is choosing to overlook for now — that complacency could be tested Friday with the July jobs report. Gold and silver surging while energy fades suggests the market is hedging geopolitical tail risk quietly. Stay long quality tech and healthcare; be cautious on utilities and wireless tower names after the SpaceX DTC wireless broadside.

COMPANY NEWS

LLY (Eli Lilly) delivered a blowout Q2: EPS of $8.38 vs. $6.00 expected, revenue $23B vs. $20.6B. Mounjaro alone beat by ~$1B; Zepbound, Trulicity, and Jardiance all topped estimates. Full-year guidance raised.

SPCX (SpaceX) posted Q2 sales of $7.8B (+92% Y/Y vs. $6.8B expected) and adjusted EBITDA of $3.5B (+191% vs. $2B expected), but capex was enormous and free cash flow was deeply negative — the stock is under pressure. On the conf call, Elon confirmed SpaceX is standardizing AI compute on Nvidia and targeting 10GW of capacity by end-2027, sending NVDA sharply higher. SpaceX also flagged entry into the DTC wireless market in a way that could bypass tower infrastructure entirely, hammering AMT, CCI, SBAC, T, TMUS, and VZ.

DIS (Disney) reported FQ3 EPS of $2.06 vs. $1.86, op. income $5.55B vs. $5.23B, with Entertainment and Experiences driving the beat. F2026/F2027 guidance reiterated; management flagged softer-than-expected Moana box office and a weaker domestic SVOD ad environment heading into Q4.

SHOP (Shopify) posted GMV of $115.5B vs. $112.1B and free cash flow of $654MM vs. $550MM. Q3 guide calls for sales +31-33% vs. consensus +26.7%.

ANET (Arista) beat on EPS ($1.02 vs. $0.89), sales (+37.7% Y/Y to $3.03B vs. $2.82B), and guided Q3 revenue to $3.3B vs. $2.95B expected.

CVS reported Q2 EPS of $2.85 vs. $1.85, sales of $106B vs. $100B, with a medical cost ratio of 87.4% — roughly 200 basis points better than feared. Full-year EPS guide raised to ~$8 from ~$7.40.

AMD came in essentially inline for Q2 with Q3 sales guided to $13B vs. $12.5B, but gross margins of 56% fell ~25 basis points short — the stock is underperforming.

UBER posted Q2 EBITDA/revenue inline; bookings modestly beat on Delivery and Freight strength, but free cash flow of $2.8B missed the $3.2B consensus.

BKNG (Booking) beat on bookings ($51B vs. $49.7B) and EPS ($2.54 vs. $2.43), with management citing resilient travel demand into Q3.

WYNN reported revenue of $1.86B vs. $1.83B and property EBITDAR of $568.3MM vs. $556MM, with Macau driving the outperformance.
