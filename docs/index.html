VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top performers in the market today as Meta Muse is generating strong demand signals and Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is highly anticipated. OpenAI is reportedly considering a Muse-like agentic product in response, which validates the competitive significance of the launch. The Muse catalyst is also sparking a broader agentic AI sector reprice.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging alongside other CPU-linked names on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream consumer market — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This structural demand argument is being treated as a fresh tailwind for CPU suppliers. The move is sector-driven rather than company-specific news.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming today alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis as investors reprice CPU exposure higher. The narrative is that agentic AI workloads will drive meaningfully more CPU utilization than traditional inference tasks. This is a sector-level re-rate rather than Intel-specific news.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the broader PC supply chain. This is a demand signal that runs counter to the AI hardware enthusiasm elsewhere in tech today. The warning adds pressure to PC-adjacent names.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, clearing a major regulatory hurdle. The stock is jumping sharply on the news alongside Warner shares. The settlement reportedly involves Paramount agreeing to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the Paramount merger to proceed. The deal had faced meaningful legal risk from multiple state attorneys general. Resolution of this overhang is being treated as a significant positive for both companies.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company's Monday analyst meeting disappointed investors by failing to provide more granular financial targets. The market had been expecting clearer medium-term guidance and the absence of specifics is being read as a negative signal. This adds to recent pressure on the stock.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers that are experiencing traffic headwinds, driven by price promotions and package deals according to a Wall Street Journal report. The company appears to be successfully defending attendance through strategic discounting while competitors struggle. This is a relative positive for Disney in a softening leisure environment.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article flagging poor price action in debt tied to its massive data center project in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter, with efforts to offload the associated loans having 'hit a wall.' This raises questions about the financial structure and demand underpinning Oracle's large-scale infrastructure buildout. The article adds a cautionary note to Oracle's AI infrastructure narrative.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and other potash-linked names are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a 'massive' supply deal with Belarus at pricing 'substantially less' than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct competitive pricing threat to North American potash producers. Mosaic and Nutrien are also falling on the same headline.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with notable Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points, and Russell 2000 +50 basis points. The dominant macro themes are diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. The US and China held trade talks over the weekend with Bessent calling them 'very successful,' but the two sides failed to extend the existing trade truce, which doesn't expire until November 10.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on tone with a geopolitical relief bid layered in. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly relies on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the Treasury's ~20% target. No Fed commentary is a notable driver today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

AI agentic enthusiasm is exploding with Meta Muse demand signals and OpenAI reportedly rushing to build a competitive product, validating the theme broadly. CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel are catching a specific bid on the thesis that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior generations. Oil falling sharply on Houthi ceasefire hopes reduces inflation risk and boosts consumer-facing sectors. Treasuries are calm and yields are drifting lower, providing a supportive backdrop for growth and rate-sensitive equities.

BEAR CASE

The Nasdaq is doing nearly all the heavy lifting while equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points, signaling narrow market breadth that is historically fragile. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured over the weekend and expires November 10, keeping a policy risk overhang alive. Middle East tensions remain structurally elevated — the Houthis have dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, oil tanker costs are surging, and Iran has warned of fierce retaliation if military action resumes. Big Tech AI off-balance sheet liabilities are up roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, representing a growing hidden risk.

Market Outlook

Mildly bullish lean for today's session, driven by the Meta Muse AI demand surge and geopolitical de-escalation optimism pulling oil lower. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could extend or reset the AI agentic trade depending on what is revealed.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU names on the Meta Muse agentic AI theme. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi ceasefire hopes and diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly. Potash and materials names are under pressure after Trump's Belarus supply deal comments. REITs and data center industrials are catching a bid on lower yields and AI infrastructure demand. Transports are lagging with rails and package delivery names underperforming. Insurance and payments are also seeing weakness.
