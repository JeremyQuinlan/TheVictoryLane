VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point tailwind from tariff refunds and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps came in at plus 1.6% versus the Street's plus 3.1%, though management noted positive sales linearity in the quarter. The stock is rallying on that linearity commentary despite the soft underlying quality.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll delivered strong fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind still leaves EPS of 42 cents versus 36 cents expected. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged. The clean beat on the bottom line is the key takeaway.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR missed fiscal Q4 EPS with 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year over year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited the difficult macro landscape. The margin disappointment is the central concern for investors.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's Connect keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET. Shopify announced it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform, a significant commerce integration, while Amazon has blocked Muse from its storefront. OpenAI is now reportedly considering a competing product, validating the agentic AI race.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases directly on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful partnership that positions Shopify as an early beneficiary of agentic AI commerce. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a differentiated edge in the agentic checkout race.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's positioning in the Physical AI and robotics ecosystem.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares are under pressure after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The rate cuts directly pressure revenues and margins for the lab testing sector. LabCorp is seeing the same hit.

▼ LH LabCorp Other

LabCorp is falling alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS announcing larger-than-expected cuts to Medicare lab service payment rates. Both companies are direct casualties of the reimbursement reduction, creating a sector-wide negative reprice in lab testing.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in trial participants. The result is a meaningful clinical milestone that strengthens Viking's position in the highly competitive GLP-1 obesity drug space.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst day and outlined a 2029 sales target of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street at CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21%. The company also announced a $1 billion share buyback authorization, providing an additional capital return catalyst.
