Market Outlook

Neutral to bullish lean on legacy software as a group. The key catalyst to watch is confirmation of the Schneider Electric/PTC deal and any read-through to other legacy software names. The narrative around legacy software has shifted — AI is not disrupting these firms as feared, and in some cases is acting as a growth tailwind.

Sector Watch

Legacy software and IT services are in focus as M&A activity and solid earnings results drive a narrative shift. AI is no longer seen as an immediate disruptor to legacy firms and in some cases is acting as a growth tailwind. The Schneider/PTC deal, Silver Lake/Workday chatter, and strong results from Salesforce and Accenture have collectively propelled the group higher over the past two months.

▲ PTC PTC Inc. M&A

Schneider Electric is reportedly nearing a deal to acquire PTC for over $20 billion, per the Financial Times. A $20B equity value would imply roughly $185 per share, compared to PTC's all-time high near $215 hit in August 2025. This is a significant premium catalyst for PTC and a read-through for legacy industrial software broadly.

▲ WDAY Workday M&A

Silver Lake/Workday deal chatter, which first surfaced in Reuters on August 13, is cited as one of the recent data points helping to re-rate legacy software stocks higher. No new update in this note, but it remains a live catalyst for the sector.

▲ CRM Salesforce Earnings Beat

Salesforce's Q2 earnings report on August 26 is highlighted as a key data point that helped shift the narrative around legacy software. No new details provided, but it contributed to the broader sector re-rating thesis.

▲ ACN Accenture Earnings Beat

Accenture's fiscal Q4 earnings report on October 1 is cited as another positive data point supporting the legacy software and services re-rating. No specific numbers provided in this note beyond the date reference.
