Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 11:46 AM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is showing strong demand signals and is one of the top-performing stocks in the market today. Anticipation is building for Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, and reports indicate OpenAI is scrambling to develop a competing product — validating Meta's early-mover position in consumer-facing agentic AI.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is among the top outperformers today as traders pile into CPU-linked stocks on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse may help bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This creates a direct, durable revenue tailwind for AMD's processor business.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI/CPU-intensity thesis sparked by Meta Muse momentum. The market is repricing CPU-exposed names higher on the view that the next wave of AI compute demand will favor central processors over prior GPU-dominant workloads.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that pressures the broader PC supply chain. The warning is a negative read-through for component suppliers and signals consumer PC demand remains under pressure.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general, clearing a major legal obstacle that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The stock is jumping sharply on the news, as the path to completing the deal now appears significantly clearer.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are also rallying on the Paramount settlement news, which removes a key regulatory roadblock to the merger. The deal reportedly involves Paramount preserving CNN's editorial independence and potentially divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for clearer near-term guidance. The lack of specificity is being read as a negative signal on visibility into the GLP-1 franchise trajectory.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers who are experiencing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals. The WSJ report highlights Disney's ability to drive volume through strategic discounting while peers struggle, a positive differentiation in the leisure sector.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article highlighting poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the facility reportedly having hit a wall. This raises questions about the health of Oracle's hyperscale data center buildout strategy and its off-balance sheet obligations.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and the broader potash complex — including Mosaic and Nutrien — are slumping after Trump announced the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct pricing threat to North American potash and nitrogen producers.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher on Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance, as the SPX gains 94 basis points versus only 30 basis points for the equal-weight S&P, reflecting a tech-led rally. The key macro drivers are diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly — including a potential Trump-Iran meeting — and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off planned airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. China-US trade talks produced an AI dialogue framework but no extension of the existing trade truce, though the truce doesn't expire until November 10 so markets aren't alarmed.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting a mild risk-on but geopolitically cautious tone. The dollar is not a major focus today, with rates taking a back seat to equity and oil dynamics. Separately, Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt and above the Treasury's own 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse-driven AI rally is broad-based and has a credible fundamental thesis — agentic AI is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, creating a strong tailwind for AMD, ARM, and Intel. Diplomatic de-escalation hopes around the Middle East are pulling oil below $100, which is a macro tailwind for consumers and inflation. Treasuries are catching a modest bid, risk appetite is healthy across crypto, REITs, travel, and consumer discretionary, suggesting broad market participation beyond just mega-cap tech.

BEAR CASE

The equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points versus Nasdaq up 153 basis points, revealing that this rally is narrow and concentrated in AI-adjacent names — a fragile foundation. The Middle East situation remains highly unstable: the Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend, Iran is warning of fierce retaliation if the US re-engages militarily, and oil tanker costs are surging. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured, and OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of $280 billion through 2030 highlights the massive capital burn embedded in AI valuations.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean on the session, driven by two clear themes: the AI/agentic compute trade exploding higher on Meta Muse demand signals and Zuckerberg's upcoming keynote, and oil dropping sharply on geopolitical de-escalation hopes. The key catalyst to watch today and this week is Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night and any diplomatic developments at the UN General Assembly, particularly around Iran.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer, led by AI and CPU-linked names on Meta Muse/agentic AI optimism. Energy is underperforming sharply as Brent crude slumps nearly 400 basis points below $100 on Middle East de-escalation hopes. Potash and materials are under pressure on Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. REITs, data center-linked industrials, travel and leisure, crypto-linked stocks, and asset managers are all participating in the rally. Transports are lagging. Insurance and payments are also underperforming.
