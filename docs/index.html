VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

Fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 beat the Street's $54.03 but the quality was poor — 145 basis points of gross margin help came from tariff refunds and 105 basis points from a non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps of plus 1.6% missed the Street's plus 3.1% and the opex ratio worsened 100 basis points year over year. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final eight weeks and expressed confidence heading into fiscal 2027.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced an acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. RealSense is expected to generate $80 to $90 million in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth. The deal positions Cognex directly in the high-growth Physical AI and robotics perception market.

◆ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

Fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents beat the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat held even excluding an 11-cent tariff refund tailwind — adjusted EPS of 42 cents still topped consensus. Sales missed, falling 3.3% organically to $923.4 million versus the Street's $943.2 million, though orders rose 3.5% year over year. Full-year fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed to $3.88 to $4.03 billion from $3.93 to $4.13 billion while EPS guidance was left unchanged at $1.85 to $2.15.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Pentair named Bob Hau as its new CFO effective November 1st, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman staying through that date to ensure a smooth transition. Hau brings prior CFO experience at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International. This is a leadership transition event with no financial results attached.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

Fiscal Q4 EPS of 78 cents missed the Street's 87 cents as gross margins fell 230 basis points year over year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85%, more than offsetting slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited stubborn interest rates, elevated fuel costs, and inflationary pressures weighing on household budgets and retail demand. Bright spots include healthy dealer inventory levels, structural cost actions underway, and a surge of roughly 75% in North American towable RV order backlog year over year, though no formal fiscal 2027 guidance was provided.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking shares spiked in pre-market trading after reporting positive Phase trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in participants. The result is a significant positive catalyst for the company's GLP-1 program and adds competitive pressure to the broader obesity drug landscape. This is a meaningful clinical milestone that validates VK2735 as a credible contender in the GLP-1 space.
