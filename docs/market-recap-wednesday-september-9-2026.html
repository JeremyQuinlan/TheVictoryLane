RATES & FED

Treasuries sold off hard Wednesday, with yields jumping 3–5 basis points across the curve, pressured by spiking oil prices and a Treasury buyback that disappointed — the $6B operation beat the "at least $4B" initial guidance but fell well short of the $10B+ some had hoped for. The Fed is now priced for ~36 basis points of rate hikes this year, with roughly 60% odds of a move at the September 16 meeting.

MARKET OUTLOOK

The bear case is getting harder to dismiss: you've got oil above $100, yields climbing, Mid-term political risk rising, and a fiscal picture that isn't improving. Tech is doing its best to hold the tape together — the AI enthusiasm around Astra and Meta's Muse is real, and conference circuit commentary has been broadly constructive — but it can't absorb every macro shock indefinitely. Watch Thursday's PPI and Friday's CPI closely; if inflation reaccelerates on the back of energy, the Fed's path gets uncomfortable fast. The FOMC decision next Wednesday is shaping up to be the most consequential near-term catalyst.

GEOPOLITICAL

- Trump stated Wednesday the war won't end until after the midterms, offering zero strategic clarity on a path to de-escalation (Politico).

- Iranian officials told Bloomberg the regime has "enough missiles for a lengthy conflict" and will not relent under US economic pressure (Bloomberg, NYT).

- Pakistan pledged to honor its defense pact with Saudi Arabia if the Kingdom is attacked (Bloomberg).

- Iran is increasingly turning to cryptocurrency to circumvent US sanctions (FT).

- CIA director Ratcliffe is set to take a more prominent role in Russia-Ukraine negotiations — a development welcomed in Europe (FT).

- The White House escalated trade tensions with Canada, warning of potential bans on alcohol, motorcycles, and dairy imports effective September 29 (Washington Post).

COMPANY NEWS

AAPL (Apple) held its product event Wednesday. The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max get $100 price increases — low-end of the $100–200 range anticipated. The headline is the iPhone Duo (foldable), priced at $1,999 — below the $2–2.5K consensus — and widely seen as Apple's most significant launch since the iPhone. Early production reports suggest manufacturing hiccups limiting output to a few hundred units per day (Nikkei); if Apple can hit ~10M units in year one, that's roughly $20B of incremental revenue, a ~4–5% top-line bump.

CMCSA (Comcast) warned at the Goldman conference that fiber competitor pricing is "irrational," which will weigh on broadband net adds. Stock sold off; CHTR fell in sympathy.

CHWY (Chewy) posted a beat-and-raise — Q2 EBITDA $226.7MM vs. Street $212MM, revenue +7.3% to $3.33B — but management guided for margin pressure in Q3, which is what the market focused on.

ASO (Academy Sports) beat and raised on Q2 EPS (helped by tariff refunds), with comps roughly inline. A notably cleaner print than recent updates from DKS and ONON.

SIG (Signet) beat on FQ2 EPS ($2.19 vs. Street $1.73, tariff refunds a factor) and comps (+2.2% vs. Street +1.7%); full-year EPS guide raised to $10.45–12.15 from $9.20–11.

SUNB (Sunbelt Rentals) posted Q2 EBITDA of $1.315B vs. Street $1.245B, with margins of 42.2% (~60 basis points above consensus) and revenue +11.2% to $3.115B. Full-year guide raised. URI climbed in sympathy.

SYY (Sysco) reaffirmed F27 guidance, unveiled a $500MM AI-powered efficiency program targeting cost synergies by F29, and raised long-term targets to sales +4–7% and EPS +9–11%.

KMB (Kimberly-Clark) warned at Barclays that Q3 will miss due to a product launch delay, distributor disruption, and freight costs — stock dropped; CLX, GIS, and PG fell in sympathy.

SFD (Smithfield Foods) preannounced FQ3 operating income of $115–175MM vs. consensus ~$296MM, citing hog market spread compression. Second major protein downside alert in a week after TSN's guidance cut.

META (Meta) formally launched Muse, a personal AI agent, on Tuesday. The firm's Watermelon frontier model could launch within weeks.

DIS (Disney) reaffirmed ~12% EPS growth in fiscal 2026 at the Goldman conference, with another year of double-digit growth expected in F27.
