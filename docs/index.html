VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is driving the entire AI complex higher today on reports of strong consumer demand for the agentic AI app. Investors are also anticipating Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could be the next major catalyst. Meta is one of the top-performing names in the entire market today.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help push into the mainstream consumer market — is far more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, creating a direct demand tailwind for AMD's processor business. The stock is among the top outperformers in tech today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying sharply alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis. The market is repricing CPU-linked names higher on the view that Muse-style agentic applications will require significantly more compute per task than traditional generative AI workloads.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the stock. This is a notable negative data point for the broader PC supply chain and component makers.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with reports indicating Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The stock is jumping sharply on the news as the deal overhang clears.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the primary legal obstacle to the two companies' merger proceeding. The deal resolution removes a significant regulatory uncertainty that had been hanging over both stocks.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for greater visibility into the obesity drug franchise's financial trajectory.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the facility described as having 'hit a wall.' This raises questions about the financial structure underpinning Oracle's aggressive AI infrastructure buildout.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers, many of whom are seeing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals according to a Wall Street Journal report. This is a positive data point for Disney's core consumer business at a time when the broader theme park industry is under pressure.

▼ MOS Mosaic Other

Potash names including Mosaic, CF Industries, and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus priced substantially below current Canadian potash prices. The news is a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers and the group is among the worst performers in the market today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse agentic AI wave is igniting a broad tech rally with real fundamental legs — agentic AI is far more CPU-intensive than prior iterations, creating a genuine demand catalyst for AMD, ARM, and Intel. Diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly, including a potential Trump-Iran meeting, is delivering a meaningful oil price relief valve that supports consumer and industrial sentiment. Treasuries are catching a bid alongside equities, suggesting this is not a pure risk-on melt-up but a more credible macro re-rating. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes an overhang and adds a media M&A spark to the session.

BEAR CASE

The rally is extremely narrow — equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points versus Nasdaq at 153 basis points, signaling the breadth is thin and concentrated in a handful of mega-cap AI names. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured Sunday despite 'very successful' talks, leaving a live cliff-edge in November that markets may be underpricing. Middle East geopolitical risk remains elevated — the Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend, Iran is warning of fierce retaliation to any US military action, and the State Department is flagging rapid escalation risk. AI off-balance-sheet liabilities for Big Tech have surged roughly $300 billion in just 12 months, a growing and underappreciated risk for the sector leading today's rally.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously bullish today, driven by two distinct catalysts — the Meta Muse-driven AI frenzy lifting Nasdaq names and the oil drop on Middle East de-escalation hopes. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could extend or deflate the AI enthusiasm; also watch whether US-China trade truce extension talks progress before the November 10 expiration deadline.
