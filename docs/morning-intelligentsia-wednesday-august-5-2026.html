GEOPOLITICAL

- Iran/Hormuz: Trump echoed Bessent's comment that a deal announcement could arrive within days — but investors should be clear-eyed: this "deal" means Iran assuming control of Hormuz and imposing fees on passing ships, not a genuine peace. US munition stockpiles are critically depleted (Reuters), leaving Washington with no credible military leverage. Expect major exemptions for China and others, but this is structurally worse than the pre-war status quo (NYT).

- US-Brazil: Washington revoked the Brazilian ambassador's visa as diplomatic tensions escalate (WSJ).

- China drones: Beijing tightened drone export controls targeting the US (Bloomberg).

- IEEPA tariff refunds: Washington has issued ~$100B in IEEPA tariff refunds per a court filing — roughly 60% of total collected (FT).

COMPANY NEWS
