VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — 145 basis points of gross margin tailwind came from tariff refunds and 105 basis points from a non-cash LIFO impact. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive comp linearity in the quarter. Operating expenses worsened to 33.4% of sales, up 100 basis points year over year.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding an 11-cent tariff refund tailwind — core EPS of 42 cents still topped consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year over year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited the difficult macro landscape.

▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, in contrast to Amazon which blocked Muse. Investor expectations are growing increasingly bullish into Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday at 7pm ET.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, differentiating it from Amazon which has blocked Muse. This positions Shopify as a key beneficiary of agentic AI commerce adoption.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the study. This is a meaningful data point in the competitive obesity drug landscape.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market anticipated. Lab testing stocks broadly under pressure on the news.

▼ LH LabCorp Other

LabCorp shares fell alongside Quest Diagnostics after new CMS Medicare lab service payment rates came in with a larger-than-expected reduction. Both names are underperforming meaningfully on the day.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million contingent consideration.

▲ ONON On Holding Buyback

On Holding held its analyst meeting and outlined plans to achieve 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21%. The company also adopted a $1 billion buyback authorization.
