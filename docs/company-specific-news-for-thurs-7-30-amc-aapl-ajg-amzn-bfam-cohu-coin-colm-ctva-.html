MARKET OUTLOOK

The earnings tape tonight is telling two very different stories. Mega-cap tech is broadly delivering, but the quality of the beats matters — AAPL's China weakness and Services miss are real problems for a stock priced on ecosystem premium, while AMZN's AWS numbers are simply extraordinary. The hyperscaler capex arms race is accelerating fast, with AMZN hiking full-year capex guidance to ~$220B, and free cash flow deeply negative at -$9B — investors cheering AWS growth should keep one eye on the cash burn. Industrials are getting a tariff-refund tailwind that flatters headline EPS, so strip those out before drawing conclusions on underlying demand.

COMPANY NEWS

AAPL (Apple) posted FQ3 EPS of $2.02 vs. the Street's $1.89, with sales up 16% to $109.4B — but roughly $0.11 of that EPS beat came from tariff refunds. Mac was a standout at $10.35B vs. the Street's $8.6B, but Services badly disappointed at $30.74B vs. $31.58B expected, and Greater China revenue came in at $18.8B vs. the Street's $19.5B. The September quarter top-line outlook of ~10% growth is roughly 200 basis points below consensus, with management citing FX headwinds and supply chain constraints.

AMZN (Amazon) delivered Q2 operating income of $27.5B vs. the Street's $23.6B, on sales up 20% to $200.6B. AWS grew 37% on an FXN basis — fastest in 18 quarters — beating consensus by more than 500 basis points, with AWS operating margins of 39.4%, nearly 600 basis points above plan. Advertising accelerated 400 basis points to +26% growth. The catch: free cash flow was -$9B vs. the Street's -$1B forecast, and full-year capex guidance was raised to ~$220B from ~$200B. September quarter revenue guidance of $199.5B at the midpoint missed the Street's $204B.

MPWR (Monolithic Power) beat Q2 EPS at $6.50 vs. the Street's $5.87, with sales of $980.6MM vs. $903MM expected, driven by the Enterprise Data segment. Q3 sales guidance of $1.14-1.16B towers over the Street's $986.4MM.

RDDT (Reddit) beat on Q2 EBITDA at $343MM vs. the Street's $295MM, with DAUs roughly inline and ARPU driving the revenue upside. Q3 guidance for both sales and EBITDA is above consensus.

COIN (Coinbase) missed Q2 EBITDA badly at $208MM vs. the Street's $322MM, with sales of $1.22B falling short of $1.29B as total crypto spot trading volume fell 25% Q/Q and volatility stayed low. Sentiment was already cautious, and the full-year expense guide was trimmed to $4.2-4.45B.

LYV (Live Nation) posted Q2 adjusted operating income of $817MM vs. $785MM expected, on sales of $7.66B vs. $7.53B, with Concerts and Ticketing both outperforming. Management called it "the strongest concert ticket sales we've ever seen."

SYK (Stryker) beat Q2 EPS at $3.69 vs. the Street's $3.48, with operating margins up 170 basis points year-over-year, but organic revenue growth of 9% was inline and the full-year guide wasn't raised — a disappointment given the beat.

MHK (Mohawk) posted Q2 EPS of $3.67; even ex-tariff refunds, EPS of $3.04 crushed the Street's $2.59 on volume growth, pricing, mix, and share gains. Q3 ex-tariff EPS guide of ~$2.43 is modestly ahead of the Street's $2.40.

MTD (Mettler-Toledo) delivered Q2 EPS of $11.46 vs. the Street's $10.80, citing better-than-expected organic sales growth including strong China performance, with the full-year EPS guide nudged higher.

EMN (Eastman) beat Q2 EPS at $1.97 vs. $1.81, with EBIT margins up 350 basis points Q/Q, and Q3 EPS guidance of $1.97 also topped the Street's $1.89.

DXCM (DexCom) beat Q2 EPS at 70c vs. 61c with full-year margin and earnings guidance raised; revenue growth outlook unchanged at +11-13%.

ILMN (Illumina) reported Q2 EPS of $1.31 and sales of $1.159B vs. the Street's $1.23 and $1.131B; full-year EPS guide raised to $5.30-5.40 from $5.15-5.30.

RBLX (Roblox) missed on DAUs (123MM vs. 128.7MM expected) and hours engaged (29B vs. 30.8B), with bookings at the low end of guidance and Q3 bookings guidance below consensus.

COHU beat Q2 EPS at 26c vs. 14c and guided Q3 sales to $170MM vs. the Street's $146MM, citing AI compute momentum with its pipeline raised to $850MM.

FND (Floor & Décor) posted Q2 EPS of 58c vs. 56c, with comps of -2.1% better than the feared -3.7%, and full-year EBITDA guide nudged to $550-585MM.

CTVA (Corteva) beat Q2 EPS at $2.30 vs. $2.24 and raised full-year EPS guidance to $3.60-3.80, though quarterly sales fell slightly short.

IR (Ingersoll Rand) beat modestly on EPS/revenue and raised full-year revenue guidance to +4.5-6.5%, though EBITDA margin guidance was trimmed.

MTZ (MasTec) missed on EBITDA margins (~8.8% vs. ~9% expected) and free cash flow (-$59MM vs. +$172MM expected), but raised full-year sales guidance to $18.2B and EBITDA to $1.6B.

AJG (Arthur Gallagher) beat Q2 EPS by 2 cents at $2.84, with organic growth slightly ahead in Risk Management.

BFAM (Bright Horizons) beat Q2 revenue/EBITDA ($779.2MM/$130.5MM vs. $125.4MM expected) and raised full-year EPS guide to $5.05-5.15.
