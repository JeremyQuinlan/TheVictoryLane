Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is generating real, measurable demand — not just hype — and that enthusiasm is lifting the broader AI ecosystem. CPU-linked stocks like AMD, ARM, and Intel are catching a bid as agentic AI infrastructure requires significantly more CPU than prior GPU-heavy architectures, opening a new demand vector. If Muse adoption continues to accelerate, it could re-anchor the bull case for AI infrastructure spending.

BEAR CASE

SB Energy's IPO delay — the second AI-linked IPO pulled in a week following Holtec — signals that institutional investors are pushing back on elevated valuations in the data center space. Anthropic has already been pushed from an October to a November IPO target, adding to the sense that the primary market for AI is losing momentum. A sustained freeze in AI IPOs could signal that smart money is growing skeptical of the sector's near-term growth assumptions.

Market Outlook

Mixed leaning. The Muse-driven AI rally provides a bullish surface read, but the SB Energy IPO delay and broader cooling in data center sentiment suggest the market is becoming more discriminating. The key catalyst to watch is whether IPO pushback spreads further and begins to weigh on AI infrastructure names.

Sector Watch

AI infrastructure and data center names are under pressure from cooling IPO sentiment, with two AI-linked IPOs delayed in one week (SB Energy and Holtec). CPU-linked semiconductor stocks are outperforming on the agentic AI demand narrative, marking a potential rotation away from GPU-centric plays toward CPU exposure.
