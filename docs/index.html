Macro · Rates

This is a company-specific earnings and news digest for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, covering BMO reporters and pre-market catalysts. No broad index or overnight macro commentary is provided in this edition. Risk tone must be inferred from the individual company results, which are mixed overall.

Market Outlook

No explicit directional market call is made in this edition. The earnings slate is mixed — JBL and CAG beat on earnings but guidance nuances temper enthusiasm, while CALM is a clear drag. AI-linked names remain under a high-bar scrutiny lens per Adam's note on JBL.

Sector Watch

Cruise sector showing back-to-back positive data points with both Carnival and Norwegian flagging better-than-expected results, suggesting sustained consumer demand. AI infrastructure and networking remain high-bar trades where even large beats may not move stocks meaningfully to the upside. Egg and agricultural commodity names face ongoing pricing headwinds.

VK · Key Names
▲ AAPL Apple Product Launch

Apple will launch a new smart home device on October 13, marking its entry into a category the company views as strategically critical. This is a long-anticipated product designed to expand Apple's ecosystem footprint into the home. The launch date is now confirmed, giving investors a concrete catalyst to watch.

◆ CAG Conagra Earnings Beat

Conagra beat FQ1 EPS handily at 41 cents versus the Street's 29 cents, with adjusted operating margins of 11.5% crushing the 9.2% estimate. Sales were roughly inline with organic revenue down 1.1% versus the expected negative 1.3%. However, full-year guidance was left unchanged, implying the beat will not carry forward and that FQ2 through FQ4 face a softer outlook.

▼ CALM Cal-Maine Foods Earnings Miss

Cal-Maine posted a deeply disappointing FQ1 with EPS of negative $1.26 versus the Street's negative $0.77 estimate, and revenue collapsed 41.5% to $539 million versus the $552 million consensus. Conventional egg sales fell nearly 60% year-over-year with a negative 35.2% operating margin, the core driver of the disaster. Because EPS was negative, the company's variable dividend policy means no dividend will be paid this quarter.

▲ CI Cigna Guidance Raise

Cigna reaffirmed its 2026 full-year guidance and issued a long-term financial target of 10 to 14% EPS CAGR through 2030. Using the midpoint of 12% growth off $30.45 in 2026 EPS implies 2030 EPS of roughly $48, which is above the current Street consensus of $45.82. The reaffirmation and above-consensus long-term target provide a modest positive catalyst.

◆ FDS FactSet Research Systems Guidance Cut

FactSet beat FQ4 EPS by delivering $4.52 versus the $4.37 estimate, up 11.6%, with organic revenue growth of 7.1% topping the 5.75% estimate. However, operating margins contracted 80 basis points year-over-year to 33% due to higher compensation and technology costs. F27 guidance midpoints disappointed on both organic ASV growth at 5.75% versus the 6% consensus and EPS at $19.45 versus $19.76, which Adam says will likely pressure the stock.

◆ GIS General Mills Other

General Mills announced a CEO transition effective January 1, 2027, with COO Dana McNabb succeeding Jeff Harmening as Chief Executive Officer. Harmening will move to the role of Executive Chair of the Board. The leadership change is a known transition from within the existing management team.

▲ HPE Hewlett Packard Enterprise Guidance Raise

HPE raised its F27 Networking segment revenue growth guidance to high teens to low 20% year-over-year, up from the prior 14 to 17% range. Juniper integration cost synergy targets were also raised to $800 million from the prior floor of at least $600 million. Data Center Networking revenue is expected to achieve a low-to-high 50% CAGR through F29, a notably aggressive long-term outlook.

◆ HRL Hormel Foods M&A

Hormel announced the acquisition of Brakebush Brothers, a leading value-added chicken producer, from the Brakebush family for $1.055 billion. The deal is expected to be accretive to adjusted EPS beginning in fiscal year 2028. This represents a meaningful strategic expansion into the value-added poultry category.

◆ JBL Jabil Earnings Beat

Jabil crushed FQ4 estimates with EPS of $4.40 versus $4.07 and revenue of $10.6 billion versus the $9.7 billion estimate, while FQ1 and full F27 guidance midpoints are well above consensus on both EPS and revenue. The Intelligent Infrastructure segment — the AI-linked business — grew 56% to $5.85 billion but margins at 6.5% came in just below the 6.65% estimate. Adam cautions that the bar for AI names is very high and what looks like a massive beat on paper may ultimately be treated as merely inline.

▲ NCLH Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Guidance Raise

Norwegian Cruise Line said Q3 results will come in ahead of prior guidance while its full 2026 outlook is affirmed. This is the second positive cruise industry data point in two days, following Carnival's encouraging report on Tuesday. The read-through suggests continued resilience in consumer demand for cruise travel.
