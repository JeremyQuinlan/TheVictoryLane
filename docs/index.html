VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top-performing names in the market today as Meta Muse demand indications are strong and investors anticipate Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing agentic AI product in response, which validates the significance of Muse's consumer AI push. The broader AI complex is repricing higher on the thesis that Muse could bring agentic AI to the mainstream.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging alongside other CPU-linked stocks on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse may help bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI generations. This creates a direct demand tailwind for AMD's CPU business. The Meta Muse catalyst is being read as a sector reprice event for CPU makers.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis sparked by Meta Muse optimism. The idea is that mainstream adoption of agentic AI workloads would drive meaningfully higher CPU demand, benefiting Intel's core business. This is a sentiment and narrative-driven move rather than company-specific news.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with Bloomberg confirming a deal has been reached. The settlement reportedly involves preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Shares are jumping on the news as the key regulatory hurdle to the Warner deal appears cleared.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are rising alongside Paramount after the attorney general settlement clears the path for the two companies' merger to proceed. The deal removes the most significant remaining regulatory obstacle to the combination. Investors are now focused on closing timing and deal mechanics.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the PC supply chain. This is consistent with Acer's CEO comments that Chinese memory supply is easing component shortages and capping prices, suggesting a soft demand rather than supply environment. The guidance is a near-term negative for the PC ecosystem.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for greater visibility on the obesity drug franchise. The lack of specific guidance is being read as a negative signal about near-term confidence in the business trajectory. This follows a period of heightened competitive pressure in the GLP-1 space.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday, focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about the health of Oracle's hyperscale data center expansion strategy. This is an emerging credit-market signal worth monitoring given Oracle's aggressive infrastructure buildout.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers according to a Wall Street Journal report, with price promotions and package deals helping Disney defy an industry-wide traffic slowdown. Many competitors are seeing headwinds while Disney is using strategic pricing to defend attendance. This is a relative positive for Disney's parks segment within a challenged leisure environment.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below current Canadian potash rates. This directly undercuts the pricing power of North American potash and fertilizer producers. The geopolitical angle — sourcing from sanctioned Belarus — adds complexity but the market is trading the demand destruction for incumbent suppliers.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher on Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points. The two dominant macro themes are an AI stock surge driven by Meta Muse optimism and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off Houthi airstrikes at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is also running high ahead of the UN General Assembly in New York, with hopes for Middle East de-escalation including a potential Trump-Iran summit.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve. The dollar backdrop is relatively calm as risk appetite is concentrated in tech rather than broad cyclicals. Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt — above the department's stated 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is driving genuine excitement around consumer agentic AI, and the OpenAI competitive response validates that this is a real product cycle worth repricing. CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel are benefiting from the thesis that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior AI iterations. Oil's sharp decline on Houthi de-escalation hope removes a key macro headwind and lifts travel, leisure, and consumer sectors. Treasuries catching a bid alongside equities suggests a benign, low-volatility risk environment.

BEAR CASE

Breadth is narrow — the equal-weight S&P is barely positive while Nasdaq surges, suggesting the rally is concentrated and fragile. The US-China trade truce was not extended at Sunday's talks despite optimistic rhetoric, leaving the November 10 expiration as a live risk overhang. Big Tech AI off-balance sheet liabilities have risen roughly $300 billion in the last 12 months, a growing but underappreciated risk. Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East remain elevated — the Houthi de-escalation is unconfirmed and Iran has explicitly warned against any new US military action.

Market Outlook

Mildly bullish tilt for the session with a narrow AI-driven leadership profile. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night, which is pulling forward AI enthusiasm today. Oil's drop is a tailwind for consumers and risk sentiment broadly, but the narrow breadth — equal-weight S&P lagging Nasdaq by over 120 basis points — suggests this is a tech-specific rally, not a broad risk-on move.

Sector Watch

AI and CPU-linked tech is the clear outperformer today on Meta Muse/agentic AI optimism — names like AMD, ARM, Intel, Marvell, Palantir, and Cadence all moving higher. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi de-escalation hopes, hitting APA, COP, Devon, EOG, and Exxon. Potash and fertilizer names are being hit hard by Trump's Belarus supply deal comments. Travel and leisure are catching a bid from lower oil prices. REITs and data center industrials are also outperforming. Transports are lagging despite oil's decline, with rails and parcel names all lower.
