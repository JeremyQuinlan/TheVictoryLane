VK · Key Names
◆ SPCX SpaceX Earnings Beat

SpaceX reported Q3 revenue of $14.6B, up 161% year-over-year and well above the Street's $12.8B estimate, with EBITDA of $8.8B versus consensus of $6.6B. The Super Intelligence segment was the standout, growing 830% driven by new cloud services agreements, the Cursor acquisition, and Grok adoption. However, Connectivity subscriber numbers missed at 13.6MM vs. 14.2MM expected, and the market's real focus is on Musk's call commentary around compute targets, a $100B ARR goal, new cloud contracts, Starlink Mobile, and potential Tesla combination.

▲ GOOGL Alphabet Partnership

SpaceX referenced an existing cloud services compute deal with Google, and investors will be watching for any update on the scope or expansion of that partnership on the earnings call.

▲ AMZN Anthropic (Amazon-backed) Partnership

Anthropic is cited as an existing SpaceX cloud compute customer. Any color on the size or renewal of that agreement could be a read-through for AI infrastructure demand.

▼ T AT&T Other

SpaceX's Starlink Mobile ambitions are framed as a potential competitive threat to AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon, though skepticism remains high given spectrum constraints and the massive capex a nationwide network would require.

▼ TMUS T-Mobile Other

T-Mobile is named alongside AT&T and Verizon as an incumbent that Starlink Mobile could theoretically threaten, though the competitive risk is seen as limited given SpaceX's spectrum and financial constraints.

▼ VZ Verizon Other

Verizon is included in the group of carriers facing potential long-term disruption from Starlink Mobile, with the caveat that most analysts remain skeptical of SpaceX's ability to execute a nationwide consumer wireless network.

◆ TSLA Tesla M&A

Speculation about a SpaceX and Tesla combination has been circulating for some time and has intensified following the recent Roadster unveil, which incorporated SpaceX technology. Investors will be listening closely to Musk on the call for any commentary on a potential merger.
