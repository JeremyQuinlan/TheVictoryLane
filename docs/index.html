VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant market catalyst today, with reports of strong consumer demand for the app and Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night acting as a major anticipated event. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing agentic AI product, validating the significance of the Muse launch. Meta is one of the top performers in the entire market today.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

Agentic AI, which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream consumer market, is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, making AMD a direct beneficiary. The stock is outperforming broadly today alongside the AI theme. This represents a sector reprice of CPU-linked names on the agentic AI narrative.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the thesis that agentic AI compute demands are heavily CPU-intensive, directly benefiting traditional CPU makers. This is a thematic sector reprice rather than company-specific news. Intel is listed as one of the top outperformers in tech today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. guided that PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the stock today. This is a headwind for the broader PC supply chain at a time when the rest of tech is surging. The news contrasts sharply with the AI-driven enthusiasm elsewhere in tech.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general that clears the key legal obstacle threatening to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The stock is jumping sharply on the news, as the deal now appears on track to close. This resolves a major overhang that had been building since advanced settlement talks were reported over the weekend.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the merger to proceed. The deal reportedly involves Paramount agreeing to preserve CNN's editorial independence and potentially divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Both stocks are among the top performers in the media sector today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting. Investors were disappointed by the lack of specificity despite the company touting future blockbuster product launches. This is a catalyst miss at a high-profile capital markets day event.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals according to the Wall Street Journal. This is a positive differentiation story for Disney at a time when the broader travel and leisure sector is under pressure. The commentary adds a bullish data point for Disney's most important earnings segment.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday, focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising concerns about Oracle's aggressive data center expansion strategy. This adds a credit-market-level risk signal to Oracle's AI infrastructure build-out.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and other potash names including Mosaic and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus priced substantially below current Canadian potash prices. This is a direct pricing threat to North American fertilizer producers and a significant negative for the sector. The news hit the group hard with CF, MOS, and NTR all falling together.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher on Monday with notable Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The key macro drivers are diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly, where Trump may meet Iran's president, and a steep drop in oil after Trump called off planned Houthi airstrikes at the last minute. The US-China trade truce extension remains unresolved but the November 10 deadline limits immediate urgency, while both sides agreed to a new AI dialogue framework.

Treasuries are seeing a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, consistent with the risk-on but geopolitically cautious tone. The dollar and Fed policy are not a primary focus today. Treasury plans to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt — above the department's own target of roughly 20%.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is driving a massive repricing of AI-linked stocks, particularly CPU names like AMD, ARM, and Intel, as agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior AI iterations. Diplomatic de-escalation hopes at the UN General Assembly are bringing oil down sharply, which is broadly deflationary and supportive of consumer spending. Treasuries are catching a bid, which helps rate-sensitive sectors like REITs, and the Paramount-Warner deal clearing a major legal hurdle adds a positive M&A sentiment boost.

BEAR CASE

Market breadth is weak — the equal-weight S&P is up just 30 basis points while Nasdaq surges, suggesting this rally is narrow and momentum-driven rather than fundamentally broad. The Middle East situation remains genuinely dangerous — Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia escalated sharply over the weekend and oil tanker costs are surging, meaning the geopolitical risk premium in energy could snap back fast. The US-China trade truce was not extended on Sunday, and with the November 10 deadline approaching, that overhang is building. Big Tech's off-balance-sheet AI liability exposure is up roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, a growing risk not yet priced in.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean, driven by two powerful themes: the AI agentic computing surge tied to Meta Muse and the oil price decline on Middle East de-escalation hopes. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote Wednesday night at the Connect Conference, which could extend or deflate the AI rally. Breadth is narrower than the headline suggests — equal-weight S&P is lagging badly — so this is a momentum and mega-cap tech story more than a broad market lift.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU-linked names on the Meta Muse agentic AI theme. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Middle East de-escalation hopes, hitting names like COP, EOG, OXY, and XOM. Potash and materials are getting hit on the potential Belarus supply deal. REITs are catching a bid with yields lower. Data center industrials like Eaton and GE Vernova are rallying on AI infrastructure demand. Travel and leisure names are broadly higher. Transports are lagging despite the macro-positive backdrop.
