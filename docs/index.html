▲ ASML ASML Holding Earnings Beat

ASML reported Q3 EPS of EU12.93, well above the Street's EU10.71, driven by gross margins roughly 140 basis points better than expected and operating margins of 44.4% versus the 41.8% consensus. Revenue was a hair light at EU11.66B versus EU11.7B expected. Q4 guidance calls for sales of ~EU14.7B at the midpoint versus the EU14B consensus and gross margins of 57% versus the 56.4% estimate. Management noted customers are placing orders through 2029 and 2030 on AI-driven demand, and the company is now investigating capacity increases for 2028 beyond the previously communicated 30% ramp.
