VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone beat FQ4 EPS estimates ($56.05 vs. $54.03 consensus), but the quality was low — the upside was driven by tariff refunds (145bp gross margin tailwind) and a non-cash LIFO benefit (105bp tailwind) rather than core execution. Domestic comps missed badly at +1.6% vs. the Street's +3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100bp year-over-year. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final 8 weeks and expressed confidence heading into fiscal 2027.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500MM upfront plus an additional ~$106.5MM. RealSense is expected to generate $80-90MM in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth, making this a meaningful bet on the robotics and Physical AI buildout.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll delivered solid FQ1 EPS upside (53c vs. 36c consensus), and importantly the beat holds up even excluding an 11-cent tariff refund tailwind — adjusted EPS was 42c, still ahead of the Street. Sales missed, declining 3.3% organically to $923.4MM vs. $943.2MM expected, though orders rose 3.5% year-over-year. Full-year sales guidance was trimmed modestly while EPS guidance was left unchanged, and FQ2 EPS guidance is roughly in line with consensus.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Pentair named Bob Hau as its new CFO effective November 1st, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman staying on through that date to ensure a smooth transition. Hau brings significant blue-chip CFO experience, having previously held the role at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International.

◆ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed FQ4 EPS estimates (78c vs. 87c consensus) as gross margins fell 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4%, well below the Street's 13.85% estimate, despite revenue coming in slightly above expectations at $2.311B. Management struck a cautious tone, citing stubborn interest rates, elevated fuel costs, and inflation pressuring household budgets, and declined to provide formal fiscal 2027 guidance. Bright spots included healthy dealer inventory levels, structural cost actions underway, and a dramatic ~75% surge in North American towable RV order backlog year-over-year.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics shares spiked in pre-market trading after reporting positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, with patients achieving 22% weight loss. This is a highly significant data readout that positions VK2735 as a competitive asset in the crowded but high-stakes obesity drug market.
