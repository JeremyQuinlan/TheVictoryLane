MARKET OUTLOOK

The macro calendar is front-loaded with risk this week, and the jobs number on Friday is the one that matters most. A rebound from July's ugly -23K print is expected (+60K consensus), but the bar is low enough that a modest beat could still firm up sentiment without forcing the Fed's hand. Tech earnings are the other driver — AVGO in particular needs to match Nvidia's energy after that 70% F28 sales guide lit up the tape. The AI infrastructure buildout shows no signs of slowing, but frothy valuations across the board mean execution has to be near-perfect. One soft quarter from a bellwether here and the repricing will be swift.

GEOPOLITICAL

- The G20 finance ministers and central bank governors convene August 31–September 1, a potential forum for currency and trade policy signals worth monitoring closely given the current macro backdrop.

COMPANY NEWS

MDT (Medtronic) reports FQ1 Tuesday pre-market. Management's portfolio reshuffling, new product launches, and solid execution have driven top-line momentum, leaving sentiment relatively constructive. Street expects organic revenue growth of ~11.3%, operating margins of 23.8%, and EPS of $1.39.

CRDO (Credo Technology) reports FQ1 Tuesday post-market. AI-linked demand is driving explosive growth — Street models revenue +112% to $473MM, gross margins ~68%, operating margins ~49.3%, and EPS +122% to $1.15. The concern is that expectations may already be too elevated, though surging EPS estimates have helped compress the PE multiple.

DELL (Dell Technologies) reports FQ2 Tuesday post-market. Sentiment is extremely bullish heading in, with SMCI's blowout results ratcheting up the bar further. Street looks for total revenue +51% to $44.92B (ISG +69.3% to $28.5B, CSG +20.3% to $15.04B), AI-optimized server sales +95% to $15.98B, operating margins +160 basis points to 9.3%, and EPS +111% to $4.91. Gross margin pressure remains the key risk.

MDB (MongoDB) reports FQ2 Tuesday post-market. Atlas growth momentum keeps sentiment sanguine despite valuation concerns. Street models total revenue +24.4% to $736MM (Atlas +26% to $553MM), RPOs +88% to $1.49B, operating margins of 21%, and EPS of $1.61.

PANW (Palo Alto Networks) reports FQ4 Tuesday post-market. Security software is in favor as corporations race to defend against AI-powered threats. Consensus expects revenue +32.2% to $3.352B, NGS ARR +59% to $8.86B, RPOs +33% to $20.95B, operating margins of 29.3%, and EPS of 98 cents.

AVGO (Broadcom) reports FQ3 Wednesday post-market. The stock has lagged lately on concerns around its Google TPU role, balance sheet strain from customer financing, and software portfolio softness post-IBM. Street models total revenue +85% to $29.44B, AI chip revenue +206% to $15.9B, operating margins of 66.7%, EPS +91% to $3.23, and free cash flow +108% to $14.6B. After Nvidia's 70% F28 sales guide, Broadcom will face pressure to deliver a similarly bold long-term outlook.

HPE (HP Enterprise) reports FQ3 Wednesday post-market. Bullish setup on AI demand plus an enterprise IT upgrade cycle amplified by the Juniper networking acquisition. Consensus: revenue +30% to $11.878B, Networking +77.5% to $2.935B, and EPS of 93 cents.

SNOW (Snowflake) reports FQ2 Wednesday post-market. The AI consumption thesis is driving strong sentiment — FQ1's blowout in May sent the stock sharply higher and it hasn't looked back. Street expects product sales +30.6% to $1.424B, RPOs +35.7% to $9.363B, net retention of 125.4%, and EPS of 45 cents. Valuation remains the lone overhang.

CIEN (Ciena) reports FQ3 Thursday pre-market. Networking exposure to AI data centers plus solid telecom demand make for a bullish setup. Consensus: revenue +34.5% to $1.64B (Optical Networking +41.6% to $1.154B), gross margins of 44.9%, and EPS +158% to $1.73.

LULU (Lululemon) reports FQ2 Thursday post-market. Sentiment has been cautious — management turmoil, rising competition from Alo and Vuori, and weak reads from DKS, JD Sports, and ONON. That said, the bar is low, the turnaround is underway, and LULU lacks the footwear exposure that appears to be the industry's softest spot. Street models revenue -2.6% to $2.45B, gross margins of 54.4%, operating margins of 11.6%, and EPS of $1.81.

ZS (Zscaler) reports FQ4 Thursday post-market. Despite a favorable macro backdrop for security software (the "Mythos effect" driving corporate IT upgrades), sentiment is mixed given execution concerns and rising competition after FQ3's sharp post-earnings selloff. Consensus: sales +22% to $877.3MM, ARR +24.1% to $3.74B, and EPS +22.6% to $1.09.
