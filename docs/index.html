VK · Key Names
▲ COST Costco Other

Costco reported September comps with US core (ex-FX and gas) up 8%, approximately 80 basis points above consensus and a sharp acceleration from the +5.6% posted in August. This is a high-quality beat driven by underlying consumer demand, not one-time items. The acceleration is a strong positive signal for the consumer spending environment.

◆ LEVI Levi Strauss Earnings Beat

Levi reported FQ3 EPS of 48 cents versus the Street's 36-cent estimate, but the bulk of the outperformance came from tariff refunds — ex those refunds, EPS was roughly 37 cents, essentially inline. Tariff refunds provided a 330 basis point tailwind to operating margins and a 370 basis point tailwind to gross margins. Full-year EPS guidance was raised to $1.54-$1.56 from $1.46-$1.52, and organic revenue growth guidance moves to +6% from +5.5-6%, though DTC and US revenue were soft in the quarter with management noting a recent reacceleration heading into holiday.

◆ LULU Lululemon Restructuring

Lululemon's new CEO announced a refreshed leadership team, naming Maggie Gauger — formerly President and CEO of Athleta — as President and Chief Product Officer, and Joseph Godsey from Walmart Canada as Chief Operating Officer, both newly created roles. Nikki Neuburger and Ted Dagnese are departing the company. Searches are underway for Chief Brand Officer, Chief Communications Officer, and Chief Technology Officer roles, signaling the organization remains in transition.

▲ WOLF Wolfspeed Other

The Department of War's Office of Strategic Capital announced a $1.5 billion conditional loan commitment to Wolfspeed. This is a significant capital infusion from the government that could help stabilize the company's balance sheet and support its silicon carbide semiconductor manufacturing buildout. The conditionality of the loan means execution milestones will matter.
