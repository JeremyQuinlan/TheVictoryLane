MARKET OUTLOOK

The earnings tape this morning is broadly constructive — beats are coming in across consumer and industrials, and tariff refunds are quietly juicing margins in ways the Street didn't fully model. That's a net positive for sentiment, but investors should strip out the one-time refund benefit to get a clean read on underlying business momentum. The structural demand recovery in industrials (per AIT's commentary) looks increasingly durable, which is encouraging for the cyclical trade. TMT is more mixed — Cellebrite's ARR miss is a reminder that enterprise software sales cycles are still grinding. Stay selective.

COMPANY NEWS

DDS (Dillard's) delivered a large FQ2 EPS beat at $6.25 vs. the Street's $4.05, though $1.82 of that came directly from tariff refunds — strip those out and you get $4.43, still above consensus but not nearly as impressive. Same-store sales were roughly inline at +1% vs. the expected +0.85%.

NOMD (Nomad Foods) posted Q2 sales and EBITDA inline with its July 20 preannouncement, reaffirmed prior sales/EBITDA guidance, but trimmed its EPS outlook due to higher interest expense from a recent refinancing — a move management had flagged in advance. The company cited secured price increases, gross margin expansion, and restored momentum with key retail partners.

TPR (Tapestry) beat FQ4 EPS by a few cents ($1.32 vs. $1.27), with gross margins up 180 basis points and operating margins up 250 basis points driving the upside. Coach grew FXN +14% while Kate Spade came in at -7%, better than feared. F27 guidance midpoints (EPS $7.85, revenue $8.45B) came in just below consensus ($7.87/$8.47B), a marginal miss, but the company plans to return $1.7B to shareholders in F27 and is raising its dividend 16%.

WWW (Wolverine World Wide) beat Q2 EPS at 40c vs. the Street's 38c, with revenue of $506.4MM (+6.1%) topping the $500.8MM estimate. Merrell grew +10.3% and Saucony +9%; operating margins expanded 80 basis points to 10% vs. the expected 9.4%. Full-year EPS guidance was raised to $1.60 from ~$1.50.

YETI reported a large Q2 EPS beat at 67c vs. 54c expected, with roughly 8c attributable to tariff refunds. Adjusted gross margins rose 170 basis points (110 from operations, 60 from refunds), and the company bought back 2.8M shares for $130MM. FY EPS guidance was raised to $2.94–$3.00 from $2.83–$2.89.

AIT (Applied Industrial Technologies) posted strong FQ4 upside with EPS of $3.17 and revenue of $1.35B vs. Street estimates of $2.92 and $1.292B respectively, and guided F27 ahead of consensus. Management noted demand recovery appears "durable and increasingly influenced by structural and secular tailwinds," even as they flagged ongoing inflationary headwinds.

ARX (Accelerant) is being taken private by Thoma Bravo at $20.25/share in cash — a roughly 50% premium to Wednesday's close — in a deal valued at over $4B.

CLBT (Cellebrite) disappointed on Q2 ARR, citing longer sales cycles and weaker-than-expected conversion from Inseyets. Full-year ARR growth guidance was trimmed to +14–16% from +18–19%.

DT (Dynatrace) is acquiring AI Observability leader Arize in a cash-and-stock deal valued at $915MM, expected to be approximately 200 basis points accretive to ARR growth and 175 basis points dilutive to non-GAAP operating margin in fiscal 2027.

IBM announced a strategic partnership with OpenAI to help enterprises deploy AI at scale. Worth noting: IBM already has partnerships with Anthropic and a range of other AI firms, so the incremental read-through here is limited despite the headline attention OpenAI partnerships tend to generate.

JD (JD.com) beat Q2 EPS handily at RMB6.29 vs. the Street's RMB5.40, with revenue of RMB346.4B (-2.9% Y/Y) still ahead of the RMB342B estimate. Marketing expenses fell nearly 25% Y/Y and G&A dropped nearly 5%, driving the margin outperformance alongside solid JD Retail profitability and continued loss narrowing at JD Food Delivery.

OPEN (Opendoor) announced a capital structure transaction adding $440MM in growth capital at a 0% coupon while reducing shares outstanding by 5%, with no net share issuance expected until the stock exceeds $10.38.
