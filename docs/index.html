VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO has been pushed from October to November, and year-end ARR projections of greater than 100 to 110 billion dollars came in below the most bullish expectations of 120 billion dollars or more. The company is considering launching a powerful new model before the IPO to recapture momentum lost to OpenAI's Astra, and plans to have 5 gigawatts of compute by year-end doubling to 10 gigawatts by end of 2027. The IPO is still targeting a raise of over 100 billion dollars at a valuation above 2 trillion dollars.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to that facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising concerns about the company's data center financing strategy.

◆ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. A settlement could require Paramount to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central, potentially clearing a major regulatory hurdle for the deal.

◆ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery's pending merger with Paramount moves closer to resolution as Paramount enters advanced settlement talks with California regulators. The potential deal terms include preserving CNN editorial independence and divesting select cable properties, which could accelerate closing of the transaction.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. This raises concerns about franchisee financial health and the broader consumer spending environment for quick service restaurants.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on 10 or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, representing a meaningful commercial recovery opportunity for the troubled program after earlier setbacks. This would be a positive development for Boeing's space and defense segment.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk M&A

Novo Nordisk is facing growing shareholder pressure to diversify away from its concentrated GLP-1 exposure through mergers and acquisitions. Investors are increasingly concerned about long-term concentration risk even as GLP-1 demand remains robust.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture will embed a team of evaluators to monitor safety and alignment at Anthropic, representing a new type of enterprise AI governance contract. While a relatively small item, it signals a growing commercial opportunity around AI oversight services.

▼ MU Micron Sector Reprice

Acer's CEO noted that rising Chinese memory supply, particularly from CXMT which announced its next-generation platform has entered mass production, is helping ease component shortages and placing a lid on memory prices. This is a headwind for pricing power at Western memory suppliers including Micron.

▼ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its store shelves as the grocery chain pushes back against supplier price hikes, signaling growing retailer resistance to elevated consumer goods pricing. This is a negative for Red Bull and illustrative of broader shelf-space risk for premium-priced consumer brands.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by deteriorating AI sentiment and escalating Middle East conflict. Houthis dramatically ramped up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities over the weekend, raising fears of further energy supply disruption. Ukraine also launched a massive drone strike on Russia including Moscow, adding to global risk-off pressure heading into the week.

Global monetary policy remains pointed in a hawkish direction — the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiked this month. The BOJ's most recent hike was slightly more dovish than expected, but paradoxically triggered macro concern by pressuring the yen lower and rekindling fears of Japan selling Treasuries to defend its currency. Kansas City Fed President Schmid made hawkish remarks Friday, warning that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil prices.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Even a small improvement in Middle East geopolitics could trigger an outsized drop in energy prices, with favorable knock-on effects including lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The UN General Assembly running September 22nd through 28th brings together officials from key Middle East countries including Iran, creating a real forum for diplomatic progress. The Trump/Xi summit could also deliver select tariff reductions on roughly 30 billion dollars worth of goods, providing a modest but real risk-on catalyst. Saudi Arabia is already working to increase ship-to-ship oil transfers to compensate for supply shortages, and Pentagon escorts have helped push Hormuz oil shipments to a six-month high.

BEAR CASE

Iran continues to tighten its grip on global energy supplies through Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and an increasingly aggressive Houthi campaign that now appears to be directly targeting Saudi Arabian cities. The AI industry faces a fresh cluster of negative headlines including OpenAI's staggering projected negative free cash flow of 280 billion dollars from 2026 to 2030, rising off-balance sheet AI liability exposure for Big Tech up roughly 300 billion dollars in the last year, and a delayed Anthropic IPO timeline. Global monetary policy remains hawkish across the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC simultaneously, and the latest spike in energy prices has yet to fully work its way through corporate earnings and consumer demand.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautiously bearish into the week open given the net negative weekend news flow — AI sentiment headwinds, geopolitical escalation, and hawkish central banks are the key overhangs. The Trump/Xi summit on Thursday September 24th is the single biggest catalyst to watch, with potential for modest tariff relief on both sides providing an upside surprise.

Sector Watch

AI and Big Tech face a cluster of negative sentiment drivers including rising off-balance sheet liability exposure up roughly 300 billion dollars in the last year, OpenAI's massive projected cash burn of 280 billion dollars in negative free cash flow from 2026 to 2030, and a slightly delayed Anthropic IPO. Memory/semiconductors face a potential pricing headwind as Chinese supplier CXMT enters mass production on its next-generation platform. Energy remains the dominant macro sector story — the Iran war is the primary driver of diesel price spikes and motor oil shortages, though Hormuz throughput has hit a six-month high under Pentagon escort. Auto sector is under pressure with Volkswagen issuing a large downside preannouncement cutting full-year operating margin guidance to up to 1 percent versus prior guidance of 4 to 5.5 percent.
