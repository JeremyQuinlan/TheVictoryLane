Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro

AI stocks surged Monday driven by bullish hype around Meta's personal AI agent, Muse, which has seen a surge in demand since its launch earlier this month. A byproduct of Muse mania is a spike in CPU-linked stocks like AMD, ARM, and INTC, as agentic AI infrastructure demands significantly more CPU versus prior GPU-heavy iterations. However, sentiment is cooling on the broader AI/data center boom, highlighted by SB Energy delaying its IPO after investors balked at its $50B+ desired market cap.

Market Outlook

Mixed to cautious. While AI equities are rallying on Muse enthusiasm, multiple negative AI headlines hit after Friday's close, and SB Energy's IPO delay — the second AI-linked IPO pulled in a week following Holtec — signals growing market skepticism toward the sector. The key catalyst to watch is whether broader AI sentiment can hold up amid this cooling IPO market, and whether Anthropic can still come public by year-end (now targeting November instead of October).

Sector Watch

AI/Data Center: Rally in AI equities on Muse hype is being partially offset by cooling IPO sentiment — two AI-linked IPOs (SB Energy, Holtec) delayed in one week signals investor skepticism on lofty valuations. Semiconductors: CPU-linked chips (AMD, ARM, INTC) outperforming on agentic AI infrastructure demand shift away from GPUs.
