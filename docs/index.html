Macro · Rates

US equity futures are rallying Monday morning with S&P +49 points/65 basis points, Nasdaq +329 points/110 basis points, and Russell 2000 +47 basis points despite a weekend full of negative headlines. Asian and European markets are also broadly higher, with data center pick-and-shovel stocks outperforming in Europe. The primary driver of strength appears to be optimism around the UN General Assembly in New York, where diplomatic progress on the Iran/Middle East conflict is possible — a potential catalyst for lower oil prices.

Treasuries have a bid this morning with yields down 2-3 basis points across the curve. The market is pricing in roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes over the Fed's final two meetings of the year, with about 50/50 odds of a hike at the October 28th meeting, plus an additional 35 basis points of increases priced for 2027. Fed Presidents Schmid and Kashkari both made hawkish remarks recently, warning that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil, and Treasury plans to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt financing.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Nearly all of the market's current macro problems trace back to elevated oil prices, and even a small diplomatic improvement at the UN General Assembly could trigger an outsized decline in energy prices with powerful knock-on effects — lower yields, reduced inflation, better corporate margins, and improved consumer confidence. China and Pakistan are reportedly urging Iran to rein in the Houthis, suggesting growing global pressure for de-escalation. The Trump/Xi summit on Thursday could yield modest tariff reductions on roughly $30 billion in goods, adding another incremental positive for risk sentiment. Market breadth is healthy with gains across Asia, Europe, and US futures despite overwhelmingly negative weekend news flow.

BEAR CASE

The weekend news flow was nearly universally negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest drone strike on Russia, Anthropic's IPO was delayed with underwhelming 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI projects negative free cash flow of roughly $280 billion through 2030, and Oracle faces serious debt issues tied to its New Mexico data center. The geopolitical landscape remains grim with Iran tightening its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, aligned militias, and the Houthis, while oil tanker costs surge and motor oil is being rationed at US retailers. Diplomatic optimism around the UN GA may prove to be wishful thinking, and the US is still expected to hike aggregate tariffs on Chinese imports back to pre-SCOTUS IEEPA levels in coming weeks.

Market Outlook

Adam's read is cautiously bullish for the session, driven almost entirely by diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly and the hope that even a modest de-escalation in the Middle East could trigger a meaningful drop in oil and unleash a cascade of positive macro effects. The key catalyst to watch is any signal from the UN GA meetings — particularly whether Trump meets with Iran's president — and the Trump/Xi summit on Thursday.
