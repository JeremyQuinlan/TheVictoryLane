VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street at $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by 145 basis points of tariff refund tailwind and 105 basis points of non-cash LIFO benefit to gross margins. Domestic comps disappointed at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive comp linearity in the quarter. The stock is rallying as the linearity commentary provides reassurance despite the mixed quality of the beat.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street at 36 cents, and importantly would have beaten even excluding tariff refunds — ex-refunds EPS was 42 cents, still ahead of consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged. The quality of the earnings beat is notable and the stock is an outperformer on the session.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street at 87 cents, with gross margin weakness of 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% more than offsetting slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited a difficult macro landscape. The miss on margins and the cautious macro tone are the key negatives.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect Wednesday night, and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform — a meaningful distribution win. Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform, which is a notable competitive friction point. Investor expectations are building bullishly into the Wednesday event.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases directly on its e-commerce platform, positioning Shopify as a key partner in the emerging agentic commerce ecosystem. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a differentiated edge in AI-driven shopping. The partnership is a modest positive catalyst for the stock.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The rate cuts are sector-wide and weigh on near-term earnings visibility. This is a regulatory-driven headwind with no immediate offset.

▼ LH Lab Corp Other

Lab Corp shares dropped alongside Quest Diagnostics after CMS announced Medicare lab service rate cuts that were larger than expected. The move is a direct hit to reimbursement rates and pressures the earnings outlook for both major lab testing companies. The stock is among the notable underperformers on the session.

▲ BABA Alibaba Product Launch

Alibaba unveiled what it called China's most powerful AI chip and hinted at plans to train a model with up to 10 trillion parameters, while management discussed operating more than 20 gigawatts of data center capacity by 2032. The announcements position Alibaba as a serious competitor in the global AI infrastructure race. This is a significant escalation in China's AI ambitions and a positive catalyst for the stock.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's capabilities in robotic vision at a time when Physical AI demand is accelerating. The strategic fit is strong but the price tag is meaningful.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, including 22% weight loss in the study. This is a meaningful clinical milestone in the highly competitive GLP-1 obesity drug space. The results are a bullish catalyst and reinforce Viking's positioning as a credible competitor to Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly.
