VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant market theme today, with reports of strong early demand for the agentic AI app. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is being heavily anticipated. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing product, which validates the significance of Muse and the agentic consumer AI race.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

Agentic AI applications like Meta Muse are significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, making CPU-linked chipmakers a direct beneficiary of the Muse narrative. AMD is among the top outperformers in the market today on this thesis.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis, as the market reprices CPU exposure higher on the view that mainstream agentic AI adoption would require substantially more processing power. A notable bounce for a stock that has struggled.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with reports confirming an agreement has been reached. The stock is jumping sharply on the news, clearing a major regulatory overhang for the deal.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the merger to proceed. The deal reportedly involves Paramount preserving CNN's editorial independence and potentially divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. issued a cautious outlook, saying it anticipates PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. The stock is sliding and dragging other PC-linked names lower, adding a cautionary data point to the consumer hardware demand picture.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for clearer guidance on the obesity drug franchise trajectory.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article, focusing on poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans linked to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about the economics of Oracle's aggressive infrastructure buildout.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers, many of whom are seeing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals according to the Wall Street Journal. This is a positive data point for Disney's most important earnings driver heading into its next report.

▼ MOS Mosaic Other

Potash stocks are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially less than what is currently paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers including Mosaic, CF Industries, and Nutrien.

Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points. Risk tone is constructive, driven by AI enthusiasm and a sharp drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is building ahead of this week's UN General Assembly, where Trump could potentially meet with Iran's president, raising hopes for Middle East de-escalation.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1-3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the softer geopolitical risk premium as oil drops sharply. No major Fed commentary in today's news flow. Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the roughly 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse demand signals are strong and OpenAI is reportedly rushing to build a competing agentic consumer product, validating the thesis that agentic AI is the next major adoption wave. CPU-linked stocks like AMD, ARM, and Intel are catching a major bid on the view that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior iterations. Oil dropping roughly 400 basis points removes an inflationary headwind and boosts consumer sentiment and travel/leisure stocks. Treasuries are catching a modest bid, keeping rates supportive for growth and rate-sensitive sectors.

BEAR CASE

Breadth is narrow — the equal-weight S&P is barely up 30 basis points while the Nasdaq carries the headline number, suggesting this is a thin, momentum-driven move rather than broad participation. The US-China trade truce extension was not reached over the weekend, with the November 10 expiration looming as a potential landmine. Middle East tensions remain genuinely dangerous — the Houthi escalation against Saudi Arabia is intensifying, Iran is issuing warnings, and a diplomatic breakthrough is far from certain. Big Tech AI off-balance sheet liability exposure has surged roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, a growing structural risk that markets are largely ignoring.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean today, driven by two dominant themes: the AI agentic breakout narrative centered on Meta Muse and the sharp retreat in oil prices on Houthi/Middle East de-escalation hopes. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night — the market is already front-running it aggressively. The narrow breadth (equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points vs Nasdaq +153) is worth watching as a caution flag.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU names on the Meta Muse agentic narrative. Energy is underperforming sharply as Brent crude drops roughly 400 basis points on Middle East de-escalation hopes — energy names like COP, EOG, OXY, and XOM are all lower. Potash and materials are under pressure after Trump's Belarus supply deal comments. Travel and leisure names are catching a bid alongside the oil drop. REITs and data center industrials are also outperforming. Transports and payments are lagging.
