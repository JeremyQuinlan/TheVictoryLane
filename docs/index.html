◆ PEP Pepsi Earnings Beat

Pepsi reported Q3 EPS of $2.34, beating the Street by roughly 2%, with organic sales growth of 3.1% — the highest since Q4 2023 — topping estimates of 2.65%. However, the full-year core EPS growth outlook was cut to approximately 3% at the midpoint from roughly 5% due to North America margin pressure, with core operating margins contracting 35 basis points year over year. International organic revenue growth accelerated to 8%, a bright spot, while North America beverages underperformed.
