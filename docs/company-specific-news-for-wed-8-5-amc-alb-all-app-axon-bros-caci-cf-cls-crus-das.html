MARKET OUTLOOK

The August 5 AMC earnings wave is a mixed bag — plenty of beat-and-raise stories, but the market was clearly positioned for more from names like APP and SNDK, and guidance cuts at HONA and HUBS sting. Consumer-facing names (BROS, ELF, DASH, EXPE) are holding up better than feared, which is a modest positive for the soft-landing narrative. The TDUP guide cut — explicitly blaming "price-sensitive buyers" — is worth watching as an early warning on low-end consumer stress.

COMPANY NEWS

BROS (Dutch Bros) beat on EBITDA ($113.7MM vs. Street $105MM) and revenue ($551MM vs. Street $525MM), with comps of +5.8% topping the +5.4% estimate. Full-year guidance is raised — comps now seen at +5-6% vs. prior +4-6%, EBITDA $385-390MM vs. prior $370-380MM.

ELF (e.l.f. Beauty) posted a blowout Q1, with sales of $479.4MM crushing the Street's $431MM. Full-year EPS guide moves higher, though only by ~$0.20 despite a ~$1.00 EPS beat in the quarter — a muted raise that may disappoint.

TDUP (ThredUp) trimmed full-year guidance, now seeing sales +11% vs. prior +14% and EBITDA margins of 4.7% vs. prior 6.1%, citing "ongoing economic uncertainty affecting the most price-sensitive buyers."

ALL (Allstate) delivered a blowout Q2 — EPS $8.99 vs. Street $5.93 — on a combined ratio roughly 600 basis points better than expected at the headline level and ~200 basis points better on the core. Premiums Written were light (+2.6% overall, just +0.4% in auto).

MET (MetLife) posted adjusted EPS of $2.43 vs. Street $2.30, driven by strong underwriting and broad volume growth.

MCK (McKesson) beat FQ1 EPS at $9.93 vs. Street $9.53 and raised full-year EPS guidance to $44.20-45.00 from $43.80-44.60, citing strength in core distribution, oncology, and biopharma services.

ALB (Albemarle) beat Q2 EBITDA at $858MM vs. Street $779MM and raised Specialties guidance on stronger pricing and volume, while trimming capex on efficiency gains.

CF (CF Industries) missed Q2 EPS at $4.73 vs. Street $5.58.

HONA (Honeywell Aerospace) cut full-year guidance in its first standalone earnings release — organic growth now +4-5% vs. prior +7-9%, adjusted EBIT $4.35-4.45B vs. prior $4.65-4.75B. Particularly damaging given bullish prints across the rest of aerospace.

NTR (Nutrien) missed Q2 EPS at $2.61 vs. Street $2.77 but nudged potash volume guidance slightly higher.

APP (AppLovin) posted a modest Q2 revenue miss ($1.924B vs. Street $1.94B, +53% Y/Y) and a light Q3 guide midpoint ($2.07B vs. Street $2.072B). Not bad in absolute terms, but the market expected much more.

AXON beat Q2 EBITDA at $242MM vs. Street $220MM, revenue +35% to $904MM vs. Street $875MM, future contracted bookings up 41% Y/Y to $15.1B, and raised full-year sales growth guidance to +32-34% from +30-32%.

CACI (CACI International) beat FQ4 EPS at $8.91 vs. Street $7.23 and guided FY27 EPS of $32.96-33.86 vs. Street $30.96.

CLS (Celestica) announced a $3B equity offering to fund AI compute and data center networking growth investments.

DASH (DoorDash) beat Q2 EBITDA at $914MM vs. Street $843MM on revenue of $4.454B vs. Street $4.34B. Q3 guidance is roughly inline; management guided H2 margins to follow a similar seasonal pattern to H2:25.

DUOL (Duolingo) beat on sales, EBITDA, bookings, and DAUs, raising full-year EBITDA guidance. Paid subs of 12.7MM were a touch light vs. Street 12.79MM.

EBAY beat Q2 across EPS, sales, and GMV, with full-year guidance moving higher on advertising momentum and marketplace resilience.

ETSY beat Q2 on GMS, revenue, take rate, and EBITDA margins, announced a new $2B buyback, and raised full-year GMS and EBITDA margin guidance. Also announced a 12% workforce reduction.

EXPE (Expedia) beat on EPS, sales, and bookings, raising full-year bookings growth to +8-9% from +6-8% and EBITDA margin expansion guidance to +150-175 basis points from +100-125 basis points.

FIG (Figma) beat across Q2 sales, earnings, op. margins, and net retention, raising full-year revenue guidance to +39%. Left full-year op. margin guide unchanged at 9% despite posting 10% in Q2 — a mild disappointment.

HUBS (HubSpot) trimmed full-year FXN sales growth to +16% from +17% and guided Q3 revenue at $924-925MM vs. Street $943MM.

MELI (Mercado Libre) posted revenue +50% Y/Y — fastest pace in four years — with GMV and TPV also beating. Op. margins contracted 550 basis points to 6.7% vs. Street 6.9% on elevated investment, though absolute op. income topped estimates.

MSI (Motorola Solutions) beat Q2 EPS/revenue at $4.41/$3.133B vs. Street $3.85/$3B and raised full-year EPS to $17.62-17.72 from $16.87-16.99. Backlog ticked down sequentially and Q3 EPS guidance was merely inline.

PAYC (Paycom) beat Q2 and raised full-year guidance, citing automation strategy execution.

SNDK (Sandisk) delivered a blowout FQ4 EPS of $39.25 vs. Street $34.37 and added $14B to its buyback, but FQ1 sales guidance midpoint of $10.3-10.8B missed the Street's $11.1B and EPS guide of ~$45 fell short of Street $45.57.
