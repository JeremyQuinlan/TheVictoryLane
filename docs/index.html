VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — tariff refunds added 145 basis points to gross margins and a non-cash LIFO impact added another 105 basis points. Domestic comps grew only 1.6% versus expectations of 3.1%, and the operating expense ratio worsened by 100 basis points year-over-year. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final eight weeks and expressed confidence heading into fiscal 2027.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. RealSense is expected to generate $80 to $90 million in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth. The deal positions Cognex deeper into the high-growth robotics and Physical AI supply chain.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat holds up even excluding an 11-cent tariff refund tailwind — adjusted EPS would have been 42 cents, still ahead of consensus. Sales declined 3.3% organically to $923.4 million, missing the $943.2 million estimate, though orders rose 3.5% year-over-year. Fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed modestly while EPS guidance was maintained, and fiscal Q2 EPS guidance is in line with consensus.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Pentair named Bob Hau as its new CFO, effective November 1st, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman staying on to ensure a smooth transition. Hau brings prior CFO experience at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International. The leadership change is a modest operational update with no financial guidance revision attached.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margins fell 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% — well below the 13.85% expected — despite revenue coming in slightly above estimates at $2.311 billion. Management cited stubborn interest rates, elevated fuel costs, inflation, and rising material costs as meaningful headwinds throughout the selling season. Bright spots included healthy dealer inventory levels, structural cost reduction actions underway, and a roughly 75% surge in North American towable RV order backlog year-over-year.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics shares surged in pre-market trading after reporting positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in trial participants. The result is a meaningful data point in the competitive obesity drug space and validates VK2735 as a credible contender. This is a significant positive catalyst that could re-rate the stock and lift sentiment across the GLP-1 sector.
