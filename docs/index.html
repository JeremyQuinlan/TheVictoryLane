VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone beat FQ4 EPS by roughly two dollars ($56.05 vs. $54.03 estimate), but quality was poor — the upside was driven by tariff refunds adding 145 basis points to gross margins and a non-cash LIFO benefit adding 105 basis points. Domestic comps missed badly at +1.6% versus the +3.1% expected, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100 basis points year-over-year. Management noted improving sales linearity in the final eight weeks and expressed confidence heading into fiscal 2027.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced an acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional ~$106.5 million. RealSense is expected to generate $80–90 million in revenue, representing more than 50% year-over-year growth. The deal positions Cognex in the fast-growing physical AI and robotics perception space.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll delivered solid FQ1 EPS upside at 53 cents versus the 36-cent estimate, and importantly the beat holds even excluding an 11-cent tariff refund tailwind — core EPS of 42 cents still topped consensus. Sales missed, declining 3.3% organically to $923.4 million versus the $943.2 million expected, though orders rose 3.5% year-over-year. Fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed slightly but EPS guidance was left unchanged, and FQ2 EPS guidance midpoint is in line with consensus.

◆ PNR Pentair Other

Pentair named Bob Hau as its new CFO effective November 1, with current interim CFO Bob Fishman staying on through the transition. Hau brings prior CFO experience at Fiserv, TE Connectivity, and Lennox International. This is a leadership transition event with no financial results attached.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR missed FQ4 EPS at 78 cents versus the 87-cent estimate as gross margins collapsed 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4%, far below the 13.85% expected, with management citing stubborn interest rates, elevated fuel costs, and inflation squeezing household budgets. Revenue of $2.311 billion was slightly above the $2.17 billion estimate but still down 8.4% year-over-year. Some bright spots emerged: dealer inventory is healthy, structural cost actions are underway, and the North American towable RV order backlog surged roughly 75% year-over-year, though the company declined to provide formal fiscal 2027 guidance.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking shares spiked in pre-market trading after reporting positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in participants. The result is a meaningful positive data point in the competitive GLP-1 obesity drug landscape. This reinforces Viking as a credible challenger in the weight-loss drug market.
