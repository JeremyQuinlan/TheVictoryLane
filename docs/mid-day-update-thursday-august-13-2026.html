RATES & FED

Treasuries are catching a strong bid with yields down 6–7 basis points across the curve, driven by the one-two punch of a softer PPI and falling oil prices. Fed expectations have shifted dovish, with markets now pricing just 24 basis points of hikes remaining this year. Worth noting: the core PPI wasn't as clean as the headline — services ran hot — so don't get too comfortable with the dovish narrative.

---

MARKET OUTLOOK

The bull case is intact but nuanced: two consecutive cooler inflation prints, blowout AI infrastructure numbers from Lenovo and a potential $2T+ Anthropic IPO (FT), and falling oil are all tailwinds. That said, the market is ruthlessly selling individual tech earnings beats — CBRS, COHR, and CSCO are all in the red despite topping estimates, which tells you expectations were priced to perfection. The real tell this week is whether the Treasury rally holds as services inflation stays sticky. Retail sales Friday and Michigan sentiment will set the tone heading into next week's FOMC minutes.

GEOPOLITICAL

- Japan's government is said to support a near-term BOJ rate hike amid concerns over continued yen weakness, even as Japan's July PPI came in slightly cooler at +7.2% versus +7.4% expected — still very hot in absolute terms (Bloomberg).

- Mexico is pushing Washington to lower tariffs on North American autos as bilateral trade negotiations progress, a move that could reduce vehicle retail prices (WSJ).

- Russia has ramped up attacks on Ukrainian ports and Black Sea shipping, threatening global food supplies and trapping Ukrainian harvests (Reuters, NYT).

- Energy Secretary Wright's remarks suggest significantly more oil is escaping the Middle East region than the market had assumed — that, not an Iran deal, appears to be the proximate cause of Brent's slide (Bloomberg).

- South Korea is mandating financial literacy courses for new investors before they can trade single-stock funds (FT).

COMPANY NEWS

CSCO reported FQ4 EPS of $1.22 vs. the Street's $1.17 on sales of +18% to $17.3B, with operating margins an impressive 35.9%. F27 guidance is bullish at $5.08 EPS vs. $4.84 consensus and $72.8B revenue vs. $69.1B — but FQ1 gross margin guidance disappointed, hence the stock's red tape despite the beat.

COHR posted FQ4 EPS of $1.74 vs. $1.62 expected on sales of +33.8% to $2.046B, with Datacenter & Communications leading the top-line beat. FQ1 guide of $1.95 EPS/$2.3B revenue is well ahead of consensus; shares are down regardless — sell the news in full effect.

CBRS grew core revenue +103% to $209.9MM vs. $194MM expected with gross margins of 41% vs. 36.7% expected, and raised full-year guidance. However, RPOs barely moved quarter-over-quarter, expectations were sky-high, and Cerebras remains a small challenger against AI chip giants.

TPR delivered FQ4 EPS of $1.32 vs. $1.27 with gross margins +180 basis points and operating margins +250 basis points, but F27 guidance midpoints at $7.85 EPS/$8.45B revenue came in just under consensus of $7.87/$8.47B — the stock is tumbling.

DDS beat big on FQ2 EPS ($6.25 vs. $4.05), though $1.82 of that came from tariff refunds; ex-refunds, EPS was $4.43, still a beat. Same-store sales came in at +1% vs. +0.85% expected.

YETI crushed Q2 EPS at 67c vs. 54c expected (~8c from tariff refunds), with adjusted gross margins up 170 basis points. The company repurchased 2.8M shares for $130MM and raised full-year EPS guidance.

AIT (Applied Industrial Technologies) beat FQ4 EPS/revenue at $3.17/$1.35B vs. $2.92/$1.292B and raised intermediate financial targets.

JD posted Q2 EPS of RMB6.29 vs. RMB5.40, with marketing expenses down nearly 25% year-over-year driving the margin outperformance; revenue came in at RMB346.4B vs. RMB342B expected.

DT (Dynatrace) is acquiring AI observability leader Arize in a cash-and-stock deal valued at $915MM, expected to be ~200 basis points accretive to ARR growth but ~175 basis points dilutive to non-GAAP operating margin in fiscal 2027.

ARX (Accelerant) will be taken private by Thoma Bravo at $20.25/share cash — roughly a 50% premium to Wednesday's close — in a deal valued at over $4B.

SNDK laid out a long-range plan targeting 15–19% annual revenue growth from F28–F30 with gross margins around 80% and operating margins near 75%, largely below current Street models.

IBM announced a strategic partnership with OpenAI to help enterprises deploy AI at scale across core business operations.

NFLX shares are climbing after Bill Ackman's Pershing Square disclosed a stake.

Anthropic investors believe the company could achieve a $2T+ valuation at its planned October IPO, with ARR potentially hitting $100–120B by year-end — more than 10x growth over 2026 (FT). Separately, Anthropic is in talks to acquire world-model startup Decart AI for ~$6B (Bloomberg), and DeepSeek announced significant price increases on its flagship V4 models (Bloomberg).

Lenovo reported blowout FQ1 results with sales of $36.9B vs. $22.4B expected (+43%), led by ISG revenue of $8.5B vs. $6.1B expected (+98%); gross margins jumped 180 basis points year-over-year to 16.5% (Lenovo investor release).
