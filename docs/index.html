Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The UN General Assembly running September 22-28 in New York brings together leaders from key Middle East nations including Iran, creating a genuine window for diplomatic progress. Trump signaled openness to meeting with Iran's president and reportedly called off Houthi air strikes at the last minute, suggesting back-channel diplomacy is alive. If oil prices were to sustainably decline, the knock-on effects would be powerful: lower yields, reduced inflation, margin relief, and improved consumer confidence. China and Pakistan are reportedly pressuring Iran to rein in the Houthis, indicating growing global alarm that could accelerate a deal.

BEAR CASE

Nearly all weekend news flow was net negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest-ever drone strike on Russia, and Iran warned of fierce retaliation if the US resumes military action. On the AI front, headlines included a delayed Anthropic IPO, disappointing Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, massive OpenAI cash burn of roughly $280 billion in negative free cash flow through 2030, and Oracle data center debt issues. The US-China trade truce extension was not reached and Washington is expected to raise aggregate tariff rates on Chinese imports back toward pre-SCOTUS IEEPA levels in coming weeks. Monetary policy globally remains hawkish with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month.

Market Outlook

Adam leans cautiously bullish on a tactical basis for Monday, driven entirely by UN General Assembly diplomatic optimism and the possibility of Middle East de-escalation. The key catalyst to watch is any signal of diplomatic progress between the US and Iran at the UN General Assembly this week, as even a modest improvement in geopolitical tensions could trigger a sharp drop in oil and a cascade of favorable macro effects across equities, yields, and consumer confidence.
