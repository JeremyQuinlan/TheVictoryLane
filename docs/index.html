VK · Key Names
◆ AKAM Akamai Partnership

Akamai announced an expanded AI compute infrastructure deal with Anthropic worth $11.6 billion in contractual commitments over seven years, with the potential to grow by an additional $9 billion for a total deal value of up to $20 billion. Akamai will issue Anthropic warrants for up to 5% of shares outstanding at an exercise price of $111.33. Capex associated with the base commitment is estimated at roughly $5.5 billion — nearly half the committed revenue amount — with no impact expected on 2026 revenue guidance but a significant spike in capex ahead.

◆ COST Costco Earnings Beat

Costco reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $6.75, though $0.15 came from tariff refunds, making the clean beat modest at $6.60 versus the Street at $6.53. Membership fee revenue came in at $1.85 billion, slightly ahead of the $1.832 billion consensus, and renewal rates ticked up 10 basis points sequentially both globally and in the US and Canada. Headline comps of plus 9.4% were strong, though ex-gas and FX the figure was plus 6.7%, and merchandise gross margins were essentially in line with expectations.

▲ LEA Lear Buyback

Lear's board boosted its share buyback authorization from $600 million to $1.5 billion, representing approximately 26% of the company's current market capitalization. Management cited confidence in the company's outlook and its ability to generate free cash flow as the rationale for the expanded program.

◆ TMUS T-Mobile Other

T-Mobile raised its dividend by 15% to $1.17 per share, bringing its yield to 2.83%. That yield remains well below AT&T at 4.36% and Verizon at 5.98%, and also trails the 10-year Treasury at 5.21%, limiting the income appeal of the hike in the current rate environment.
