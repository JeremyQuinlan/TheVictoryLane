Macro · Rates

This newsletter is focused entirely on upcoming tech earnings rather than broad market context. No index moves, risk tone, or overnight action are discussed. The publication date is October 17, 2026, ahead of a heavy tech earnings week.

Market Outlook

No explicit directional market call is made. The tone is cautiously constructive on big tech, with sentiment extremely bullish on Meta and moderately bullish on SpaceX heading into their respective reports. The key catalysts this week are SpaceX Tuesday after close and Meta Wednesday after close.

Sector Watch

Technology and AI infrastructure are the dominant themes this week. The neocloud and data center buildout narrative remains front and center, with SpaceX emerging as an unexpected player alongside traditional hyperscalers. AI monetization — specifically around large language model products and compute contracts — is the key debate heading into earnings. Telecom incumbents face a watch-and-wait dynamic around Starlink Mobile, though near-term disruption risk appears limited.

VK · Key Names
▲ SPCX SpaceX Earnings Beat

SpaceX reports FQ3 earnings Tuesday after the close. Street is modeling revenue of $12.88B, EBITDA of ~$6.6B, capex of ~$19.6B, and free cash flow of -$11.6B. Key focus areas beyond the Q3 numbers include compute capacity goals (2GW by end of 2026, 10GW by end of 2027), a $100B ARR target by year-end, new compute contract pipeline beyond Google and Anthropic, Starlink Mobile competitive positioning, and ongoing Tesla merger speculation.

◆ META Meta Earnings Beat

Meta reports FQ3 earnings Wednesday after the close with sentiment extremely bullish on the strength of its core business and the Muse AI product family. Street is modeling revenue of $63.3B (+23.5% YoY), DAP of 3.66B (+3.5%), and capex of $41B (+117%), which pushes free cash flow to -$9.2B for the quarter. A $10B charge from a state AG settlement will skew headline EPS, and there is speculation the company may revisit plans to sell a large stock offering to shore up its balance sheet.

▲ GOOGL Alphabet Partnership

Alphabet is mentioned as an existing compute contract partner with SpaceX. The relationship underscores the demand for SpaceX's neocloud data center buildout, though no standalone Alphabet earnings commentary is provided.

▲ T AT&T Other

AT&T is cited as a potential competitive threat target from SpaceX's Starlink Mobile initiative. However, skepticism remains high that Starlink Mobile has sufficient spectrum or financial capacity to mount a serious nationwide challenge.

▲ TMUS T-Mobile Other

T-Mobile is mentioned alongside AT&T and Verizon as an incumbent carrier that Starlink Mobile could theoretically disrupt. Analyst skepticism over SpaceX's spectrum and capex capacity limits the near-term threat.

▲ VZ Verizon Other

Verizon is referenced as one of the three major carriers that Starlink Mobile is hyped to compete against. Near-term disruption risk is seen as limited given SpaceX's current spectrum and funding constraints.

◆ TSLA Tesla M&A

Tesla is mentioned in the context of longstanding speculation about a potential merger or combination with SpaceX. Speculation has intensified following the recent Roadster unveil, which incorporated SpaceX technology. Investors may press management on this topic during SpaceX's earnings call.
