No specific Fed or Treasury yield levels are mentioned in this note. The rate environment remains a headwind for rate-sensitive sectors.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously constructive at the company level — Accenture is the standout bull catalyst and a potential read-through for IT services and AI adoption. However, the unchanged guidance from McCormick and ongoing rate pressure on housing temper enthusiasm. The key catalyst to watch today is whether Accenture's blowout bookings lift the broader IT services and consulting complex.

Sector Watch

IT services and consulting could see a lift from Accenture's blowout quarter, particularly the AI-driven managed services bookings. Housing and mortgage-sensitive names face continued pressure as rising rates have broadly frozen buyer activity. VK · Key Names
▲ ACN Accenture Earnings Beat

Accenture beat FQ4 EPS with $3.29 versus the Street's $3.20, driven by 7% constant-currency revenue growth against a 3% forecast and bookings of $22.2 billion well above the $20 billion estimate, producing a book-to-bill of 1.2 times. Managed services bookings were especially strong at a 1.4 times book-to-bill. F27 guidance calls for 4.5% constant-currency revenue growth at the midpoint, above the Street's 3.8%, with EPS of $14.60 roughly inline; the modest EPS miss versus consensus is tax-rate driven, not operational.

▲ AYI Acuity Brands Earnings Beat

Acuity reported FQ4 EPS of $5.77, up 11% and above the Street's $5.56 estimate, with the beat driven by margin expansion — gross margins jumped 130 basis points and operating margins rose 10 basis points, both excluding tariff refunds. Revenue came in at $1.244 billion, right inline with expectations, with the ABL segment slightly ahead and AIS slightly below.

▲ CAH Cardinal Health Contract Win

Cardinal Health announced an extension of its distribution agreement with CVS Health through June 30, 2032, providing long-term revenue visibility. In connection with the renewal, the company reaffirmed its fiscal year 2027 non-GAAP EPS growth guidance of 13% to 15%, implying $12.40 to $12.60, and maintained its long-term EPS growth target of 12% to 14%. The deal removes a key contract renewal overhang.

◆ MKC McCormick Earnings Beat

McCormick beat FQ3 EPS with 86 cents versus the Street's 76 cents, driven by organic revenue growth of 1.9% — roughly 25 basis points above plan — and solid margin expansion with adjusted gross margins up 180 basis points and operating margins up 70 basis points. Despite the strong quarter, the company left its full F26 guidance unchanged, and Adam notes that general investor negativity toward food staples will likely prevent a sustained rally.
