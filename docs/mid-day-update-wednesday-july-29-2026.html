RATES & FED

Treasuries are under selling pressure with yields up 3–4 basis points across the curve. Markets are pricing approximately 44 basis points of hikes for the year, with odds of a hike today sitting around 33%. The base case expectation is no action today, a few hawkish dissents, and another vague Powell presser — which would be the worst of all worlds relative to just hiking and giving the market clarity.

---

MARKET OUTLOOK

Three forces are hitting simultaneously — Middle East escalation, Fed anxiety, and a mixed earnings tape — and the combination is doing real damage to cap-weighted indices while equal-weight holds up better, which tells you the pain is concentrated in the high-multiple, high-momentum tech and AI names. Forced selling by overleveraged funds is amplifying the moves; banks are reportedly demanding more collateral on heavy-tech portfolios (FT). Until the Fed resolves its communication problem and the Middle East stabilizes, expect continued rotation out of mega-cap tech into defensives, energy, and select financials. The earnings season itself is actually reasonably healthy underneath the noise — outperformers are genuinely beating — but the bar for tech is brutally high and "modest upside" is being punished. Energy is the clearest tactical long here given Brent's move.
