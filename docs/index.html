VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the central catalyst of the day, with strong demand indicators for the agentic AI app driving broad AI sector outperformance. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is a major near-term event risk. OpenAI is reportedly considering developing a competing product, validating Muse's market impact.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is outperforming as agentic AI — the type of AI Muse could help bring mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, making CPU-linked stocks direct beneficiaries. The Muse-driven narrative is acting as a re-rating catalyst for the CPU space broadly. AMD is among the top outperformers in tech today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis — more compute-heavy AI workloads are broadly bullish for CPU makers. This is a sector-level re-rating rather than a company-specific catalyst, but Intel is explicitly named as a beneficiary. Outperforming in tech today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. issued a cautious outlook on PC unit volumes, saying it anticipates a roughly 5 percent decline in calendar year 2027. This is a headwind for the broader PC supply chain and is weighing on the stock today. Acer's CEO separately noted Chinese memory supply is easing component shortages and capping prices, adding further margin pressure context.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general that clears a major legal obstacle threatening to block the Warner Bros. merger. The stock is jumping sharply on the news. Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central as part of the deal.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the merger to proceed. This removes a significant regulatory overhang that had weighed on both stocks. Both names are among the top performers in media today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting. Investors were disappointed by the lack of specificity despite the company touting future blockbuster drug launches. The vague guidance is being read as a negative signal on near-term visibility.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article on Friday focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico called Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about execution on its hyperscale data center ambitions. This adds a credit-market warning signal to Oracle's AI infrastructure story.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are facing traffic headwinds, according to a WSJ report, thanks to price promotions and package deals. This is a positive data point for Disney's highest-margin segment at a time when consumer discretionary spending is under scrutiny. No major financial update — this is a third-party press read.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below current Canadian potash rates. This is a direct price and volume threat to North American potash producers. The move is hitting the entire fertilizer complex hard today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is generating strong demand signals and pulling the entire AI complex higher, with agentic AI's CPU intensity providing a secondary tailwind for AMD, ARM, and Intel. Diplomatic de-escalation in the Middle East — Trump backing off Houthi strikes and the prospect of a Trump-Iran meeting at the UN — is helping push oil lower, which is a broad macro positive for risk assets. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes an overhang in media, and the China-US AI dialogue, while incomplete, signals continued engagement rather than confrontation.

BEAR CASE

The equal-weight S&P is barely up 30 basis points, revealing that the rally is narrow and concentrated in mega-cap tech — breadth is weak beneath the surface. The US-China trade truce extension was not finalized, and it expires November 10, leaving a live tariff risk overhang. Geopolitical risk in the Middle East remains structurally elevated — Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia escalated sharply over the weekend, Tehran issued warnings about US military action, and oil tanker costs are surging, all of which could reprice energy and inflation expectations quickly.

Adam's tone is cautiously bullish for today's session, with AI and agentic computing as the dominant catalyst.
