Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro

US equity futures are nearly flat Wednesday morning — S&P +0.05%, Dow +0.02%, Nasdaq -0.04%, and Russell 2000 -0.27%. Asian markets were mixed, with Hong Kong down roughly 1% dragged by Alibaba (-4.4%) and Tencent (-2.4%), while Korea and Taiwan outperformed. Treasuries are flat across the curve, the market is pricing ~33 basis points of hikes this year and ~45 basis points in 2027, Brent is flat near $99.50, gold is down 1.0%, silver down 2.5%, and bitcoin off 0.4%.

Market Outlook

Adam's focus today is squarely on two catalysts: progress in US-Iran nuclear negotiations (which could meaningfully move oil and broaden market risk sentiment) and Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote at 7pm ET (where investors expect bullish commentary on Muse adoption and the forthcoming Watermelon frontier model). The tape is cautiously constructive but directionless ahead of these events, with pick-and-shovels/semis buoyed by AI enthusiasm while financials, software, and select consumer names face pressure from Muse disruption fears. Key earnings before the open — CTAS, GIS, PAYX — plus the MCD analyst meeting and US flash PMIs at 9:45am ET round out the morning agenda.

Sector Watch

Financials came under pressure Tuesday on fears that Meta's Muse AI agent could disrupt sub-industries like retail brokers and wealth managers. Refinery stocks sold off after Trump signaled support for a diesel export ban. Software/services names including ADBE, BKNG, and INTU face renewed disruption fears from the success of Muse and anticipation of further AI agents launching in coming weeks. Semis and AI pick-and-shovels have been buoyed by Muse enthusiasm over the last several sessions. Homebuilders are cautious — KBH macro commentary cited weakening conditions, higher mortgage rates, and geopolitical uncertainty weighing on buyer confidence.
