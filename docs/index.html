▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are experiencing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals. ▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Guidance Cut

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for clearer guidance. The lack of specificity at the capital markets event is being read as a negative signal. Shares are underperforming in Europe this morning.

▼ ORCL Oracle Massive Capex

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article on Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the 'Project Jupiter' facility having 'hit a wall.' This adds to a broader weekend narrative of off-balance-sheet AI infrastructure liabilities becoming a growing risk for big tech. The debt market stress around this project is a bearish datapoint for Oracle's aggressive data center expansion strategy.

This is a new name to watch as it moves toward its IPO.

Macro · Rates

US equity futures are rallying Monday morning with S&P up 49 points or 65 basis points, Nasdaq up 329 points or 110 basis points, and Russell 2000 up 47 basis points. Asian markets closed broadly higher led by Korea KOSPI up 1.65% and Hang Seng up 1.18%, while European indices are up 85-120 basis points with data center pick-and-shovel stocks outperforming. The rally is somewhat surprising given a weekend full of negative headlines — escalating Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine's largest drone strike on Russia, and a string of troubling AI industry news — with diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly appearing to be the primary driver.

Treasuries have a bid this morning with yields down 2-3 basis points across the curve. Fed expectations are holding fairly steady with the market pricing in roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes over the final two meetings of the year, with about 50/50 odds of a hike at the October 28th meeting, and an additional 35 basis points of increases expected in 2027. Fed officials remain hawkish — Kansas City Fed President Schmid warned Friday that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil, and Minneapolis Fed President Kashkari echoed those sentiments Sunday, saying inflation is reaching all corners of the economy.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The UN General Assembly running September 22-28 in New York brings together officials from Iran and multiple Middle Eastern countries, creating a rare opening for diplomatic progress. Even a small improvement in Middle East tensions could trigger an outsized drop in oil prices, which would cascade positively into lower yields, reduced inflation, better corporate margins, and improved consumer confidence. Trump reportedly called off air strikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday and said he would be open to meeting Iran's president, signaling a potential de-escalation. The Trump/Xi summit on Thursday could also deliver modest tariff reductions and a trade truce extension, providing an additional boost to sentiment.

BEAR CASE

Nearly all weekend news flow was net negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest drone strike on Russia, and a wave of troubling AI headlines hit after Friday's close including a delayed Anthropic IPO, disappointing Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI's massive cash burn, and Oracle data center debt issues. Geopolitical risks remain deeply entrenched with Iran tightening its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and the Houthis, while tanker shortages and surging motor oil prices signal real economic pain filtering through the system. The US-China trade truce extension was not reached over the weekend, and Washington is expected to hike aggregate tariff rates on Chinese imports back to pre-SCOTUS levels in coming weeks, maintaining a ceiling on any sustained risk rally.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautiously mixed with a short-term bullish tilt driven purely by UN General Assembly diplomatic optimism and the possibility of a Middle East de-escalation, which would be the single biggest macro catalyst for lower oil, lower yields, and improved risk sentiment. The key catalyst to watch today and this week is any signal of diplomatic progress between the US and Iran at the UN General Assembly, as well as the Thursday Trump/Xi summit for potential tariff relief.

Sector Watch

AI infrastructure and data center names face headwinds from a cluster of negative weekend headlines — delayed Anthropic IPO, disappointing Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of roughly 280 billion dollars from 2026 to 2030, and Oracle data center debt stress — yet European data center pick-and-shovel stocks are outperforming this morning, suggesting the market is selectively discounting the bad news. Energy sector faces a complex setup with Brent crude sliding 170 basis points to around 102.10 dollars amid UN diplomatic optimism, but structural supply constraints from tanker shortages, Hormuz tensions, and surging motor oil prices remain firmly in place. Chinese memory producer CXMT announced its next-generation platform has entered mass production, which Acer's CEO noted is helping ease component shortages and placing a lid on prices — a modest negative read for memory pricing at Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.
