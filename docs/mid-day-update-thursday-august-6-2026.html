RATES & FED

Treasuries are under selling pressure with yields up 2–4 basis points across the curve. Two separate articles (WSJ, FT) are stoking fresh anxiety about Fed independence, reporting that Trump has been calling Fed Chair Warsh repeatedly since his appointment — a clear break from the White House–central bank separation norm that's held for decades. Markets are absorbing it for now, but it's a slow-burn risk that isn't going away.

MARKET OUTLOOK

The tape is holding up surprisingly well given the combination of tech earnings disappointments, rising yields, and Fed independence noise — credit solid non-tech earnings and decent macro data (strong Q2 productivity, subdued jobless claims) for keeping the bulls in the game. The software complex is getting genuinely hit hard (DDOG, HUBS, FIG all disappoint), and that's worth watching as a leading indicator for enterprise spending. Oil's spike on Iran-Oman Hormuz deal uncertainty adds another layer of complexity heading into Friday's jobs report. The path of least resistance feels sideways-to-cautious until we get clarity on July payrolls and next week's CPI.
