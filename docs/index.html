Tuesday, September 22, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 22, 2026 · 05:26 AM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta's personal AI agent Muse has seen a surge in demand since launching earlier this month and is viewed as the product that could bring agentic AI to the mainstream consumer market. Meta was among the best-performing stocks in the entire market on Monday. Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote kicks off Wednesday night at 7pm ET, where investors expect additional bullish Muse and AI product announcements.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD rallied Monday on the Muse-driven CPU optimism, as agentic AI is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. The stock was called out specifically as a beneficiary of the Muse demand surge alongside ARM and Intel.

▲ ARM Arm Holdings Sector Reprice

ARM outperformed Monday as the Muse/agentic AI narrative drove enthusiasm for CPU-exposed names. Agentic AI's higher compute demands relative to prior AI generations make ARM a direct beneficiary of accelerating adoption.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel was among the outperformers Monday, lifted by the same Muse/CPU tailwind driving AMD and ARM higher. The thesis is that mainstream agentic AI adoption will meaningfully accelerate CPU demand.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. was one of the weakest stocks in the S&P 500 on Monday after the company said it anticipates PC unit volumes falling approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. The downbeat forecast weighed on the broader PC segment.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with the transaction now expected to close within the next two weeks. Key concessions include keeping operations in California, not selling studio lots for at least five years, releasing at least 30 theatrical films annually, and preserving editorial independence at CNN and CBS.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who had hoped for clearer guidance on its pipeline and revenue trajectory.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries sank Monday after Trump announced the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at prices substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. The news hit the entire fertilizer sector hard, with CF, Mosaic, and Nutrien all declining sharply.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, thanks to strategic price promotions and package deals. The company is bucking a broader theme of softness in theme park attendance.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of negative weekend reporting focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility having hit a wall. This adds to the accumulating list of negative AI infrastructure headlines weighing on sentiment in the data center space.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks posted solid gains on Monday led by tech, with the S&P 500 up 114 points or 149 basis points, the Nasdaq surging 226 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P and Russell 2000 meaningfully lagged. The rally was driven by two main themes: enthusiasm around Meta's Muse AI agent and hopes that the UN General Assembly in New York this week could cool Middle East tensions. Despite the index-level strength, a large number of individual stocks hit 52-week lows, underscoring the narrow breadth of the advance.

Treasuries were mixed with 2-year yields finishing flat while 10 and 30-year yields dipped 4 basis points. The market is pricing in roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes across the Fed's final two meetings of the year. Both Chicago Fed President Goolsbee and St. Louis Fed President Musalem delivered hawkish commentary, with Musalem explicitly warning that further monetary tightening will be needed given persistent demand and recurring supply shocks.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

All the macro problems trace back to elevated oil prices, and a sustained drop would meaningfully improve the outlook for rates, inflation, the Fed, corporate margins, and consumer confidence. The White House appears motivated to cool Middle East tensions, and the UN General Assembly provides a rare forum for diplomacy with Iranian officials in attendance. Meta's Muse is generating enormous enthusiasm as a potential mainstream agentic AI breakthrough, lifting the broader CPU and AI infrastructure trade. Major upcoming catalysts — Meta Connect Wednesday, OpenAI Dev Day the following Tuesday, and Micron earnings — give bulls near-term positive events to trade into.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical landscape deteriorated over the weekend with Houthis launching brazen attacks on Saudi cities and Ukraine executing its largest-ever drone strike on Russia, yet Brent is still above $100 and refined product supply remains severely strained. Fed officials are clearly not done hiking, and the forces keeping yields elevated — resilient growth, persistent inflation, AI-driven issuance, tariffs, and fiscal imbalances — are not going away. A slew of negative AI headlines accumulated including the Anthropic IPO delay, SB Energy IPO pushback, Oracle data center debt trouble, and Texas restricting new data center permits. France is emerging as a fresh sovereign bond risk given political paralysis and deteriorating fiscal dynamics.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously mixed — the bull case hinges entirely on a geopolitical resolution that cools oil prices and eases inflation pressure, while the bear case is structural and multi-layered. The key catalyst this week is the UN General Assembly and Trump's Tuesday address, followed by Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night and the arrival of China's Xi in Washington.

Sector Watch

Tech broadly outperformed, led by AI and CPU-exposed names on Muse enthusiasm. Energy underperformed despite elevated Brent as Brent fell over 350 basis points on UN diplomacy hopes. Fertilizers were hit hard on the Belarus potash deal news. Insurance, consumer staples, managed care, transports, and cable/telecom all saw notable weakness with multiple names hitting 52-week lows. Digital REITs outperformed. The data center theme faces mixed signals — strong AI demand enthusiasm offset by the Texas permitting freeze, SB Energy IPO delay, and Oracle debt concerns.
