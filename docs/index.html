VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by 145 basis points of tariff refund tailwinds and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted comp linearity improved through the quarter. Operating expenses as a percent of sales rose 100 basis points year-over-year to 33.4%, a meaningful deterioration.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly would have beaten even excluding tariff refunds — the refund was an 11-cent tailwind, meaning organic EPS of 42 cents still cleared the bar. Guidance is mixed — fiscal 2027 sales guidance was trimmed but EPS outlook was left unchanged. A clean beat with quality underpinnings.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS estimates, reporting 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited a difficult macro landscape. The miss was driven by fundamental margin pressure, not one-time items.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's new Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect on Wednesday night, with investor expectations growing more bullish. Shopify confirmed it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform, a meaningful agentic commerce win — contrasting with Amazon's decision to block Muse from its storefront. OpenAI is also reportedly developing a competing product, validating the category.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify announced it will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, positioning the company as the preferred agentic commerce partner versus Amazon, which blocked Muse. This is a strategic differentiator that could drive incremental transaction volume and platform relevance in the emerging AI agent shopping category.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market anticipated. The rate cuts are a direct headwind to revenue and margins for lab testing companies. The move is a sector-wide negative, not company-specific.

▼ LH Labcorp Other

Labcorp shares fell alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS's announcement of larger-than-expected Medicare lab service rate cuts. As with Quest, this is a direct reimbursement headwind with no company-specific offset. The magnitude of the cut versus expectations is the key negative surprise.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced the acquisition of RealSense — described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI — for approximately $500 million upfront plus up to an additional $106.5 million. The deal is a strategic move to expand Cognex's capabilities in Physical AI and robotics, a high-growth end market.

▲ CPRI Capri Holdings M&A

A WWD article published Tuesday speculates that Capri Holdings — parent of Michael Kors and Jimmy Choo — may be exploring a sale. No deal has been confirmed, but the report introduces M&A optionality for the stock. The company has been under pressure and a sale could represent a meaningful premium catalyst.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, including 22% weight loss in trial participants. This is a meaningful clinical data point that strengthens Viking's competitive position in the increasingly crowded but high-value obesity drug market.
