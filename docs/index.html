VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — tariff refunds added 145 basis points to gross margins and a non-cash LIFO impact added another 105 basis points. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive comp linearity, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100 basis points year over year.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind, EPS was still 42 cents versus the Street's 36 cents. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year over year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion versus $2.17 billion expected. Management cited a difficult macro landscape.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect Wednesday night, and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform. Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform, but the Shopify integration and growing buzz are building bullish expectations into the event.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify announced it will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful agentic commerce partnership. This stands in contrast to Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a competitive edge in the emerging AI-agent shopping channel.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The rate cuts are a direct headwind to revenue and margins for lab testing firms.

▼ LH Labcorp Other

Labcorp is down alongside Quest Diagnostics following new CMS Medicare lab payment rates that came in worse than expected. Both lab testing companies face a meaningful reimbursement headwind from the larger-than-anticipated rate cuts.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal positions Cognex more aggressively in the Physical AI and robotics space.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the trial data. This is a meaningful clinical milestone that reinforces VK2735 as a competitive asset in the crowded but high-stakes obesity drug market.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst day and outlined 2029 sales targets of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21% estimate. The company also adopted a $1 billion share buyback authorization, adding a capital return angle to the growth story.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Iran has reportedly offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the US takes initial steps to ease military pressure, and Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline appears to be coming partially back online, which could relieve oil supply pressure. AI enthusiasm remains elevated ahead of a major catalyst cluster — Meta Connect, OpenAI DevDay, and Micron earnings — with Meta's Muse agent seeing surging downloads and Shopify integrating it for agentic checkout. Pockets of tech, housing, travel, and consumer staples are all showing strength, suggesting the market has enough underlying demand to hold levels.

BEAR CASE

Financials are broadly underperforming with banks, brokers, insurers, and asset managers all in the red, which is a notable drag and could signal risk-off rotation beneath the surface. CMS lab payment rate cuts larger than anticipated are hitting Quest and LabCorp, and managed care names are also weak, pointing to healthcare sector headwinds. Texas Governor Abbott halting data center permitting adds regulatory risk to the AI infrastructure buildout, and SB Energy's delayed IPO due to investor pushback on valuation suggests sentiment on the data center boom may be cooling at the margin.

Market Outlook

Neutral to cautiously optimistic lean — the market is treading water ahead of a dense AI catalyst window over the next 10 days, including Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night, OpenAI's DevDay on September 29th, and Micron earnings on September 30th. The key catalyst to watch today is any diplomatic signal out of the UN on Iran and the Strait of Hormuz.
