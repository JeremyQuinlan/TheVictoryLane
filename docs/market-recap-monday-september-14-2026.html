RATES & FED

The 2-year yield ticked up 2 basis points while the 10- and 30-year were roughly unchanged. ---

MARKET OUTLOOK

The equal-weight S&P actually finishing green Monday while the cap-weighted indices sold off tells you something important: this isn't broad deterioration, it's a rotation story driven by AI anxiety. The Anthropic "pacing the frontier" essay rattled pick-and-shovel names but arguably benefits hyperscalers, software, and Treasuries if capex does slow — the bulls have a credible counterargument here. Wednesday's Fed decision is the week's fulcrum; a confident one-and-done message could actually rally the long end of the curve and remove a key equity headwind. The bigger structural risk remains energy — Hormuz, the Houthis' Red Sea stranglehold, and the Saudi East-West Pipeline outage are compounding toward a genuine supply crisis, and without a diplomatic off-ramp in sight, oil is the market's most dangerous variable.

GEOPOLITICAL

- The GCC-Iran foreign ministers meeting in Oman, originally scheduled for Monday, was postponed due to lack of consensus on Hormuz traffic management — a significant diplomatic setback (FT). Saudi Arabia reportedly holds only 5–7 days of export-ready oil at Yanbu if the East-West Pipeline stays offline (Reuters).

- Iran's internal chain of command appears badly broken — the commercial ship attacks that blew up the summer MOU occurred without IRGC leadership authorization and infuriated moderate factions; the Supreme Leader remains in hiding and unable to adjudicate (NYT). Trump nonetheless said Iran "wants to make a deal, quickly and badly" and the US is "open to" reengaging with Tehran.

- Trump claimed Russia and Ukraine agreed not to strike each other's energy infrastructure — though the pledge's durability is deeply uncertain — and separately told Kyiv to stop hitting Russian diesel refineries (Reuters). Russia's new Geran drones and Banderol cruise missiles, built with Chinese components, are giving Moscow a battlefield edge (WSJ).

- VP Vance said the US is speaking directly with the Houthis in Yemen, claiming "we feel like we are on top of the situation." The Houthis have extended control over Yemen's west coast, giving them substantial leverage over Red Sea shipping lanes.

- China's state media blasted Anthropic's AI slowdown call as a "Cold War tactic" aimed at blocking Chinese advancement; the foreign ministry cited US "fearmongering" (Reuters, Bloomberg). Separately, China's spy agency warned that rapid AI advancement could undermine domestic political and social stability (FT).

- The EPA announced Monday it plans to no longer limit climate pollution from power plants (NYT).
