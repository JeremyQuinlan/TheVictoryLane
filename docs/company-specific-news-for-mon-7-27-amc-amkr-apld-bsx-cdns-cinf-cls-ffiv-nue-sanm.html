COMPANY NEWS

CINF (Cincinnati Financial) missed badly on Q2 EPS — $1.43 vs. the Street's $1.82 — with the shortfall driven by a combined ratio of 100.8%, roughly 300 basis points worse than anticipated, as elevated catastrophe losses weighed heavily. Net premiums written also came in just below consensus. The miss stands out given that peers TRV and CB reported strong results with minimal cat losses this cycle.

BSX (Boston Scientific) announced a post-close restructuring program that will trigger $700–800MM in charges while generating approximately $500MM in annualized savings, implying meaningful headcount reductions ahead.

UHS (Universal Health Services) posted Q2 EBITDA of $677.9MM vs. the Street's $683MM, and trimmed full-year EBITDA guidance to $2.61–2.717B from a prior range of $2.641–2.789B. Not a disaster in isolation, but last week's beat-and-raise from THC had set a higher bar.

NUE (Nucor) delivered clean upside on Q2 EPS ($4.84 vs. $4.50), EBITDA ($2.02B vs. $1.92B), and sales ($10.4B vs. $10B), driven by higher average selling prices and volumes in the steel mills segment. Management guided Q3 consolidated earnings above Q2 levels, implying EPS north of $4.84 against a current consensus of $4.95.

AMKR (Amkor) beat on Q2 EBITDA ($400MM vs. the Street's $336MM) but guided Q3 sales to a $2B midpoint, short of the $2.11B consensus — a near-term headwind, though the recent Nvidia partnership keeps sentiment broadly constructive.

APLD (Applied Digital) blew out FQ4 adjusted revenue ($240.4MM vs. $95MM expected) and adjusted EBITDA ($42.4MM vs. $25.3MM), and management flagged roughly $20B in newly signed long-term contracted revenue across three hyperscaler leases. The catch: free cash flow was -$1.157B, with capex of $1.289B running at approximately 5.4x quarterly revenue.

CDNS (Cadence Design Systems) beat Q2 EPS and revenue ($2.11/$1.584B vs. $2.06/$1.574B), grew backlog to $8.1B, and raised full-year EPS guidance to $8.10 from $7.90 while lifting the revenue growth outlook to 19% from 17%. Management cited "accelerating demand for AI-driven solutions."

CLS (Celestica) put up one of the stronger prints of the cycle — Q2 EPS of $2.54 vs. $2.30, on sales of $4.7B (+62% YoY vs. $4.4B expected). Full-year guidance was raised to EPS/sales of $11.30/$20.5B from $10.15/$19B, and the preliminary 2027 sales outlook implies a number north of $34B vs. Street expectations of $27.6B. The early 2027 framing is the real headline here.

FFIV (F5) beat FQ3 handily — EPS of $4.73 vs. $3.98, sales of $865MM vs. $832MM, with operating margins outperforming by roughly 160 basis points. FQ4 guidance midpoints of $4.20/$880MM also cleared the Street ($4.13/$857MM). Fundamentals are clean; the only knock is that expectations heading in were already elevated.

SANM (Sanmina) posted FQ3 EPS of $3.31 vs. the Street's $2.77, but the FQ4 sales guidance midpoint of $3.45B missed the $3.52B consensus — a disappointment that lands at a delicate moment given softening sentiment toward AI infrastructure names. The FQ4 EPS range did come in ahead of plan, and management talked up FY27 and FY28 prospects.
