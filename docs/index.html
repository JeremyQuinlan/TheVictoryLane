VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by a 145 basis point gross margin tailwind from tariff refunds and a 105 basis point non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps of +1.6% missed the Street's +3.1% estimate, and the operating expense ratio worsened 100 basis points year-over-year to 33.4% of sales. Management noted positive comp linearity through the quarter, which is providing some relief.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind still yields 42 cents versus the 36-cent estimate. Guidance is mixed with the fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged. The clean earnings beat is the key takeaway here.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the 13.85% estimate. Revenue came in at $2.311 billion, down 8.4% year-over-year but slightly above the $2.17 billion estimate. Management cited a difficult macro landscape as a headwind.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads as investor expectations build ahead of Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET. Shopify announced it will allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful distribution win — in contrast to Amazon blocking Muse from its platform. OpenAI is reportedly developing a competing product, validating the agentic AI race.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases directly on its e-commerce platform, a notable agentic commerce partnership. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify merchants an edge in AI-driven consumer spending. The deal positions Shopify as a key infrastructure layer in the emerging agentic commerce ecosystem.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday following new Medicare payment rates from CMS for lab services, with the reductions coming in larger than the market had anticipated. The negative rate surprise is a direct hit to revenue without an easy offset. Lab Corp is experiencing the same headwind and both names are underperforming.

▼ LH Laboratory Corporation Other

Lab Corp shares are selling off alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS announcing Medicare payment rate cuts for lab services that were worse than expected. This is a sector-wide policy headwind impacting both major lab testing companies simultaneously. The magnitude of the reduction caught the market off guard.

▲ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced an acquisition of Intel's RealSense business, the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. This is a meaningful strategic move to build out capabilities at the intersection of robotics and AI. The deal expands Cognex's addressable market in automation and Physical AI applications.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, with patients achieving 22% weight loss. This is a clinically meaningful result that keeps Viking competitive in the increasingly crowded obesity drug space. The data point is a positive catalyst for the stock.

▲ ONON On Holdings Buyback

On Holdings held its analyst meeting and outlined plans to achieve 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street's CHF 5.4 billion estimate, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21% forecast. The company also adopted a $1 billion buyback authorization. Both the above-consensus long-term targets and the capital return program are bullish signals.
