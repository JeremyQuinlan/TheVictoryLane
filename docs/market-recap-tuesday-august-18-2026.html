MARKET OUTLOOK

The bear case is gaining traction, and it's not just noise — rising yields are the real threat here, and they're moving higher despite a string of dovish July data points (CPI, PPI, import/export prices all soft). The culprits are structural: a US national debt on track to top $40T by end of August and $50T by 2029 (Barron's), record IG bond issuance for a third straight month (Bloomberg), AI-linked debt flooding the market (FT), tariffs, a shrinking workforce, and Fed credibility concerns. Tech's vulnerability is increasingly capital-markets dependent — when capex swamps operating cash flow and borrowing costs rise, the data center buildout gets threatened. Tuesday's price action underneath the surface was actually better than the headline numbers suggest (software, insurance, healthcare, energy all held up), but the rate overhang isn't going away.

GEOPOLITICAL

- Iran has threatened to go on the offensive in the Strait of Hormuz if diplomacy fails (Reuters), while Houthi forces are escalating Red Sea attacks as the conflict's violence spreads regionally (WSJ).

- Pennsylvania Governor Shapiro is imposing stringent new requirements on data center development in the state — described as conditions most developers will find "exceptionally difficult" to meet — the latest instance of political pushback against the AI build-out (Washington Post).
