Macro · Rates

US equity futures are rallying Monday morning with S&P up 49 points or 65 basis points, Nasdaq up 329 points or 110 basis points, and Russell 2000 up 47 basis points. European indices are up 85-120 basis points with outperformance in industrials, basic resources, tech, and banks. Asian markets were broadly higher overnight — Hang Seng up 1.18%, KOSPI up 1.65%, Taiwan TAIEX up 1.14% — despite a weekend full of negative news flow including escalating Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and Ukraine's largest drone strike on Russia. The primary driver of equity strength appears to be optimism around the UN General Assembly in New York this week and the prospect of diplomatic progress on Iran.

Treasuries have a bid this morning with yields down 2-3 basis points across the curve. The market is pricing in roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes over the Fed's final two meetings of the year, with about 50/50 odds of an increase at the October 28th meeting, and another 35 basis points of additional hikes penciled in for 2027. Fed Presidents Schmid and Kashkari both made hawkish remarks over the weekend, warning that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil and is reaching all corners of the economy. Treasury is also set to issue over one trillion dollars in T-bills over the next 12 months, pushing short-term debt above 24% of marketable debt, above its own 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly is the primary driver of today's rally, with Trump signaling openness to meeting Iran's president and China and Pakistan reportedly pressuring Iran to rein in the Houthis. Even a modest improvement in Middle East tensions could trigger an outsized drop in oil prices, which would cascade into lower yields, reduced inflation, better corporate margins, and improved consumer confidence. Saudi Arabia is also working to increase ship-to-ship oil transfers to compensate for supply shortages, providing some near-term supply relief. The Trump-Xi summit on Thursday could also yield select tariff reductions and a trade truce extension, adding a second potential positive catalyst for the week.

BEAR CASE

Nearly all weekend news flow was net negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest drone strike on Russia yet, and a deluge of troubling AI headlines hit after Friday's close including a delayed Anthropic IPO, underwhelming Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI's roughly 280 billion dollar negative free cash flow outlook through 2030, and Oracle data center debt problems. Monetary policy globally remains firmly hawkish with the Fed, ECB, and BOJ all having hiked this month and multiple additional hikes priced in. Oil tanker costs are surging, motor oil is being rationed in the US, and the structural energy supply disruption from the Iran war shows no sign of resolution — diplomacy at the UN GA may simply be wishful thinking.
