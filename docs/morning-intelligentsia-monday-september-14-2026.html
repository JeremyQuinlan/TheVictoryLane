MARKET OUTLOOK

This is a genuinely ugly macro setup: oil is in crisis mode, AI capex sentiment just took a significant hit from Dario Amodei's pacing essay, and the Fed is hiking into it all on Wednesday. The bull case hinges entirely on a diplomatic breakthrough in the Middle East — cool the oil fire and most of these headwinds reverse quickly. But with the GCC-Iran Oman meeting postponed, hardliners apparently running loose inside the IRGC, and Saudi Arabia holding only 5–7 days of export buffer at Yanbu, that resolution feels distant. Software and defensives are the right place to hide; AI infrastructure names and rate-sensitive long duration are the places to avoid until clarity emerges.

GEOPOLITICAL

- The GCC-Iran foreign ministers meeting in Oman, meant to address Hormuz traffic management, has been postponed due to lack of consensus (FT). US Energy Secretary Wright separately downplayed any prospect of a breakthrough (Bloomberg).

- Iran is deeply fractured — hardline factions apparently carried out commercial ship attacks this summer without authorization from IRGC leadership, infuriating moderates. The Supreme Leader remains in hiding, removing the one arbiter who could resolve internal disputes (NYT).

- Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline is shut following attacks by Iran-aligned Iraqi militias; Riyadh has only 5–7 days of oil reserves at Yanbu to sustain exports if the pipeline stays offline (Reuters). All three critical oil export routes — Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, and the East-West Pipeline — are now severely compromised.

- Houthis now control virtually the entire western coast of Yemen, giving them enormous leverage over the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb (newsletter).

- Iran received Chinese satellite imagery that assisted in lethal strikes on a US base in Jordan in July (WSJ). Russia's new Geran drones and Banderol cruise missiles, built with Chinese components, are giving Moscow a battlefield edge in Ukraine (WSJ). Trump told Zelensky to stop hitting Russian diesel refineries (Reuters).

- BRICs nations pushed for Middle East peace at their New Delhi summit (FT). China blasted Anthropic's AI slowdown call as a "Cold War tactic" designed to block Chinese advancement; China's foreign ministry condemned US "fearmongering" (Reuters, Bloomberg).
