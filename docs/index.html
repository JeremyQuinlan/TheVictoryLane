◆ ANTHROPIC Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO has been pushed from October to November, and its projected year-end ARR of greater than 100 billion dollars disappointed bulls who were hoping for 120 billion dollars or more. The company is considering launching a powerful new model before its IPO to recapture momentum lost to OpenAI's Astra. It plans to raise over 100 billion dollars at a valuation above 2 trillion dollars when it goes public.

▼ OPENAI OpenAI Other

The Financial Times reported OpenAI expects negative free cash flow of approximately 280 billion dollars during the 2026 through 2030 period as spending dramatically outpaces revenue. Despite the massive cash burn, OpenAI appears to be gaining momentum versus Anthropic following the launch of its Astra model, with reports of a sharp inflection higher in OpenAI growth in recent weeks.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising concerns about the company's data center financing exposure.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. To resolve the suit, Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Clearing this legal hurdle would be a meaningful step toward closing the Warner deal.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Paramount's advanced settlement talks with California over the Warner deal represent a positive development for deal closure. If Paramount agrees to the proposed conditions — preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting select cable channels — the regulatory path for the merger improves meaningfully.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. Franchisee financial stress is a negative signal for system health and could weigh on Wendy's near-term unit economics and development outlook.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk Activist Entry

Shareholders are pressuring Novo Nordisk to diversify away from its heavy GLP-1 concentration by pursuing M&A, according to a Financial Times report. The pressure highlights growing investor concern about the company's dependence on a single drug class despite that class's current dominance.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on ten or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, according to the Wall Street Journal. This would represent a meaningful commercial recovery for the Starliner program after its high-profile setbacks and renewed competition from SpaceX.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture will embed a team of evaluators to monitor safety and alignment at Anthropic, announced Friday night. The partnership positions Accenture as a key player in AI governance infrastructure as regulatory and corporate pressure around AI oversight intensifies.

▼ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores as the grocery chain pushes back against supplier price increases. The move signals intensifying pressure between major retailers and consumer brands on pricing, and is a negative read for Red Bull's distribution footprint in the US.

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by deteriorating AI sentiment and escalating Middle East conflict. Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Even a small improvement in Middle East geopolitics would likely trigger an outsized drop in oil prices, with favorable knock-on effects including lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The UN General Assembly running September 22nd through 28th in New York brings together officials from key Middle East nations including Iran, creating a real diplomatic opportunity. The Trump-Xi summit could deliver select tariff reductions on roughly 30 billion dollars worth of goods, providing a modest but tangible trade tailwind. Saudi Arabia is also increasing ship-to-ship oil transfers to help compensate for supply shortages elsewhere.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical landscape is deteriorating rapidly — Iran is tightening its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, Iraqi militias, and the Houthis, who dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend. The weekend AI news dump was broadly negative: Anthropic's IPO was pushed to November, OpenAI is projecting negative 280 billion dollars in free cash flow through 2030, and Big Tech's off-balance sheet AI liabilities surged roughly 300 billion dollars in the last 12 months alone. The energy price spike is beginning to work its way through the system and could trigger demand destruction and margin compression. Hopes for UN General Assembly diplomatic breakthroughs may prove to be wishful thinking.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautious-to-bearish heading into the week. The weekend brought a cluster of negative AI headlines and worsening Middle East geopolitics, both of which represent meaningful headwinds. The key catalyst to watch is the Trump-Xi summit on Thursday September 24th, which could deliver modest tariff relief and a positive risk catalyst if talks progress.

Sector Watch

AI and Big Tech face a cluster of headwinds: off-balance sheet liability exposure up roughly 300 billion dollars in 12 months, massive OpenAI cash burn projections, and a delayed Anthropic IPO. Energy is the central macro variable — Middle East escalation via Houthis attacking Saudi Arabia is tightening global supply, driving diesel and motor oil price spikes. Autos face dual pressure from a challenging China market and Chinese memory supply dynamics affecting component pricing. Defense and aerospace benefit marginally from elevated geopolitical activity but the Pentagon is described as stretched thin.
