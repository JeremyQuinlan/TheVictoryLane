Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The UN General Assembly runs September 22-28 in New York, bringing together leaders including Iran's president, and Trump has signaled openness to a meeting — even a small diplomatic signal could crack oil prices meaningfully lower. If oil sustainably slips, the knock-on effects are substantial: lower yields, less hawkish monetary policy, reduced margin pressure, and improved consumer confidence. Data center and pick-and-shovel AI infrastructure stocks are outperforming in Europe this morning, suggesting the market is not fully abandoning the AI trade despite the negative headlines. Asian markets broadly gained overnight with Korea up 165 basis points and China up nearly 100 basis points, reflecting constructive global risk appetite.

BEAR CASE

Nearly all of the weekend news flow was net negative — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine launched its largest drone strike on Russia, and a flood of troubling AI headlines emerged including a delayed Anthropic IPO, underwhelming Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of approximately $280 billion through 2030, and Oracle data center debt problems. The geopolitical backdrop remains grim with Iran tightening its grip on global energy via Hormuz, aligned militias, and the Houthis, and diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly may prove to be wishful thinking. On trade, Washington is expected to ultimately hike the aggregate tariff rate on Chinese imports back to pre-Supreme Court IEEPA levels in coming weeks, and the one-year trade truce extension was not secured over the weekend. The Fed remains hawkish globally with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month, and markets pricing in further increases from multiple central banks in October and November.

Market Outlook

Adam leans cautiously bullish for today's session with the key catalyst being any diplomatic signal out of the UN General Assembly — specifically whether Trump meets with Iran's president and whether any language emerges that could meaningfully de-escalate Middle East tensions. The thesis is simple: nearly all of the market's macro problems trace back to oil, and even a modest geopolitical improvement could trigger an outsized drop in energy prices with a cascade of favorable downstream effects including lower yields, better margins, and improved consumer sentiment.

Sector Watch

AI infrastructure and data center names face meaningful headwinds after a weekend flood of negative headlines — delayed Anthropic IPO, underwhelming Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of approximately $280 billion through 2030, and Oracle's Project Jupiter debt issues. Despite this, data center pick-and-shovel stocks are outperforming in European trade Monday morning, suggesting some resilience in the infrastructure layer. Energy is sliding with Brent down 170 basis points to approximately $102.10, extending losses from late last week, though geopolitical risk remains elevated. Chinese memory maker CXMT announced its next-generation platform has entered mass production, which could pressure pricing for Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron over time. Theme parks are showing divergence with Disney outperforming peers via promotions while the broader industry faces traffic headwinds.
