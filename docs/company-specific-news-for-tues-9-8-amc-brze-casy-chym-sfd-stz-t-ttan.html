COMPANY NEWS

CASY (Casey's) posted a strong FQ1 EPS beat at $7.37 versus the Street's $6.77, though EBITDA came in only modestly ahead at $485MM versus consensus $480MM (Vital Knowledge). Prepared Food & Dispensed Beverage comps ran +4.8%, roughly 20 basis points above plan, and fuel margins surged nearly 17% year-over-year to ~48 cents per gallon. The soft spots: Grocery & General Merchandise comps of +2.7% missed the Street's +3.7%, and gas volume comps dipped 0.3%, suggesting elevated pump prices are weighing on demand. Full-year guidance was reiterated.

SFD (Smithfield Foods) dropped a painful preannouncement, guiding FQ3 operating income to just $115–175MM against a consensus of ~$296MM — a massive shortfall driven by "continued compression in the industry fresh pork market spread and lower hog prices," per management. The Packaged Meats segment is tracking inline, so the damage is concentrated in fresh pork. This follows TSN's own downside preannouncement just days prior, making it clear the pressure is sector-wide, not idiosyncratic.

STZ (Constellation Brands) underperformed Tuesday after management struck a cautious tone at the Barclays conference, flagging that off-premises trends deteriorated starting in August — "it was a pretty lackluster Aug relative to the start of the summer," per the company.

CHYM (Chime) announced a $590MM all-cash acquisition of Stride Bank, its banking partner of more than seven years, giving it a nationally chartered bank and a faster path to full-stack ownership versus pursuing a de novo charter. Management expects more than $100MM in net synergies by eliminating partner-bank fees and reducing funding costs. Separately, Chime issued an upside preannouncement, now guiding 2026 revenue growth to +26–27% (prior: +25–27%) with EBITDA margins of 17–18% (prior: 15–16%).

BRZE (Braze) delivered modest FQ2 beats across the board: EPS of 19 cents versus the Street's 16 cents, revenue of $227.2MM versus $220MM, and RPOs of $1.092B versus $1.088B. Full-year guidance was nudged higher to EPS of 64–65 cents (prior: 61–65 cents) and revenue of $910–913MM (prior: $895–899MM).

T (AT&T) reiterated its financial outlook and capital allocation plan as originally laid out during its Q2 2026 earnings release — no new incremental guidance.

TTAN (ServiceTitan) beat on FQ2 with revenue up 21% to $293MM versus the Street's $286MM, operating margins expanding 310 basis points to 15.2% versus the expected 13.6%, and GTV of $26.8B versus $26.3B. Full-year guidance was raised to revenue of $1.139–1.144B (prior: $1.13–1.14B) and operating income of $152–154MM (prior: $142–147MM). The one minor blemish: Q3 revenue guidance of $286MM comes in just shy of the Street's $288MM.
