Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 12:08 PM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top performers in the market today as Meta Muse is showing strong early demand and traders are front-running Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. The AI assistant is being seen as a potential mainstream breakthrough for agentic AI, pulling the entire sector higher. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing product in response.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream consumer market — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, directly benefiting CPU makers. This represents a potential demand re-rating for the CPU segment broadly. AMD is among the top outperformers in tech today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis. The logic is that if Meta Muse breaks agentic AI into the mainstream, the resulting compute workloads heavily favor CPU architectures, creating a meaningful uplift to demand forecasts. Intel is listed among the top outperformers in tech today.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the stock and the broader PC sector. This is a meaningful negative data point for near-term PC demand. The guidance is pressuring HPQ and peers.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general, clearing a key legal obstacle that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. Shares are jumping sharply on the news. Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central as part of the settlement.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement paves the way for the merger to proceed. The removal of a major regulatory overhang is the primary catalyst. Both names are listed among the top outperformers in the media sector today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for more concrete guidance. The lack of specificity at a high-profile capital markets event is being read as a negative signal. The stock is underperforming European peers.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative FT article Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility having hit a wall. This raises questions about the health of Oracle's data center financing strategy. The article adds to existing concerns about Big Tech off-balance-sheet AI liability exposure.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers, many of whom are seeing traffic headwinds, by leaning into price promotions and package deals. A WSJ report highlights the strategy as a differentiator in a soft travel environment. This is a positive data point for Disney's parks segment relative to competitors.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and other potash-linked names including Mosaic and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at prices substantially lower than current Canadian potash pricing. This represents a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers. The news is hitting the entire fertilizer complex hard.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with notable Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The two dominant themes are AI enthusiasm driven by Meta Muse momentum and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off Houthi airstrikes at the last minute. Diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly is supporting risk appetite, with hopes for de-escalation in the Middle East and a potential Trump-Iran meeting.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting a mild flight-to-safety tone even as equities rally. The dollar is not highlighted as a major driver today. Treasury supply remains a watch item as Bessent is leaning on T-bill issuance heavily, with bills set to exceed 24% of marketable debt against a 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse is generating strong demand signals and pulling the entire AI ecosystem higher, with CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel benefiting from the thesis that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive. Oil's roughly 400 basis point decline is a broad macro tailwind, easing inflation fears and lifting consumer and travel stocks. Diplomatic momentum heading into the UN General Assembly is keeping geopolitical risk premiums in check, and the US-China trade truce extension deadline is still weeks away.

BEAR CASE

The equal-weight S&P is up only 30 basis points, signaling the rally is narrow and concentrated in mega-cap tech — breadth is weak. Geopolitical risks remain acute: Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia are escalating, Iran is issuing warnings, and the Middle East situation is described as having potential for unforeseen escalation. The US-China trade truce was not extended on Sunday, and apartment debt refinancing of roughly 1.8 trillion dollars at higher rates is a slow-burning financial stress risk.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean for today's session, driven by AI re-rating on Meta Muse demand signals and falling oil prices. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which is already pulling forward AI and CPU-linked buying. Geopolitical diplomacy at the UN General Assembly is the macro wildcard — any progress on Iran or trade truce extension could extend gains, while a breakdown would pressure risk.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU-linked names on Meta Muse momentum. Energy is the key laggard as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi airstrike cancellation and diplomatic optimism — APA, COP, DVN, EOG, and XOM all under pressure. Potash and materials are being hit by Trump's Belarus supply deal comments. Travel and leisure names are rallying on falling oil and easing geopolitical fears. REITs and data center industrials are also outperforming. Transports are lagging despite oil's drop, with railroads and parcel names underperforming.
