VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta's personal AI agent Muse has seen a surge in demand since launching earlier this month and was among the best-performing stocks in the entire market Monday. The Connect Conference kicks off this week with Zuckerberg's keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET, where investors are expecting more bullish AI product announcements. Amazon separately blocked Muse from accessing its retail website, creating a competitive wrinkle.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD surged Monday on the thesis that Meta's Muse agentic AI product will drive significantly higher CPU demand, as agentic AI workloads are far more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. The stock was among the outperformers in the tech sector alongside ARM and Intel on the same CPU demand optimism.

▲ ARM ARM Holdings Sector Reprice

ARM shares rallied Monday alongside AMD and Intel as investors bet that Meta Muse's success would accelerate agentic AI adoption and drive a meaningful uplift in CPU demand across the industry.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel outperformed Monday as the Muse-driven CPU demand optimism lifted the entire CPU complex. Agentic AI's intensive compute requirements are seen as a structural tailwind for CPU vendors.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. was one of the weakest stocks in the S&P 500 Monday after management said it anticipates PC unit volumes declining approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. The downbeat forecast weighed on the PC sector broadly.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries shares sank Monday after Trump announced the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at prices substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. The news hit the entire fertilizer complex hard as it signals a potential structural shift in North American potash supply and pricing.

▼ MOS Mosaic Company Other

Mosaic fell sharply alongside the fertilizer sector after Trump's Belarus potash deal announcement, which threatens to undercut Canadian potash pricing and pressure fertilizer producers' margins.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for clearer near-term revenue and margin guidance.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount settled a lawsuit with state attorneys general that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with the transaction now expected to close within the next two weeks. Key settlement terms include keeping operations in California, not selling studio lots for at least five years, releasing at least 30 films annually in theaters, and preserving editorial independence at CNN and CBS.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are experiencing traffic headwinds, driven by strategic price promotions and package deals. The company appears to be successfully using pricing levers to defend volume while competitors struggle.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks posted solid gains on Monday led by tech, with the S&P 500 up 114 points or 149 basis points, the Nasdaq surging 226 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P and Russell 2000 meaningfully lagged. The two main drivers were enthusiasm around Meta's Muse agentic AI product and hopes that the UN General Assembly in New York this week could ease Middle East tensions. Notably, a wide swath of individual stocks hit new 52-week lows including names like Nike, McDonald's, Pepsi, and Fiserv, underscoring the narrowness of the rally.

Treasuries were mixed with 2-year yields finishing flat while 10 and 30-year yields dipped 4 basis points. The market is pricing in roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes across the Fed's final two meetings of the year. Chicago Fed President Goolsbee and St. Louis Fed President Musalem both delivered hawkish commentary, with Musalem explicitly warning that further monetary tightening will be needed given persistent demand and recurring supply shocks.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is generating enormous consumer enthusiasm and could be the product that brings agentic AI to the mainstream, lifting the entire CPU and AI infrastructure ecosystem including AMD, ARM, and Intel. If the UN General Assembly produces any diplomatic progress on Iran, oil prices could fall materially, which would ease inflation, take pressure off the Fed, and be broadly bullish for equities. The White House is actively working a $5 billion Middle East energy infrastructure fund as part of a broader strategy to reduce Hormuz dependency and cool the oil crisis. Upcoming AI catalysts — Meta Connect Wednesday, OpenAI Dev Day the following Tuesday, and Micron earnings the following Wednesday — give bulls a series of potential positive surprises to trade into.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical backdrop deteriorated sharply over the weekend with Houthi attacks on Saudi cities and Ukraine's largest ever drone strike on Russia, and global energy supplies remain under severe strain with the East-West Pipeline still offline and refined product prices dangerously elevated. Two Fed speakers delivered hawkish messages Monday, reinforcing that rate hikes remain on the table as supply shocks keep inflation sticky — and the structural forces pushing yields higher are not going away. A flood of negative AI headlines hit late last week including the Anthropic IPO delay, OpenAI's staggering projected negative free cash flow of $280 billion through 2030, Oracle data center debt stress, and Texas halting new data center permits. The market rally was extremely narrow — equal-weight S&P lagged by over 90 basis points and dozens of large-cap names set fresh 52-week lows, signaling the breadth problem under the surface is worsening.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautiously mixed — the rally was tech-narrow and driven by Muse hype and geopolitical hope, both of which are fragile catalysts. The key catalyst to watch this week is Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET, plus Trump's UN address Tuesday and the Xi arrival Wednesday, which sets up the Trump/Xi summit Thursday as the macro event of the week.

Sector Watch

Tech broadly outperformed led by the Muse/CPU agentic AI theme — semis, software, and large-cap tech all strong. Energy was a notable laggard with Brent down over 350 basis points as geopolitical optimism temporarily trumped supply concerns — XOM, CVX, COP, OXY, and refiners all lower. Fertilizers were hit hard by the Trump Belarus potash announcement. Consumer staples, managed care, insurance, transports, and cable/telecom all underperformed. Crypto-linked stocks surged with bitcoin up 680 basis points to roughly $86,600. Digital REITs outperformed while the broader data center narrative faced headwinds from Texas permitting restrictions and the SB Energy IPO delay.
