RATES & FED

Treasuries flat to down 1 basis point ahead of CPI, with investors in wait-and-see mode. Fed's Susan Collins (FT) supported holding rates last month but would back a September hike if inflation fails to cool. The Street expects July headline CPI at +3.4% Y/Y and core at +2.5% Y/Y — a 10 basis point deceleration from June on both measures.

MARKET OUTLOOK

All eyes on Wednesday's CPI, and the framing matters: a hotter print likely forces a September hike, while a modest cool-down gives the Fed cover to pause — though that scenario may actually push long-end yields higher, not lower, as it emboldens the "inflation isn't solved" crowd. Our view is that a September hike, if it comes, is actually the best-case outcome — it reinforces Fed credibility and applies genuine pressure on the long end. The forces driving yields higher (AI capex, war-related supply disruptions, heavy sovereign issuance, FX intervention) aren't going away with one softer CPI print. The AI financing complex — underscored by CoreWeave's capex running at 3.65x revenue — is a structural yield pressure that markets haven't fully priced.

COMPANY NEWS

The revenue backlog reached $104B, up modestly from $99.4B, though management flagged ~$25B in new commitments signed early in Q3.
