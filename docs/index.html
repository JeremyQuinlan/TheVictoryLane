VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by tariff refunds that provided a 145 basis point gross margin tailwind and a non-cash LIFO impact worth another 105 basis points. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive sales linearity in the quarter, which is helping the stock rally.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat holds up even excluding the tariff refund tailwind of 11 cents — clean EPS of 42 cents still topped consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged, making this a solid quality beat overall.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% expectation. Revenue came in at $2.311 billion, down 8.4% year-over-year but slightly above the Street's $2.17 billion estimate, and management cited a difficult macro landscape.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night, and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform — a meaningful distribution win. Investor expectations are growing more bullish into the event, and OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing product, validating the market opportunity.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, giving Muse a major checkout integration ahead of Meta Connect. This contrasts with Amazon's decision to block Muse from its platform, positioning Shopify as a key beneficiary of the agentic commerce wave.

▲ PSKY Peach Sky Other

PSKY is spiking after the company obtained a relatively favorable settlement deal from a group of state attorneys general, removing a major legal overhang. The resolution is being viewed as a positive clearing event for the stock.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares are under pressure after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services, with the reductions coming in larger than the market had anticipated. The rate cuts hit both Quest and Lab Corp simultaneously, creating sector-wide selling pressure in lab testing names.

▼ LH Lab Corp Other

Lab Corp is selling off alongside Quest Diagnostics following new CMS Medicare lab service payment rates that were larger reductions than the Street expected. This is a direct reimbursement headwind with no near-term offset visible.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal positions Cognex deeper into the Physical AI and robotics ecosystem at a time of accelerating industry investment.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, including 22% weight loss in the study. This is a meaningful clinical readout that reinforces Viking's competitive position in the obesity drug market.
