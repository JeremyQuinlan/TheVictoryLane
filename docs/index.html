VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta is one of the top performers in the market today as Meta Muse demand signals are coming in strong ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night. Reports indicate OpenAI is scrambling to develop a competing product, validating Meta Muse's competitive significance. The agentic AI optimism is radiating across the entire AI ecosystem.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is rallying sharply as agentic AI, which Meta Muse could help bring to the consumer mainstream, is seen as significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This is directly bullish for CPU-linked chipmakers as the market reprices the compute demand curve upward. AMD is one of the primary named beneficiaries of this theme.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis, with the market viewing the Meta Muse-driven mainstream adoption of agentic AI as a meaningful demand catalyst for CPU volumes. This is a notable change in narrative for Intel which has struggled for positive catalysts. The CPU reprice is lifting the stock alongside broader AI infrastructure names.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after providing a cautious outlook, specifically flagging that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. This is a notable demand warning that cuts against the consumer tech recovery narrative and weighs on the broader PC supply chain. The guidance cut is the main drag on the stock today.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with Paramount agreeing to preserve CNN's editorial independence and potentially divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The stock is jumping sharply on the news as a major legal overhang is removed. The deal clears a critical path toward merger completion.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement was announced, removing the key legal barrier to the Paramount merger proceeding. The deal resolving the attorney general lawsuits is a significant milestone for the combined entity's path to closing. Both stocks are among the top performers in media today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for clearer forward guidance. The lack of specificity on financial targets was interpreted as a negative signal given elevated expectations. The stock's weakness reflects ongoing investor frustration with the company's communications.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are experiencing traffic headwinds, driven by strategic price promotions and package deals that are attracting consumers. The Wall Street Journal highlighted Disney's parks resilience as a meaningful differentiator versus competitors. This is a positive read-through for Disney's near-term earnings trajectory in the parks segment.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday, focused on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about the financial structure of Oracle's data center buildout ambitions. The article adds a layer of credit market skepticism to Oracle's aggressive infrastructure expansion narrative.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially less than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct and severe pricing threat to North American potash producers and the broader fertilizer complex. The geopolitical shift in supply sourcing is the key negative catalyst.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, Dow up 37 basis points, and the equal-weight S&P a modest 30 basis points. The dominant macro theme is diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly in New York, with hopes that meetings could cool Middle East tensions, including a potential Trump-Iran presidential meeting. Oil is slumping roughly 400 basis points to below $100 a barrel after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, providing a mild tailwind to risk assets. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly relies on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the Treasury's roughly 20% target. No Fed commentary is a major driver today; rates are taking a back seat to geopolitical and AI themes.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse optimism is igniting a broad AI and CPU-linked rally, with agentic AI seen as far more compute-intensive than prior iterations, directly benefiting AMD, ARM, and Intel. Diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly could further compress the geopolitical risk premium, keeping oil prices suppressed and consumer confidence supported. Treasury yields are slightly lower, providing a supportive backdrop for growth and rate-sensitive equities including REITs and tech. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes a major legal overhang and signals M&A markets remain open.

BEAR CASE

The Middle East situation remains deeply unstable — Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend and Iran has warned of a fierce response to any resumed US military action, meaning the oil calm could reverse quickly. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured Sunday despite talks, and it expires November 10, leaving a meaningful deadline risk on the horizon. Big Tech AI off-balance sheet liabilities are up roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, a growing but underappreciated risk. HP Inc.'s warning of a 5% decline in PC unit volumes in calendar year 2027 signals consumer demand fragility beneath the AI enthusiasm.

Market Outlook

Mildly bullish lean for today's session, driven by two powerful concurrent tailwinds — an AI/agentic computing surge led by Meta Muse enthusiasm and a meaningful de-escalation in geopolitical risk as oil pulls back. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could either extend or deflate the AI rally.

Sector Watch

AI and CPU-linked tech is the standout outperformer as agentic AI themes ignite broad gains across semiconductors, software, and infrastructure. Energy is a meaningful underperformer as oil slides roughly 400 basis points on geopolitical de-escalation hopes — exploration and production names including APA, COP, DVN, EOG, and XOM are all lower. Potash and fertilizer stocks are getting hit hard on Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. REITs and data center infrastructure names are rallying on lower yields and AI capex enthusiasm. Transports are lagging with rails and parcel names underperforming. Crypto-linked equities are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points.
