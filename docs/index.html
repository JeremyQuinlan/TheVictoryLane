Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 01:51 PM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the centerpiece of today's AI rally, with reports of strong consumer demand for the app driving broad enthusiasm across the tech sector. Zuckerberg is set to deliver a keynote at Meta's Connect Conference on Wednesday night, which the market is treating as a major catalyst. OpenAI is reportedly scrambling to develop a competing agentic product, validating the strategic significance of Muse.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is outperforming sharply on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream consumer market — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This creates a meaningful demand uplift narrative for CPU makers that had previously been overshadowed by GPU dominance. AMD is among the top performers in the market today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis stemming from Meta Muse enthusiasm. The narrative is that mainstream agentic AI workloads will drive a meaningful reacceleration in CPU demand. This is one of the more notable positive read-throughs for a stock that has struggled.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the broader PC supply chain. This is notable context against the broader AI hardware enthusiasm — the consumer PC cycle remains challenged. The guidance cut is a clear negative for sentiment in the PC segment.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general that clears the path for its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery to proceed. The deal reportedly involves preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Both Paramount and WBD shares are jumping sharply on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement removes the key regulatory obstacle to the Paramount merger. The deal clears a major legal overhang that had created uncertainty around the transaction's completion. This is one of the bigger individual stock moves in the media sector today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting. Investors were looking for more specific guidance around upcoming blockbuster launches and were disappointed. The selloff reflects a high expectations bar for GLP-1 leaders heading into pipeline catalysts.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are experiencing traffic headwinds, with the company's price promotions and package deals driving relative strength. The WSJ highlighted Disney as a standout in an otherwise sluggish theme park environment. This is a positive read-through for Disney's near-term parks revenue trajectory.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico dubbed Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about demand and execution on Oracle's aggressive data center buildout. This is a headwind for sentiment around Oracle's AI infrastructure narrative.

▼ MOS Mosaic Other

Potash names including Mosaic, CF Industries, and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially below what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. If completed, this deal would be a significant negative for North American potash producers on both volume and pricing. The group is among the worst performers in the market today.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points, and Russell 2000 +50 basis points. The dominant themes are AI enthusiasm around Meta Muse and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday, fueling diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly. Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1-3 basis points across the curve, while Brent crude slumps roughly 400 basis points to below $100.

Treasuries are seeing a mild bid today with yields slipping 1-3 basis points across the curve, consistent with the risk-on but geopolitically cautious tone. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt and above the department's stated target of roughly 20%. No major Fed commentary is driving price action today; the macro catalyst is geopolitical rather than monetary.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse demand signals and Zuckerberg's upcoming Connect keynote are igniting a broad AI/tech rally, with CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel benefiting from the thesis that agentic AI is far more processor-intensive. Oil falling sharply on Houthi ceasefire hopes and UN diplomacy optimism is a macro tailwind that relieves pressure on inflation and consumer sentiment. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes a key regulatory overhang and signals M&A momentum in media.

BEAR CASE

The US and China failed to extend their trade truce at the weekend talks despite optimistic rhetoric, and the deadline is November 10 — that clock is ticking. The Middle East situation remains deeply unstable: Iran is warning of fierce retaliation to any US military action, the Houthis escalated dramatically over the weekend, and Trump's last-minute airstrike reversal suggests policy is reactive and unpredictable. Big Tech's AI off-balance sheet liabilities are up roughly $300 billion in the last 12 months alone, representing a growing and underappreciated risk for the sector.

Market Outlook

Modestly bullish lean for today's session, driven by AI/agentic enthusiasm around Meta Muse and falling oil prices on Middle East de-escalation hopes. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night and any diplomatic developments at the UN General Assembly — both could extend or reverse the current rally.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU names on the Meta Muse/agentic AI thesis. Energy is underperforming broadly as Brent crude drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi ceasefire optimism — names like COP, EOG, OXY, and XOM are all lower. Potash/materials names are getting hit hard on the Belarus supply deal headline. REITs are outperforming, likely helped by the modest drop in yields. Transports are lagging with FedEx, UPS, CSX, and rails all underperforming. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points.
