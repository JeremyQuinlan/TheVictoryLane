VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant catalyst in the market today, with reports of strong consumer demand for the agentic AI app driving outsized gains in the stock. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is generating significant anticipation and positioning. OpenAI is reportedly considering developing a competing product, validating the significance of the Muse launch.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is surging as agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, creating a direct demand tailwind for CPU makers. This is a sector reprice event for CPU-linked semiconductor names. AMD is among the top outperformers in the market today alongside ARM and Intel.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming broadly on the same agentic AI and CPU-intensity thesis driving AMD and ARM higher. The narrative that agentic workloads require substantially more CPU compute than prior AI generations is acting as a re-rating catalyst for the stock. This is a meaningful reversal of the typical AI trade that has favored GPUs.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious outlook that is weighing on the PC supply chain. This is a demand-side warning that is pressuring the stock and serving as a headwind for PC-adjacent names. The weakness stands in contrast to the broad tech rally.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with the settlement reportedly including preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The deal clears a major legal overhang that had created significant uncertainty around the transaction. Shares are jumping sharply on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the merger to proceed. The removal of this legal block is the primary catalyst for the move today. The deal structure reportedly includes certain content and editorial independence provisions to satisfy regulatory concerns.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for more concrete guidance. The lack of specificity overshadowed commentary about future blockbuster product launches. This is a sentiment and credibility setback for the stock.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are facing traffic headwinds, thanks to price promotions and package deals that are driving attendance. The Wall Street Journal highlighted Disney as a standout in an otherwise sluggish theme park environment. This is a positive read-through for Disney's most capital-intensive and high-margin segment.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article on Friday focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter, with efforts to offload loans tied to the facility having hit a wall. This raises concerns about the financial structure underlying Oracle's aggressive data center expansion. The story adds to growing scrutiny around Big Tech off-balance sheet AI infrastructure commitments.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at pricing substantially less than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct pricing and competitive threat to North American potash producers. The geopolitical backdrop — Belarus being under sanctions — adds complexity to the story.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with meaningful outperformance in tech and Nasdaq — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The dominant macro themes are AI optimism driven by Meta Muse momentum and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off Houthi airstrikes at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism heading into the UN General Assembly is cooling geopolitical risk sentiment, with hopes for potential US-Iran engagement and broader Middle East de-escalation.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on tone with a slight safety overlay from geopolitical uncertainty. The dollar context is secondary today to the oil and AI narratives. Separately, Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse-driven agentic AI narrative is pulling a wide swath of tech names higher and could represent a genuine mainstream inflection point for the technology, with CPU names like AMD, ARM, and Intel well-positioned to benefit. Oil dropping roughly 400 basis points is a broad economic positive, reducing input costs and easing inflation pressure. Diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly — including a potential Trump-Iran meeting — could further reduce geopolitical risk premium and support risk assets. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes a legal overhang and signals M&A activity remains open.

BEAR CASE

The US-China trade truce extension was not finalized during weekend talks, and the deadline is November 10, meaning uncertainty will linger and could resurface as a headwind. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech have surged roughly $300 billion in the last 12 months, a growing but underappreciated risk. Middle East tensions remain volatile — Iran warned of a fierce response to any resumed US military action, and the Houthi situation is still unresolved. The apartment debt refinancing wave of $1.8 trillion over the next decade, with $750 billion hitting in 2026 through 2028 at materially higher rates, is a slow-moving but significant financial stability risk.

Market Outlook

Mildly bullish lean for today's session, driven by the AI/agentic theme around Meta Muse and CPU-linked stocks, plus the meaningful tailwind from falling oil prices easing cost pressure across the economy. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which is setting up as a near-term event risk and potential continuation driver for the AI trade.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear winner today led by agentic AI optimism around Meta Muse, with CPU-linked semis AMD, ARM, and Intel among the top names. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on diplomatic de-escalation hopes and Trump calling off Houthi airstrikes. Potash and materials names are selling off hard on the Belarus supply deal headlines. REITs and data center infrastructure are outperforming. Transports are lagging despite the oil move, with rails and logistics names broadly lower. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up nearly 585 basis points.
