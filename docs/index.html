▲ CBRL Cracker Barrel Earnings beat with upside FY27 guidance

Cracker Barrel posted strong FQ4 upside across the board — sales came in at $849.3MM vs. the Street's $834MM, adjusted EPS of 99c crushed the 16c consensus, and EBITDA of $62.1MM topped the $41.5MM estimate. Tariff refunds provided a tailwind but weren't the whole story — EBITDA ex-refunds was still $53MM, well above consensus. Looking into fiscal 2027, the EBITDA guidance mid-point of $190MM beats the Street's $177MM.

◆ GIS General Mills Earnings beat with reaffirmed guidance, but structural headwinds persist

General Mills reported FQ1 results that were not as bad as feared — EPS of 75c (down 13% on a foreign exchange neutral basis) beat the 72c consensus, organic revenue was roughly flat vs. the Street's -1.8% estimate, and all four business segments outperformed on revenue. Gross margins fell 0.90% to 33.3% vs. the 33% estimate, and operating margins fell 1.30% to 14.4%, topping the 13.8% forecast; full-year guidance was reaffirmed. However, Adam notes sentiment toward food-focused staples will likely stay gloomy given fierce cyclical and secular headwinds despite the modestly better print.

◆ MANU Manchester United Earnings beat but FY27 guidance miss

Manchester United posted modest FQ4 upside on sales of GBP 157.5MM vs. the Street's GBP 145MM and EBITDA of GBP 28.9MM vs. the GBP 22MM estimate. However, the fiscal 2027 EBITDA guidance mid-point of GBP 215MM falls meaningfully short of the consensus at GBP 245MM. The below-consensus forward guide is a notable negative offset to the current quarter beat.

◆ MCD McDonald's Analyst Day with 2030 financial targets

McDonald's unveiled its 'McDonald's > NEXT' strategy at today's analyst meeting, headlined by an operating margin target of low-to-mid 50% by 2030 — solidly above the current consensus forecast of 49.5%. The company also plans to increase capital spending to drive growth, which is why free cash flow conversion is expected to shift lower to the mid-to-high 80% range by 2030, down from the prior 2023 goal of low-to-mid 90%. Adam's tone is skeptical of the strategic language but acknowledges the operating margin target is a clear positive relative to consensus.
