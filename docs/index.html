OpenAI's new Dots personal AI agent appears geared toward enterprise and professional users rather than everyday consumers, which Adam views as a relief for Meta. Meta's Muse product is explicitly consumer-oriented, meaning the two products may not be in direct head-to-head competition.

At Dev Day, OpenAI unveiled Dots (a personal AI agent for enterprise/professional users), GPT-5.1 Sol (near-Astra intelligence at one-fifth the cost), and Ultrafast (up to 8x faster token generation in Codex, up to 6x in the API). OpenAI's recaptured momentum since the Astra launch appears to be pressuring Anthropic's IPO timeline, which has been pushed out. Adam notes it is probably not a coincidence that the IPO delay coincides with OpenAI regaining market and mindshare over recent weeks.
