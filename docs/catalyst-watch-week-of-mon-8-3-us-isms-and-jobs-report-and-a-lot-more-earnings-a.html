MARKET OUTLOOK

The week's macro slate is heavy and the earnings calendar is relentless — this is a genuine information-dense stretch that could reset narratives fast. The jobs number (+88K consensus, up from +57K) is the obvious headline risk: a miss confirms softening labor, a beat keeps the Fed patient. ISM manufacturing and services are both expected to tick higher, which would be constructive but is largely priced in. The market's recent rotation away from pure AI momentum and toward cash-flow compounders like Spotify looks rational given stretched valuations in semis and software. Memory is the one AI-adjacent trade where the fundamental setup remains undeniably strong heading into SNDK and WDC results.

---

GEOPOLITICAL

- Geopolitical unrest is visibly weighing on travel demand at the margin — NCLH, CAR, and others have acknowledged the headwind explicitly, and it's a risk factor flagged for MAR, BKNG, ABNB, and DIS's Experiences segment this reporting season.

- Elevated energy prices remain a differential headwind: airlines and cruise lines carry direct oil exposure that large-cap hotel chains largely avoid, a distinction worth keeping in mind when comparing travel subsector setups.

- The PSKY-WBD merger timeline remains deeply uncertain as a coalition of states pursues a lawsuit; if and when the deal closes, the combined entity faces a punishingly high leverage ratio (Bloomberg, WSJ).

COMPANY NEWS

PLTR reports Monday night with the Street expecting sales +80% to $1.8B, operating margins ~60% (up more than 1,500 basis points year-over-year), and EPS +117% to $0.35. Bulls own the AI infrastructure narrative; bears are staring at a valuation that leaves zero margin for error.

AMD reports Tuesday night. Consensus: EPS +238% to $1.62, sales +47% to $11.29B with Data Center up 104% to $6.6B. The agentic AI CPU demand thesis is the key bull catalyst alongside a GPU TAM seen as large enough to support multiple winners alongside Nvidia.

LLY reports Wednesday morning. Street expects EPS of $6.01, sales +32% to $20.55B, Mounjaro +70% to $8.8B, Zepbound +36% to $4.61B. GLP-1 dominance is the story; the question is durability of the moat as competition intensifies.

CAT reports Tuesday morning with consensus EPS of $6.17 and MP&E revenue +17.2% to $18.36B. The data center/Power Generation re-rating is real but Power & Energy is still a modest slice of total revenue — rich valuation requires continued execution.

SNDK consensus: EPS ~$34.70, sales +353% to $8.62B with Datacenter revenue up 1,130% to $2.61B. WDC consensus: EPS +99.3% to $3.31, sales +41.6% to $3.69B. Memory demand from AI infrastructure plus tight supply = the setup looks as clean as it gets.

DIS reports Wednesday morning. Street models total revenue +7.5% to $25.4B, segment operating income +12.1% to $5.1B, EPS +15% to $1.85. Geopolitical/oil disruption in Experiences is the near-term swing factor; streaming profitability is the longer-term re-rating lever.

UBER reports Wednesday morning. Consensus: revenue +12.5% to $14.2B, bookings +22.2% to $57.1B, EBITDA +29% to $2.72B. Autonomous vehicle displacement risk to Mobility is the central bear case; the bull case rests on AV integration and Uber One membership growth.

TTWO reports Friday morning. Management guided F27 bookings to $8.0-8.2B but the Street is modeling $8.6B, reflecting genuine conviction around GTA launching November 19th.

FISV reports Thursday morning into a deeply depressed valuation. The key ask from investors is a reiteration of the 2027-2029 medium-term targets laid out at the May 14 analyst day — that alone could spark a meaningful relief rally.

HTZ reports Thursday morning post a negative Q2 preannouncement and dilutive capital raise. Street models Q2 EBITDA of just $67MM. Used car price commentary will be the critical variable.
