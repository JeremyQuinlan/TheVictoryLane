Macro · Rates

The newsletter is a thematic strategy piece on the AI sector rather than a broad daily market wrap. No specific index moves or overnight action are discussed. The focus is on structural capital market dynamics affecting AI spending.

No direct commentary on rates, Fed policy, yields, or the dollar is included in this edition.

Market Outlook

Adam's view is cautious-to-bearish on the broader AI sector in the near term. The key catalyst to watch is the ability of Anthropic and OpenAI to access equity and debt markets on terms that bulls expect — if those IPO timelines or valuations disappoint, the ripple effects across AI spending and valuations could be significant.

Sector Watch

AI and technology infrastructure are under structural pressure as the capital markets funding the sector become less accommodating. The core problem is that capex dramatically outpaces operating cash flow, making the entire AI build-out dependent on continued investor largess. Compute capacity is technically fungible but repurposing it away from anchor customers like Anthropic and OpenAI would be a slow and painful process. The biggest, best-capitalized players are likely to consolidate share while smaller or loss-making vendors face existential funding risk.

VK · Key Names
▼ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic is described as one of the two 'suns around which the whole AI world rotates.' If Anthropic cannot IPO on the timing and valuations anticipated by bulls, there will be negative fallout for the entire sector — less capital available for compute spending and a lower benchmark for AI valuations broadly.

▼ OPENAI OpenAI Other

OpenAI is the other central pillar of the AI ecosystem. Its ability to access public markets on favorable terms is critical; failure to do so would reduce compute spending and reprice sector valuations downward. A large portion of token consumption is driven by loss-making companies funded in part through OpenAI's ecosystem.

◆ META Meta Platforms Massive Capex

Meta tweaked up its 2026 capex budget and hinted at another large number in 2027, signaling aggressive investment to capitalize on weaker competitors being squeezed out. This positions Meta as one of the few players with balance sheet strength to crowd out underfunded AI vendors.

◆ SPACEX SpaceX Massive Capex

Elon Musk discussed bringing over 10 gigawatts of compute online next year for SpaceX, representing a massive acceleration of AI infrastructure build-out. SpaceX is using the current funding turmoil to press its advantage against weaker players, though the capital intensity of this strategy carries its own risk.
