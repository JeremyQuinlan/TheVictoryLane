Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 11:31 AM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant catalyst of the day, with reports of strong consumer demand for the app driving AI stocks broadly higher. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is a major anticipated event. The buzz is triggering competitive responses — OpenAI is reportedly considering launching a Meta Muse-like agentic product.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is among the top outperformers Monday on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, directly benefiting CPU-linked chipmakers. The Meta Muse demand narrative is acting as a sector-wide re-rating event for CPU exposure.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis, with the market pricing in that a mainstream consumer AI agent wave would meaningfully increase compute demand for CPU-intensive workloads. Intel is one of the named outperformers in today's tech surge.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after the company indicated it anticipates PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, providing a cautious demand outlook for the PC market. This is weighing on the broader PC supply chain even as AI hardware names surge.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, paving the way for the deal to proceed. Reports indicate Paramount may agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central as part of the settlement.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are jumping alongside Paramount on news that the state AG lawsuit blocking their merger has been settled, clearing a major regulatory hurdle for the deal. Both stocks are among the top outperformers in the media space today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were expecting more concrete guidance. The company did tout future blockbuster launches but lacked the specificity the market wanted.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers, many of whom are experiencing traffic headwinds, by leaning on price promotions and package deals to drive attendance. The Wall Street Journal highlighted the strategy as differentiating Disney from the broader theme park industry slump.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday, focusing on poor price action in debt tied to its massive data center project in New Mexico known as Project Jupiter. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising questions about execution on its aggressive infrastructure buildout.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries and other potash-linked names — Mosaic and Nutrien — all slumped after Trump said the US was nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus with pricing substantially below what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. The news is a direct pricing headwind for North American potash producers.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher on Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The two dominant themes are AI optimism driven by Meta Muse demand and anticipation of Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night, and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off planned airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday. Diplomatic optimism is running high ahead of the UN General Assembly in New York, with hopes that Trump could meet with Iran's president to cool Middle East tensions.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve. The Treasury is planning to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly relies on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the Treasury's target of roughly 20%. No direct Fed commentary in today's update.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse demand signal is igniting the entire AI ecosystem, with CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel seen as direct beneficiaries as agentic AI proves far more compute-intensive than prior generations. The sharp drop in oil prices — down roughly 400 basis points to below $100 on Brent — is a tailwind for consumers and risk sentiment broadly. Diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly, including potential US-Iran dialogue and a possible Trump-Xi summit with LNG tariff concessions, is reducing geopolitical risk premium across markets. Treasuries are catching a bid alongside equities, suggesting this is a genuine risk-on move rather than a rotation.

BEAR CASE

The rally is heavily concentrated in mega-cap tech and AI names, with equal-weight S&P up only 30 basis points versus the Nasdaq's 153 basis points — breadth is narrow and a handful of names are doing the heavy lifting. The US-China trade truce was not extended during weekend talks despite optimistic rhetoric, and it expires November 10th, leaving a live uncertainty overhang. Middle East risk has not been resolved — Trump merely postponed airstrikes, the Houthis dramatically escalated attacks on Saudi Arabia over the weekend, and Iran is warning of fierce retaliation if military action resumes. OpenAI's projected negative free cash flow of roughly $280 billion from 2026 to 2030 highlights the massive capital destruction still embedded in AI infrastructure buildout.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone is cautiously bullish for today's session, with the AI/Meta Muse theme and oil price decline acting as the twin catalysts driving risk appetite. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's Connect Conference keynote Wednesday night and any diplomatic developments at the UN General Assembly, particularly around US-Iran and US-China trade truce extension talks.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU-linked names on the Meta Muse agentic AI thesis. Energy is underperforming sharply as Brent crude drops roughly 400 basis points below $100 on Houthi airstrike cancellation and diplomatic optimism. Potash/materials names are getting hit on the Belarus supply deal news. REITs and data center industrials are catching a bid alongside the AI theme. Transports are lagging. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points. Travel and leisure names are broadly higher in the risk-on tape.
