BULL CASE

BEAR CASE

This signals the market is growing more skeptical of data center boom valuations despite the surface-level AI stock rally. Anthropic has also pushed its IPO target from October to November, adding to concerns that the window for AI public offerings may be narrowing.

Market Outlook

The key catalyst to watch is whether AI sentiment can hold despite cooling IPO demand and the broader question around Anthropic's IPO timeline.

AI infrastructure and CPU-linked semiconductors are the clear outperformers today on the back of Meta Muse demand. However, the AI IPO pipeline is showing stress — two AI-linked IPOs (SB Energy and Holtec) have been delayed in the past week, suggesting valuation skepticism is rising in private and pre-IPO AI names even as public AI stocks rally.
