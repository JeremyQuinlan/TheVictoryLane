VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — 145 basis points of gross margin tailwind came from tariff refunds and 105 basis points from a non-cash LIFO benefit. Domestic comps of plus 1.6% missed the Street's plus 3.1% estimate, and operating expenses rose 100 basis points year-over-year to 33.4% of sales. Management noted positive comp linearity in the quarter, which is providing some comfort to the stock.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, and importantly the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind, adjusted EPS was 42 cents, still well ahead of consensus. Guidance was mixed with the fiscal 2027 sales range trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management cited a difficult macro landscape, which adds a cautious read-through to the broader RV and consumer discretionary space.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at the Meta Connect conference Wednesday night at 7pm Eastern, and Shopify has agreed to allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform. Amazon has blocked Muse from its storefront, creating a competitive divide in agentic AI commerce. Investor expectations heading into the keynote are growing increasingly bullish.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful commercial integration that positions Shopify as a key beneficiary of the agentic AI commerce wave. This stands in contrast to Amazon blocking Muse, potentially giving Shopify a competitive advantage in capturing AI-driven transaction volume.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services, with the reduction coming in larger than the market had anticipated. The negative rate revision is a direct headwind to lab testing revenue and is also pressuring peer LabCorp simultaneously.

▼ LH Labcorp Other

Labcorp is under pressure alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS Medicare lab service rate cuts that were deeper than expected. Both names are being hit as the new payment rates represent a meaningful and unanticipated headwind to earnings estimates going forward.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced an acquisition of RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million contingent payment. The deal positions Cognex more aggressively in the Physical AI and robotics perception space.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in trial participants. This is a strong data readout that reinforces Viking's competitive position in the increasingly crowded but high-value obesity drug market.

▲ BABA Alibaba Product Launch

Alibaba unveiled what it is calling China's most powerful AI chip and hinted at plans to train a model with up to 10 trillion parameters, while management discussed plans to operate more than 20 gigawatts of data center capacity by 2032. The announcements signal aggressive AI infrastructure ambitions and are a meaningful escalation in China's domestic AI chip development narrative.
