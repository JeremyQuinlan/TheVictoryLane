VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is generating strong early demand signals and is one of the top-performing stocks in the market Monday. CEO Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is being heavily anticipated, driving AI enthusiasm across the sector. OpenAI is reportedly considering a Meta Muse-like product, validating the agentic AI consumer opportunity Meta is pioneering.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is rallying sharply on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. This is seen as a direct structural tailwind for CPU-linked chipmakers. AMD is explicitly named as one of the key beneficiaries of the Muse-driven AI narrative today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming Monday alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis tied to Meta Muse momentum. The stock is among the named outperformers in tech today. This represents a sector-level re-rating of CPU demand rather than a company-specific catalyst.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, a cautious demand outlook that is weighing on the stock. This is a negative read-through for the broader PC supply chain. The weakness contrasts sharply with the AI-driven rally elsewhere in tech.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, clearing a major legal hurdle. Reports indicate Paramount may agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Both Paramount and WBD shares are jumping sharply on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery is rallying alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears the path for the Paramount merger to proceed. The deal resolution removes the most significant regulatory overhang that had been hanging over both companies. This is a meaningful positive catalyst for the combined entity.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were expecting clearer guidance. The lack of specificity at a capital markets event is typically read as a bearish signal. This adds to existing pressure on the stock.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to its massive data center in New Mexico — efforts to offload loans tied to the 'Project Jupiter' facility have reportedly 'hit a wall.' This raises questions about the underwriting assumptions and demand for Oracle's hyperscale infrastructure ambitions. The story adds credit market scrutiny to an already complex data center financing story.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, driven by price promotions and package deals according to a Wall Street Journal report. The company appears to be successfully using strategic discounting to defend volume while competitors struggle. This is a positive read for Disney's parks segment relative to the industry.

▼ MOS Mosaic Other

Mosaic and potash peers CF Industries and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a 'massive' supply deal with Belarus, with pricing 'substantially less' than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers. The news represents a significant demand and margin headwind for the sector.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with the Nasdaq leading sharply — SPX up 94 basis points, Nasdaq up 153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just 30 basis points. The two dominant macro themes are diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly (Trump potentially meeting Iran's president, hopes for Middle East de-escalation) and Trump calling off Houthi airstrikes at the last minute Sunday night. Oil is slumping roughly 400 basis points below $100 on that geopolitical relief, while Bitcoin surges 585 basis points.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on tone without a dramatic flight-to-quality bid. Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly leans on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the roughly 20% target. No Fed commentary is a driver today — geopolitics and AI themes are dominating price action.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse optimism is igniting the entire AI and CPU-linked ecosystem — AMD, ARM, and Intel are all rallying on the thesis that agentic AI is far more CPU-intensive than prior generations. Oil's sharp drop below $100 relieves cost pressure across the economy and boosts travel, consumer, and industrial names. Treasuries rallying slightly alongside equities suggests the move is broadly credible and not purely speculative froth. The Paramount-Warner merger clearing state AG hurdles removes a meaningful legal overhang for the media sector.

BEAR CASE

The US and China failed to extend their trade truce at Sunday's talks despite optimistic rhetoric, and the November 10 expiration date will come into sharper focus as weeks pass. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech are up roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, a rising risk that the market is largely ignoring. The Nasdaq's massive outperformance versus the equal-weight S&P — 153 basis points versus 30 basis points — signals a narrow, momentum-driven rally rather than broad participation. Apartment owners face $1.8 trillion in debt refinancing over the next decade at much higher rates, a slow-moving stress point for real estate credit.

Market Outlook

Bullish lean for today's session, driven by two powerful catalysts: the Meta Muse-driven AI frenzy lifting the entire CPU and agentic AI ecosystem, and oil's steep decline on Middle East diplomatic hopes. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which is already front-running into today's tape.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer led by AI and CPU-linked names on Meta Muse agentic AI enthusiasm — the move is broad across semis, software, and cloud names. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil falls roughly 400 basis points below $100 on Middle East diplomatic hopes and the Trump Houthi airstrike reversal. Potash and materials are getting hit hard on Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. REITs and data center-linked industrials are rallying. Travel and leisure names are catching a bid, likely benefiting from lower oil. Transports are lagging despite lower fuel costs — possibly on demand concerns. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with Bitcoin up 585 basis points.
