VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the dominant catalyst of the session, with reports of strong consumer demand for the agentic AI app driving the stock to one of the top performers in the entire market. Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is the next major event risk. OpenAI is reportedly considering a competing agentic product, validating the significance of the category Meta is pioneering.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

CPU-linked stocks are outperforming sharply on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring into the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, making it structurally bullish for CPU suppliers. AMD is among the top performers in tech today alongside ARM and Intel. This is a sector re-rating event tied to the agentic AI adoption narrative.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is outperforming alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand thesis — the view that agentic workloads are far more CPU-intensive creates a demand tailwind for Intel's core business. This is a sentiment and narrative-driven move rather than company-specific news. Represents a meaningful re-rating opportunity if the agentic AI adoption curve accelerates.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle lawsuits that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, with reports confirming a deal was reached. Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central as part of the settlement. Both Paramount and WBD shares are jumping on the news.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are surging alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears a key regulatory hurdle for the pending merger. The deal removes one of the most significant legal overhangs that had clouded the transaction's timeline and probability. Investors are pricing in meaningfully higher deal certainty.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after guiding for PC unit volumes to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, delivering a cautious outlook that is weighing on the PC supply chain. This is a meaningful negative datapoint for PC-exposed names and contradicts any thesis of a near-term PC replacement cycle recovery. The stock is one of the session's notable underperformers.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for greater clarity on its growth roadmap. The lack of specificity is being read as a lack of confidence in near-term financial visibility. This is a significant negative for a stock that has already faced sentiment headwinds.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday highlighting poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload the loans having reportedly hit a wall. This raises concerns about the quality and bankability of Oracle's data center pipeline, which has been a key bull thesis for the stock. A negative read for sentiment heading into the week.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming its peers despite broader industry traffic headwinds, driven by strategic price promotions and package deals according to a Wall Street Journal report. This is a positive differentiation story as competitors struggle with softening consumer demand. Suggests Disney's parks management is executing well in a challenging leisure spending environment.

▼ MOS Mosaic Company Other

Potash names including Mosaic, CF Industries, and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus with pricing substantially less than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct pricing threat to North American potash producers and represents a bearish structural shift if the deal closes. The entire potash complex is under pressure.

Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, while the equal-weight S&P lags at just +30 basis points. The dominant macro catalysts are AI optimism driven by Meta Muse demand and a steep drop in oil prices after Trump called off Houthi airstrikes at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is running high ahead of the UN General Assembly, with hopes for Middle East de-escalation including a potential Trump-Iran meeting.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on tone with a slight safe-haven overlay given ongoing geopolitical uncertainty. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent increasingly relies on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the stated 20% target. No major Fed commentary is driving markets today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta Muse demand signals are strong and the agentic AI narrative is gaining mainstream traction, lifting the entire AI ecosystem including CPU names like AMD, ARM, and Intel. Oil's roughly 400 basis point drop eases inflation concerns and benefits consumer-facing sectors. Diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly could further reduce geopolitical risk premiums. The Paramount-Warner deal clearing state AG hurdles removes regulatory overhang and reflects a constructive M&A environment.

BEAR CASE

Market breadth is strikingly narrow — the equal-weight S&P is only up 30 basis points versus Nasdaq's 153 basis points, suggesting this rally is concentrated and fragile. The US-China trade truce extension was not finalized during Sunday's talks, leaving a November 10 cliff unresolved. Geopolitical risks remain acute — the Houthi situation is escalating, Iran is warning of fierce retaliation, and oil tanker costs are surging. Apartment owners face a roughly $1.8 trillion debt refinancing wave at significantly higher rates, a slow-moving credit stress that markets are not pricing.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautiously bullish on a narrow, tech-led basis — the AI theme is clearly in the driver's seat and Meta Muse is the ignition. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night, which could extend or fade the AI rally. Breadth is limited — equal-weight underperformance signals this is a momentum/megacap trade, not a broad market advance.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear leader with AI and CPU names surging on the agentic AI/Meta Muse catalyst. Energy is underperforming sharply as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi airstrike cancellation and diplomatic optimism. Potash and materials are under pressure on the Belarus supply deal headline. REITs and data center industrials are outperforming, benefiting from lower yields and AI infrastructure demand. Transports are lagging. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up roughly 585 basis points.
