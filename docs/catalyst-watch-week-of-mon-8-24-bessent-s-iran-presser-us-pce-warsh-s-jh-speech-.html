RATES & FED

The key rates event this week is Fed Chair-designate Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech Friday 8/28 at 10am ET — markets will be parsing every word for signals on the policy path. US PCE for July prints Wednesday 8/26, with the Street modeling headline down 10 basis points to +3.6% while core holds steady at +3.3%. Those two prints together will do a lot of heavy lifting for near-term rate expectations.

---

MARKET OUTLOOK

This is a week where macro and micro collide hard — NVDA alone could set the tone for the entire risk complex, and with PCE and Warsh's JH remarks sandwiching the print, volatility is virtually guaranteed. The AI capex narrative remains the market's load-bearing wall; if NVDA guides cleanly through and Warsh sounds even modestly dovish, the path of least resistance is higher. That said, the consumer-facing earnings slate (DG, DLTR, GAP, ULTA, KSS) will remind investors that the macro backdrop for the average American remains genuinely mixed-to-poor. Don't get lulled into complacency — this week has the ingredients for a sharp move in either direction.

GEOPOLITICAL

- Treasury Secretary Bessent told CNBC (Thursday 8/20) that a press conference is scheduled for Monday 8/24 to unveil additional financial actions against Iran — watch for potential energy market reaction depending on the severity of the measures.

COMPANY NEWS

NVDA (Nvidia, reports Wed 8/26 after close): The Street is looking for total revenue +96.5% to $91.85B, with Data Center +107.6% to $85.3B (Hyperscale ~$43.5B, ACIE ~$41.75B), GMs of 75%, op. margins of 66.3%, and EPS of $2.08. Notably, NVDA's guidance assumed zero China contribution, but H200 shipments to the mainland apparently occurred — upside risk there. The company recently announced a $500B+ arrangement with major asset managers to bring in third parties to shoulder co-investment burden, a smart move as balance-sheet aggression grows but scale remains manageable.

MRVL (Marvell, reports Thurs 8/27 after close): Sentiment is very bullish. Street models revenue +35% to $2.7B, Data Center +44% to $2.145B, GMs 58.8%, and EPS +39.3% to 93c. The expanded Google partnership is a critical endorsement of Marvell's silicon IP, and many expect FY27 guidance of +40%/$11.5B to be nudged higher.

CRM (Salesforce, reports Wed 8/26 after close): Street models FXN sales growth of 10.6%, cRPO growth 12.25% FXN, ~$1.48B in AgentForce ARR, op. margins 33.6%, and EPS of $3.28. Stock has rallied off June lows as AI disruption fears moderate slightly and capital return remains healthy.

CRWD (CrowdStrike, reports Wed 8/26 after close): Net new ARR of ~$286MM, total ARR ~$5.79B, op. margins 24.2%, EPS 28c. Security software is one of the more favored corners of the software universe right now.

INTU (Intuit, reports Tues 8/25 after close): Street expects sales +11.5% to $4.273B and EPS +30% to $3.57. AI disruption fears loom large, but a 17% headcount cut, cheap valuation, and large buyback offer a floor. Analyst day September 17.

WDAY (Workday, reports Thurs 8/27 after close): Revenue +12.3% to $2.636B, sub revenue +13.2%, op. margins ~30%, EPS $2.61. Stock has spiked recently on a Reuters report of a potential Silver Lake takeout bid.

DG/DLTR (Dollar General/Dollar Tree, both report Thurs 8/27 before open): DG: EPS $2.01, revenue +4.3% to $11.187B, comps +2.6%, GMs +35 basis points. DLTR: EPS $1.13, revenue +6.5% to $4.86B, comps +3%, GMs +60 basis points. Both carry tariff refund tailwinds, though investors are treating that as a one-time item.

AFRM (Affirm, reports Thurs 8/27 after close): GMV +29.5% to $13.405B, revenue ex-transaction costs +29% to $548.1MM, adjusted op. income +37.2% to $325MM. Sentiment is more constructive on AFRM than peer Klarna, which recently trimmed top-line guidance.

ANF (Abercrombie, reports Wed 8/26 before open): Overall comps +0.6%, Abercrombie brand +1.8%, Hollister -0.15%, op. margins 10.17%, EPS $1.96. Hollister remains the swing factor.

ZM (Zoom, reports Tues 8/25 after close): Revenue +4.2% to $1.268B, op. margins 40.4%, EPS $1.48. The Anthropic stake could be worth several billion — an underappreciated asset alongside a $7.7B net cash position.
