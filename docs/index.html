VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is showing strong early demand signals and is the primary catalyst driving today's AI rally. CEO Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night is creating additional anticipation. OpenAI is reportedly rushing to develop a competing agentic consumer product, validating Meta's first-mover positioning.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

CPU-linked stocks are outperforming on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help bring to the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations. AMD is cited as a direct beneficiary of this compute demand shift. The Meta Muse narrative is acting as a sector-wide re-rating catalyst for CPU names.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is rallying alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU demand theme. The argument is that mainstream adoption of agentic AI workloads would be a structural tailwind for CPU volumes. This is a sentiment-driven re-rating rather than company-specific news.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. guided that PC unit volumes are expected to fall approximately 5% in calendar year 2027, providing a cautious outlook that is weighing on the stock today. This is a headwind for the broader PC supply chain at a time when the rest of tech is rallying. The negative PC volume outlook contrasts sharply with AI-driven enthusiasm elsewhere in the sector.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle a lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The settlement reportedly includes preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. Shares are jumping sharply on the news as a major regulatory overhang is cleared.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are jumping alongside Paramount after the attorney general settlement clears a key legal barrier to the merger proceeding. The deal structure reportedly involves Paramount preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting select cable assets. This resolves one of the most significant regulatory risks hanging over the transaction.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company's analyst meeting failed to deliver the granular financial targets investors were hoping for. The lack of specificity on forward guidance disappointed the market despite the company touting future blockbuster drug launches. The stock is underperforming peers in the healthcare space.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming industry peers who are facing traffic headwinds, driven by strategic price promotions and package deals. The Wall Street Journal highlighted Disney as defying an industry-wide slump in theme park attendance. This is a positive data point for Disney's consumer-facing business heading into the fall season.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times report on Friday focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility have reportedly hit a wall. This raises questions about the health of Oracle's data center financing pipeline.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at prices substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. If true, the deal would be a significant pricing headwind for North American potash producers. The group is among the worst performers in the market today.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with significant Nasdaq outperformance — SPX +94 basis points, Nasdaq +153 basis points, equal-weight S&P only +30 basis points. The dominant macro driver is a steep drop in oil prices, down roughly 400 basis points to below $100 Brent, after Trump called off airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night. Diplomatic optimism is running high ahead of the UN General Assembly this week, with hopes that meetings — including a potential Trump-Iran summit — could cool Middle East tensions.

Treasuries have a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve. The Treasury is flagging a plan to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months, pushing bill supply above 24% of marketable debt and above the Treasury's own 20% target. No major Fed commentary is driving today's session.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

AI stocks are surging on Meta Muse demand signals and the upcoming Zuckerberg keynote, with OpenAI reportedly rushing to develop a competing agentic product — validating the theme broadly. CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel are getting an additional boost as agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior generations. The oil drop is a macro tailwind for consumer and transportation names, and diplomatic progress at the UN could provide a sustained risk-on catalyst. Treasuries catching a bid alongside equities suggests a relatively clean risk environment.

BEAR CASE

The Nasdaq's outperformance versus the equal-weight S&P — 153 basis points versus 30 basis points — signals narrow, momentum-driven leadership rather than broad market health. Middle East tensions remain structurally elevated with Houthi attacks escalating against Saudi Arabia and Iran warning of fierce retaliation if the US resumes military action — one misstep ends the diplomatic optimism trade quickly. The US-China trade truce extension was not secured over the weekend and expires November 10, keeping that overhang alive. Big Tech's off-balance-sheet AI liability exposure has surged roughly $300 billion in the last 12 months, representing a growing and underappreciated risk.

Market Outlook

Mildly bullish leaning today, driven by two clear catalysts — AI enthusiasm around Meta Muse and the Connect Conference keynote Wednesday, and oil's sharp pullback on diplomatic optimism. The key catalyst to watch is any developments at the UN General Assembly and whether Trump meets with Iran's president, which could sustain or reverse the oil trade.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear outperformer, led by AI and CPU names on Meta Muse agentic AI optimism. Energy is the clear underperformer as Brent crude slides roughly 400 basis points on Trump's last-minute cancellation of Houthi airstrikes and UN diplomatic optimism — XOM, COP, DVN, EOG, OXY, and others all lower. Potash and materials are under pressure on Trump's Belarus supply deal commentary. REITs and data-center linked industrials are outperforming, while transports, insurance, and payments lag. Crypto-linked stocks are surging with bitcoin up 585 basis points.
