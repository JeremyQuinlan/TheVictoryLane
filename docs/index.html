VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street's $54.03, but the beat was low quality — 145 basis points of gross margin tailwind came from tariff refunds and another 105 basis points from a non-cash LIFO impact. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive comp linearity in the quarter. The stock is rallying primarily on the linearity commentary despite the soft headline comp.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll reported fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street's 36 cents, a solid beat that holds up even excluding tariff refunds — ex-refunds EPS was 42 cents, still well above consensus. Guidance is mixed: fiscal 2027 sales range was trimmed but EPS outlook was left unchanged. The quality of the beat is better than AZO's given the earnings power persists ex-tariff benefit.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street's 87 cents, as gross margin weakness — down 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85% — more than offset slightly better-than-expected revenue of $2.311 billion. Management explicitly called out the difficult macro landscape as a headwind. The miss reinforces concerns about softness in the recreational vehicle market.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect Wednesday night at 7pm ET. Shopify will allow Muse to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, a meaningful distribution win — notably Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform. OpenAI is also reportedly considering a competing agentic product, validating the strategic importance of the category.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify announced it will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, positioning Shopify as a key agentic commerce partner. This is a notable win given Amazon has blocked Muse, potentially redirecting AI-driven purchase volume toward Shopify merchants. The partnership could be a meaningful traffic and GMV driver if agentic AI adoption accelerates.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking reported positive clinical trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, showing 22% weight loss in the trial. This is a significant data readout that reinforces VK2735's competitive positioning in the obesity drug market. The result adds credibility to Viking as a standalone GLP-1 contender against larger incumbents.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday after CMS released new Medicare payment rates for lab services that represented a larger reduction than the market had anticipated. The rate cuts are a direct hit to revenue for lab testing companies with significant Medicare exposure. Both Quest and LabCorp are under pressure on the news.

▼ LH Labcorp Other

LabCorp is selling off alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS Medicare lab service rate cuts that came in worse than expected. The cuts represent a meaningful headwind to the lab testing sector's revenue outlook and are weighing on both names today.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's exposure to robotics and AI-driven industrial vision — a strategically important and fast-growing segment. The acquisition size is meaningful relative to Cognex's scale.

▲ CPRI Capri Holdings M&A

A WWD report is speculating that Capri Holdings — parent of Michael Kors, Jimmy Choo, and Versace — could be up for sale. No formal process has been confirmed, but the report is circulating and drawing attention to potential strategic options for the company. Capri has been under pressure as its brands have struggled with slowing luxury demand.
