Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta's Muse is generating genuine consumer demand and pulling forward a new wave of agentic AI infrastructure spending. CPU-linked stocks like AMD, ARM, and Intel are benefiting as agentic AI requires significantly more CPU compute than prior GPU-heavy iterations. If Muse adoption data continues to impress, it could re-anchor bullish sentiment across the AI complex.

BEAR CASE

SB Energy's IPO delay — citing investor pushback on its $50 billion-plus valuation and cooling sentiment on the data center boom — is the second AI-linked IPO pulled in a single week, following Holtec. This back-to-back failure in the IPO market suggests institutional investors are becoming more skeptical of AI infrastructure growth stories. Anthropic's IPO timeline also slipping from October to November adds to the sense that the market's appetite for new AI paper is waning.

Market Outlook

Mixed lean. The Muse-driven AI rally is real but fragile — cooling IPO sentiment and back-to-back AI IPO delays signal the market is growing more discriminating about AI valuations. The key catalyst to watch is whether the IPO pipeline pressure spreads into publicly traded AI infrastructure names.

Sector Watch

AI infrastructure and semiconductors are bifurcating — CPU-linked names (AMD, ARM, INTC) are outperforming on the agentic AI demand thesis, while the broader data center boom narrative faces pressure from back-to-back AI IPO delays (SB Energy and Holtec), suggesting institutional sentiment on the sector is fragmenting.
