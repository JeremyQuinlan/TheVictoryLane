Morning Game Plan
Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 11:41 AM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta Muse is the central catalyst for Monday's session, with reports of strong consumer demand for the agentic AI app driving the stock to one of the top positions in the entire market. Anticipation is building for CEO Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference on Wednesday night. OpenAI is reportedly scrambling to develop a competing product, validating the significance of the Muse launch.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD is outperforming sharply on the thesis that agentic AI — which Meta Muse could help push into the mainstream — is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, making CPU suppliers direct beneficiaries. This represents a sector reprice of the CPU opportunity within the AI infrastructure buildout. The stock is among the top performers in the tech complex today.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel is catching a strong bid alongside AMD and ARM on the agentic AI CPU-intensity thesis tied to Meta Muse. The market is repricing the CPU-linked opportunity as agentic workloads are seen as far more demanding on traditional processors than prior generative AI applications. This is a meaningful narrative shift for a stock that has faced persistent headwinds.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. is sliding after issuing a cautious outlook, saying it anticipates PC unit volumes will fall approximately 5% in calendar 2027. This is a notable demand warning for the consumer PC market and is weighing on the broader PC-linked group. The guidance cut stands in contrast to the AI-driven enthusiasm lifting the rest of tech.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount has struck a deal with state attorneys general to settle the lawsuit that threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, sending shares jumping. The settlement is reported to include preserving CNN's editorial independence and potentially divesting certain cable channels including Comedy Central. The merger pathway is now significantly clearer.

▲ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Warner Bros. Discovery shares are jumping alongside Paramount after the state AG settlement clears a major legal obstacle to the two companies' merger. The deal had faced serious regulatory resistance and the resolution materially de-risks the transaction timeline. Both stocks are among the top performers in the media group today.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares are slumping in European trading after the company failed to provide sufficiently granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were looking for more specific guidance. The lack of detail is being read as a negative signal about near-term earnings visibility. The stock is underperforming the broader European market.

▲ DIS Disney Other

Disney's parks business is outperforming peers who are seeing traffic headwinds, with the company leveraging price promotions and package deals to drive attendance. The Wall Street Journal highlighted Disney as a rare bright spot in a challenged theme park industry. This is a positive data point for the parks segment ahead of the next earnings report.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday, focusing on poor price action in debt tied to a massive Oracle-linked data center in New Mexico, with efforts to offload loans tied to the so-called Project Jupiter facility having hit a wall. This raises questions about the credit quality and viability of Oracle's aggressive data center expansion strategy. The story adds an incremental overhang to the stock.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries along with Mosaic and Nutrien are all slumping after Trump said the US is nearing a massive supply deal with Belarus for potash at pricing substantially less than what is currently being paid for Canadian potash. This is a direct competitive threat to North American potash pricing and volumes. The entire potash complex is under pressure on the headline.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks are broadly higher Monday with notable Nasdaq outperformance, up 153 basis points versus the equal-weight S&P gaining just 30 basis points, as AI enthusiasm dominates the tape. Oil is slumping roughly 400 basis points to under $100 per barrel after Trump called off planned airstrikes against the Houthis at the last minute Sunday night, sparking diplomatic optimism ahead of the UN General Assembly. Bitcoin is surging nearly 585 basis points while gold slips and Treasuries catch a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve.

Treasuries are seeing a modest bid with yields down 1 to 3 basis points across the curve, reflecting the risk-on but not reckless tone in markets. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt to fund the government, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt and above the Treasury's own 20% target. No major Fed commentary is in focus today.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

The Meta Muse agentic AI catalyst is broad and powerful, lifting not just mega-cap tech but CPU-linked names like AMD, ARM, and Intel on the thesis that agentic AI is far more compute-intensive than prior iterations. Diplomatic optimism around the UN General Assembly — including a potential Trump-Iran meeting — is pulling oil sharply lower, removing a major macro headwind and supporting consumer and travel names. The Paramount-Warner merger clearing state AG hurdles removes an overhang and signals M&A conditions remain favorable. Crypto, asset managers, REITs, and data center industrials are all participating, suggesting broad market breadth underneath the Nasdaq leadership.

BEAR CASE

The US-China trade truce was not extended at the weekend talks despite optimistic rhetoric, leaving the November 10 expiration date as a live risk event that could rattle markets. AI off-balance sheet liabilities for Big Tech have risen roughly $300 billion in just the last 12 months, a growing but underappreciated risk. HP Inc.'s warning of a roughly 5% decline in PC unit volumes in calendar 2027 signals consumer tech demand may be softening even as AI capex booms. The potash deal with Belarus and broad energy weakness are pressuring materials and energy names, and transport underperformance in rails and logistics may be sending a cautious signal on the economy.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is bullish on the session, driven by two powerful concurrent themes: the Meta Muse-led AI agentic wave lifting the entire tech complex, and the geopolitical de-escalation trade pushing oil lower and lifting risk sentiment. The key catalyst to watch is Zuckerberg's keynote at the Connect Conference Wednesday night and any progress on US-Iran diplomacy at the UN General Assembly.

Sector Watch

Tech is the clear leader Monday with AI and CPU-linked names surging on the Meta Muse agentic AI theme. Energy is one of the worst performers as oil drops roughly 400 basis points on Houthi de-escalation hopes. Potash and materials are getting hit hard on the Belarus supply deal headlines. REITs and data center industrials are outperforming. Transports are lagging. Crypto-linked stocks are among the top gainers as bitcoin surges nearly 585 basis points.
