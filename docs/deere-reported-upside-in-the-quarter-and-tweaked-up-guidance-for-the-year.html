COMPANY NEWS

DE (Deere & Company) delivered a clean beat in FQ3, with EPS of $5.10 versus the Street's $4.70 estimate, on revenue of $10.99B against expectations of $10.78B — the upside driven by margin outperformance across every segment plus better financial services income. Production & Precision Agriculture came in roughly inline on sales (-6% to $3.99B vs. Street $3.94B) but beat meaningfully on margins (13.2% vs. Street 12.6%). Small Ag & Turf also landed inline on sales (+12% to $3.38B vs. Street $3.36B) with a standout margin print of 18.4% versus the Street's 15.6%. Construction & Forestry was the strongest unit outright — sales up 18% to $3.61B (Street: $3.52B) with margins of 12.1% versus 11.5% expected. Full-year net income guidance was nudged higher to $4.75–5.0B from the prior $4.5–5.0B range. Importantly, management flagged improving order book trends and called 2026 the bottom for the ag equipment cycle — stable U.S. conditions doing the heavy lifting while Brazil and Europe remain soft headwinds.
