RBRK — Down despite a massive beat; Q2 revenue $427M vs. $296M est., FY revenue guide $1.685B–$1.693B vs. $1.281B est. The stock's elevated valuation appears to be the headwind even with blowout numbers.

ADSK — Slipping despite a Q2 beat (EPS $3.30 vs. $3.12 est., rev $2.05B vs. $2.01B). Q3 adj. EPS guidance $3.04–$3.09 came in below the $3.14 est., triggering the pullback.

PYPL — Dropping after Bloomberg reported that the Advent/Stripe consortium abandoned its $60.50/share takeover bid (~$53B valuation). PayPal's board had previously rejected the offer as inadequate, leaving the stock without a near-term catalyst.

S (SentinelOne) — Lower premarket. Q2 numbers were broadly in-line, but FY adj. EPS guidance of $0.30–$0.32 missed the $0.35 est. ARR at $1.22B is steady but investor expectations ran too hot.

SOLS — Surging after the $14.5B merger with ESI was mutually scrapped. Both boards concluded shareholders preferred independence; market is reading the deal break as a win for SOLS specifically.

NVDA — WSJ reports Nvidia paused parts of its financing program that provided credit support to AI cloud customers in exchange for revenue share. Internal concerns over antitrust exposure drove the move; worth monitoring for broader AI infrastructure implications.

PD (PagerDuty) — In-line quarter; Q2 EPS $0.32 vs. $0.31 est. Announced a ~15% headcount reduction with $5.5M–$7.5M in charges. FY guidance largely in-line with consensus.

GILD — FDA approved its once-daily oral HIV combination pill for adults with suppressed viral loads. Clean regulatory win, no major guidance revision attached.

LRCX — Board approved a dividend increase from $0.26 to $0.33/share per quarter, a 27% increase. Incremental positive for the semi equipment space.

RIVN — CFO Claire McDonough stepping down to relocate east. She will remain in role for two months during transition. Leadership uncertainty is a modest negative for a company still working to scale production.
