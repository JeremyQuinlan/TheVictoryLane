Monday, September 21, 2026  ·  Updated Sep 21, 2026 · 05:26 PM ET
VK · Earnings Whispers
▲ META Meta Platforms Product Launch

Meta's personal AI agent Muse has seen a surge in demand since launching earlier this month and was among the best performing stocks in the market Monday. The Meta Connect Conference kicks off this week with Zuckerberg's keynote Wednesday night at 7pm ET, where investors expect more bullish news on Muse and other AI products. OpenAI is reportedly considering launching a competing Muse-like product, validating the agentic AI opportunity.

▲ AMD Advanced Micro Devices Sector Reprice

AMD rallied Monday on the Muse-driven CPU optimism, as agentic AI is significantly more CPU-intensive than prior AI iterations, driving demand expectations higher. The stock was cited among the tech outperformers for the session.

▲ ARM Arm Holdings Sector Reprice

ARM shares outperformed Monday as Muse enthusiasm fueled CPU demand optimism across the semiconductor ecosystem. Agentic AI's greater compute intensity is seen as a structural tailwind for CPU-architecture companies like ARM.

▲ INTC Intel Sector Reprice

Intel was cited among the tech outperformers Monday, benefiting from the same Muse-driven CPU demand narrative that lifted AMD and ARM. The agentic AI adoption wave is seen as a meaningful demand driver for CPU suppliers broadly.

▼ HPQ HP Inc. Guidance Cut

HP Inc. was one of the weakest stocks in the S&P 500 Monday after the company said it anticipates PC unit volumes falling approximately 5% in calendar year 2027. The downbeat PC forecast weighed heavily on the stock amid an otherwise strong tech tape.

▲ PSKY Paramount Global M&A

Paramount struck a settlement deal with state attorneys general that had threatened to block its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery, and the transaction is now expected to close within two weeks. Terms include keeping operations in California, not selling studio lots for at least five years, releasing at least 30 movies annually in theaters, and preserving editorial independence at CNN and CBS.

▼ NVO Novo Nordisk Other

Novo Nordisk shares slumped in European trading after the company failed to provide more granular financial targets at its Monday analyst meeting, disappointing investors who were hoping for clearer guidance on its blockbuster pipeline. The lack of specificity was seen as a missed opportunity to restore investor confidence.

▼ CF CF Industries Other

CF Industries sank Monday after Trump announced the US is nearing a massive potash supply deal with Belarus at prices substantially below what is currently paid for Canadian potash. The deal directly threatens the pricing power of North American fertilizer producers.

▼ MOS Mosaic Company Other

Mosaic fell sharply alongside CF Industries and Nutrien after Trump's Belarus potash deal announcement, which would introduce cheap potash supply into the market and undercut North American fertilizer pricing. The announcement was a direct headwind to the entire fertilizer sector.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was flagged as a negative AI headline after reports that efforts to offload loans tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center facility in New Mexico have hit a wall, raising concerns about the financial health of its data center expansion. The debt stress adds to a broader set of negative AI infrastructure headlines circulating after Friday's close.

Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro · Rates

Stocks posted solid gains on Monday led by tech, with the S&P 500 up 149 basis points, the Nasdaq up 226 basis points, and the Russell 2000 up 52 basis points, though equal-weight S&P meaningfully lagged. The two main drivers were enthusiasm around Meta's Muse agentic AI product and hopes that the UN General Assembly in New York this week could cool Middle East tensions. Despite strong index-level performance, a notable number of individual stocks hit 52-week lows, underscoring the narrow leadership.

Treasuries were mixed with 2-year yields flat while 10 and 30-year yields fell 4 basis points. The Fed funds market is pricing roughly 33 basis points of rate hikes across the final two meetings of the year. Both Chicago Fed President Goolsbee and St. Louis Fed President Musalem delivered hawkish remarks, with Musalem explicitly warning that further monetary tightening will be needed given persistent demand and recurring supply shocks.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Meta's Muse is generating significant consumer excitement and could be the product that mainstreams agentic AI, lifting the entire CPU and AI infrastructure ecosystem. Oil prices could fall meaningfully if UN General Assembly diplomacy yields progress on Iran, which would simultaneously ease inflation, rates, and the Fed's hawkish posture. The Trump administration is clearly motivated to cool geopolitical tensions given the oil price pressure, and the White House is reportedly proposing a $5 billion fund to rebuild Middle East energy infrastructure. A string of major AI catalysts remain ahead — Meta Connect, OpenAI Dev Day, and Micron earnings — that could reignite AI enthusiasm.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical backdrop is deteriorating, with Houthi attacks on Saudi cities and Ukraine's largest ever drone strike on Russia raising energy supply risk across oil, refined products, and natural gas. Multiple Fed officials are signaling more rate hikes, and the structural forces pushing yields higher — sticky inflation, AI capex issuance, fiscal imbalances, tariffs — are not abating. Negative AI headlines are stacking up including the SB Energy IPO delay, Anthropic IPO pushout, Oracle data center debt stress, OpenAI's projected negative $280 billion free cash flow through 2030, and Texas Governor Abbott halting new data center permits. Breadth remains poor with a wide swath of consumer staples, insurance, energy, and transport names hitting 52-week lows.

Market Outlook

Adam's tone leans cautiously bullish near-term on AI/tech momentum but structurally concerned about macro headwinds. The key catalyst to watch is the UN General Assembly and Trump's Tuesday address, with the Zuckerberg Meta Connect keynote on Wednesday night as the most important AI event of the week.

Sector Watch

Tech and AI-linked names sharply outperformed led by the Muse/CPU narrative, with semiconductors, cloud, and digital REITs among the strongest pockets. Energy was a notable laggard as Brent fell over 350 basis points despite worsening geopolitical conditions, on hopes for UN General Assembly diplomacy. Fertilizers were hit hard by the Trump-Belarus potash deal. Consumer staples, insurance, managed care, transports, and cable/telecom all underperformed with many names hitting fresh 52-week lows, highlighting poor underlying breadth.
