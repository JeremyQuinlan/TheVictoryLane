COMPANY NEWS

META reported Q2 EPS of $6.18, missing the Street's $7.15 estimate by 13%, as operating margins collapsed to 31% versus the expected 35.6% — costs surged 55% to $42B, though that figure includes $2.4B in legal charges and $1.18B in severance. Strip those out and operating income of ~$22.35B would have modestly beaten consensus. Sales rose 28% to $60.8B (inline), driven by ad impressions +14% and price per ad +12%. DAP grew 3% to 3.6B. Capex came in at $31B, below the $33.1B forecast, and free cash flow beat at +$784MM vs. Street's -$1.2B. The Q3 revenue guide midpoint of $62.5B trails consensus at $63.1B, and the capex range was tweaked to $130-145B. Zuckerberg pushed back on selling compute to third parties, saying he won't sacrifice capacity for short-term gains — a disappointment for investors hoping for a new revenue stream.

MSFT (Microsoft) delivered FQ4 GAAP EPS of $4.81 vs. the Street's $4.25, on revenue of $90B (+18%) against an $87.7B estimate, with all three segments beating. Intelligent Cloud grew +31% FXN (Street: +28%), Azure accelerated to +43% FXN (Street: 39.6%), and Productivity rose +14% FXN. Operating margins came in ~30 basis points better at 45%. FQ1 guidance is bullish: revenue of $90-91B with Azure growth of 45% FXN (well above the Street's 40.6%) and margins implying ~49% vs. the Street's 47.6%. Management noted it's extending data center useful life from 15 to 25 years, affecting lease accounting and shifting some capex timing — but underlying capex plans for calendar 2026 are unchanged.

CMG (Chipotle) posted Q2 EPS of 33c vs. 32c expected, with comps of +2.2% topping the Street's +1.3%. Full-year comp guidance raised to +1-3% from flat.

CVNA (Carvana) reported Q2 EBITDA roughly inline, but EBITDA margins slumped 200 basis points year-over-year, and the full-year EBITDA guidance midpoint of $2.85B fell short of the Street's $2.98B.

ORLY (O'Reilly) beat on EPS (87c vs. 86c) and comps (+6% vs. +5.4%), raising full-year comp guidance to +4-6% and EPS to $3.20-3.30.

SBUX (Starbucks) crushed FQ3 estimates with EPS of 85c vs. 65c, global comps of +7.9% vs. +5.7% expected, and operating margins up 430 basis points. Full-year EPS guidance raised to $2.55-2.65 from $2.25-2.45.

PPC (Pilgrim's Pride) missed Q2 EBITDA at $360MM vs. $371MM, with margins down 660 basis points year-over-year.

HOOD (Robinhood) reported Q2 EPS of 62c vs. 44c, with transaction-based revenue of $776MM beating the $741MM estimate — options/contracts revenue surged more than 10x.

FICO beat and raised, now guiding to EPS of $42.43 and revenue of $2.53B vs. prior $40.45/$2.45B, though quarterly sales slightly missed on Scores segment pressure.

EG (Everest Group) posted EPS of $14.85 vs. $14.47, with a combined ratio of 90% (~80 basis points better than expected), though net written premiums fell 26.3% year-over-year.

ARM reported FQ1 EPS of 45c vs. 40c on sales of $1.29B (+22%), with FQ2 guidance of 47c/$1.38B above consensus. AI commentary remains bullish, though AGI CPU guidance was unchanged and management flagged supply constraints as a ceiling on near-term upside.

LRCX (Lam Research) beat FQ4 estimates ($1.82/$6.72B vs. $1.70/$6.68B) and guided FQ1 sharply higher at $2.15/$8.1B vs. the Street's $1.84/$7.13B — a standout print in semis.

QCOM (Qualcomm) reported FQ3 EPS of $2.21 (inline) on revenue of $9.94B vs. $9.62B, with auto sales surging +61%. But FQ4 EPS guidance midpoint of $2.15 missed the Street's $2.35, signaling further margin pressure ahead.

FTNT (Fortinet) beat Q2 EPS at 90c vs. 75c, with billings spiking 33% to $2.37B vs. $2.13B. Full-year billings guidance raised to $8.8-9.1B.

EQIX (Equinix) beat on adjusted FFO at $11.78 vs. $11.30 and raised full-year AFFO guidance to $42.69-43.29 per share.

ALGN (Align) posted modest EPS upside and reiterated full-year guidance. Separately, following "constructive discussions with Elliott," the company announced new board members and an increased 2026 buyback commitment.

LHX (L3Harris) beat Q2 EPS at $3.13 vs. $2.80, with segment margins up 10 basis points to 16% and a book-to-bill of 1.2x. Full-year EPS guidance raised to $11.80-12.00.

CHRW (CH Robinson) posted EPS of $1.61, with revenue up 19.3% and operating margins expanding 360 basis points to 34.7%.

FLS (Flowserve) beat at 95c vs. 86c with bookings surging 26%, though the sales guide was trimmed due to Middle East-related headwinds.

FMC beat Q2 EBITDA ($153MM vs. $144MM) but cut full-year EBITDA guidance to $620-680MM from $670-730MM, citing a "more challenging operating environment."
