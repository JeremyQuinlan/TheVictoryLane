◆ TSM TSMC Earnings Beat

TSMC reported Q3 gross margins of 66.4%, just below the 66.5% Street estimate, while operating margins of 58.7% came in roughly 60 basis points ahead of plan. Q4 revenue guidance of $53.3B at the mid-point topped the $50.4B consensus, but margin guidance disappointed with gross margins guided to 66% (in line) and operating margins to 57.5% (below the 58% forecast). Management flagged persistent margin dilution from international and US fab ramps, reiterated 2026 capex of $60-64B, and delivered strongly bullish AI demand commentary while noting weakness in consumer and price-sensitive chip segments.
