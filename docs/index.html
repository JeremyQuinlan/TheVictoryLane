VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ AZO AutoZone Earnings Beat

AutoZone reported fiscal Q4 EPS of $56.05 versus the Street at $54.03, but the beat was low quality — driven by tariff refunds adding 145 basis points to gross margins and a non-cash LIFO benefit adding another 105 basis points. Domestic comps came in at +1.6% versus the Street's +3.1%, though management noted positive sales linearity in the quarter, which is providing some comfort.

▲ MLKN MillerKnoll Earnings Beat

MillerKnoll posted solid fiscal Q1 EPS of 53 cents versus the Street at 36 cents, and critically the beat holds up even excluding tariff refunds — stripping out the 11-cent tailwind still leaves EPS at 42 cents, well above consensus. Guidance is mixed with fiscal 2027 sales trimmed but EPS outlook left unchanged.

▼ THO THOR Industries Earnings Miss

THOR Industries missed fiscal Q4 EPS at 78 cents versus the Street at 87 cents, with gross margins falling 230 basis points year-over-year to 12.4% versus the Street's 13.85%. Revenue came in at $2.311 billion, down 8.4% year-over-year but above the Street's $2.17 billion estimate, and management cited a difficult macro environment.

▲ META Meta Product Launch

Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads ahead of Zuckerberg's keynote at Meta Connect on Wednesday night. Shopify will allow Muse to complete purchases on its platform, expanding the agentic commerce ecosystem, while Amazon has blocked Muse from its site. Investor expectations are growing increasingly bullish into the event.

▲ SHOP Shopify Partnership

Shopify announced it will allow Meta's Muse personal AI agent to complete purchases on its e-commerce platform, positioning itself as a key enabler of agentic commerce. This follows Amazon's decision to block Muse, potentially giving Shopify a competitive advantage in the emerging AI shopping agent ecosystem.

▼ DGX Quest Diagnostics Other

Quest Diagnostics shares were hit Tuesday following new Medicare payment rates from CMS for lab services, with the rate reductions coming in larger than the market had anticipated. This is a sector-wide headwind also hitting LabCorp.

▼ LH LabCorp Other

LabCorp shares are under pressure alongside Quest Diagnostics following CMS announcing Medicare lab service rate cuts that were steeper than expected. The move represents a direct hit to reimbursement revenue for both major lab testing firms.

◆ CGNX Cognex M&A

Cognex announced it will acquire RealSense, described as the market leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and Physical AI, for approximately $500 million upfront plus an additional $106.5 million. The deal expands Cognex's footprint into the Physical AI and robotics perception space.

▲ VKTX Viking Therapeutics FDA Approval

Viking Therapeutics reported positive trial results for its GLP-1 drug VK2735, including 22% weight loss. The data keeps Viking competitive in the increasingly crowded but high-value obesity drug market.

▲ ONON On Holdings Guidance Raise

On Holdings held its analyst meeting and outlined plans to achieve 2029 sales of at least CHF 5.6 billion versus the Street at CHF 5.4 billion, with EBITDA margins of 22% or better versus the Street's 21% estimate. The company also adopted a $1 billion buyback authorization, adding shareholder return credibility to the growth story.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

AI-related names are outperforming broadly as investor excitement builds into the Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night and subsequent catalysts including OpenAI DevDay and Micron earnings. Meta's Muse personal AI agent is seeing surging downloads, and Shopify's integration with Muse for agentic checkout expands the commercial ecosystem. Iran's reported offer to reopen Hormuz within seven days and the partial restart of Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline are easing energy concerns, which could reduce a key macro headwind. BEAR CASE

Financials are broadly under pressure with banks, brokers, insurers, and asset managers all underperforming, which could signal broader caution on economic growth. Lab testing stocks Quest and LabCorp are getting hit hard on larger-than-expected CMS Medicare rate cuts, a reminder of regulatory risk across healthcare. Texas Governor Abbott's permit freeze on new data center projects adds a potential constraint on the AI infrastructure buildout, and SB Energy delaying its IPO on weak investor reception signals some sentiment cooling around the data center boom. THOR Industries' earnings miss and commentary about a difficult macro landscape adds a cautionary note on the consumer and industrial side.

Watch those AI events as the key near-term catalysts.
