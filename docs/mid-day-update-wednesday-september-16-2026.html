MARKET OUTLOOK

A rate hike today is the base case and, frankly, the right call — it ends the "will they or won't they" paralysis and, if communicated cleanly, could actually pressure yields lower by reinforcing Fed independence. The risk is Warsh fumbling the presser and sowing confusion, which would hit both asset classes simultaneously. Today's data didn't help: import prices accelerated to +7.0% YoY vs. +6.1% in July, export prices jumped to +8.6%, and the NAHB housing index fell 3 points to a bleak 32. Retail sales for August were a genuine bright spot, but inflation is sticky and the housing sector is cracking. Watch the equal-weight S&P — its flatness today tells you more than the cap-weighted headline.
