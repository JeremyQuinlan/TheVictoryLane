Macro · Rates

US equity futures are rallying Monday morning with S&P +49 points/65 basis points, Nasdaq +110 basis points, and R2K +47 basis points despite a weekend full of negative headlines including escalating Middle East geopolitics and troubling AI news. Asian and European markets are broadly higher, with the primary driver appearing to be optimism around the UN General Assembly in New York this week and hopes for diplomatic progress on the Iran conflict. Brent crude is sliding 170 basis points to around $102.10, gold is down 60 basis points, and Treasuries have a bid with yields down 2-3 basis points across the curve.

Fed rate expectations are holding fairly steady with the market pricing in roughly 33 basis points of hikes over the final two meetings of the year, with about 50/50 odds of an increase at the October 28th meeting, and an additional 35 basis points expected in 2027. Kansas City Fed President Schmid warned Friday that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil, and Minneapolis Fed President Kashkari echoed those hawkish sentiments Sunday morning. The Treasury is set to issue over $1 trillion in T-bills over the next 12 months as Bessent leans heavily on short-term debt, pushing bills above 24% of marketable debt versus the Treasury's own 20% target.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Nearly all of the market's macro headaches right now trace back to oil, meaning even a modest geopolitical improvement at the UN General Assembly could trigger a sharp drop in energy prices with powerful knock-on benefits — lower yields, less hawkish monetary policy, margin relief, and a consumer confidence boost. Trump signaled openness to meeting with Iran's president at the General Assembly, and China and Pakistan are reportedly pressuring Iran to rein in the Houthis, suggesting global alarm is building toward a potential de-escalation. The Trump/Xi summit Thursday could also deliver modest tariff reductions and a trade truce extension, adding another pillar of positive sentiment.

BEAR CASE

The weekend news flow was overwhelmingly negative — Houthis launched a brazen missile and drone attack on Saudi Arabia, Ukraine hit Russia with its largest drone strike of the war, and a cascade of troubling AI headlines hit after Friday's close including a delayed Anthropic IPO, underwhelming Anthropic 2027 ARR projections, massive OpenAI cash burn, and Oracle data center debt problems. Monetary policy globally remains pointed hawkish with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all having hiked this month and multiple additional central bank hikes priced in coming weeks. The US-China trade truce extension was not reached Sunday and Washington is expected to ratchet aggregate tariffs on Chinese imports back toward pre-SCOTUS IEEPA levels in the coming weeks.

Market Outlook

Adam's lean is cautiously bullish for today's session driven entirely by geopolitical optionality — specifically the idea that even a small diplomatic breakthrough at the UN General Assembly could trigger an outsized drop in oil and unleash a cascade of bullish macro effects including lower yields, reduced inflation pressure, and improved consumer confidence. The key catalyst to watch is any signal from Trump-Iran talks or broader Middle East diplomacy at the General Assembly this week, alongside the Trump/Xi summit Thursday.
