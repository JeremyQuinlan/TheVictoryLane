VK · Earnings Whispers
◆ ANTH Anthropic Other

Anthropic's IPO has been pushed to November from October, with year-end ARR projections of greater than 100 billion dollars per the NYT and greater than 110 billion dollars per the WSJ, below some bull expectations of 120 billion dollars or higher. The company is targeting a raise of over 100 billion dollars at a greater than 2 trillion dollar valuation when it goes public. Momentum concerns have grown as OpenAI's Astra launch has driven a sharp inflection in OpenAI growth relative to Anthropic over recent weeks, prompting Anthropic to consider releasing a new powerful model ahead of the IPO.

▼ ORCL Oracle Other

Oracle was the subject of a negative Financial Times article Friday focusing on poor price action in debt tied to its massive Project Jupiter data center facility in New Mexico. Efforts to offload loans tied to the facility have reportedly hit a wall, raising concerns about Oracle's data center financing strategy.

◆ PSKY Paramount M&A

Paramount is in advanced settlement talks with California's attorney general to resolve a lawsuit against its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. As part of a potential settlement, Paramount could agree to preserve CNN's editorial independence and divest certain cable channels including Comedy Central.

◆ WBD Warner Bros. Discovery M&A

Paramount's advanced settlement talks with California over the Warner deal could clear a key regulatory hurdle for the merger. The potential conditions — preserving CNN's editorial independence and divesting cable assets like Comedy Central — represent a meaningful concession but may allow the deal to proceed.

▼ WEN Wendy's Other

Wendy's saw pressure Friday after one of its largest franchisees, Meritage Hospitality Group, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. This raises concerns about franchisee financial health and potential near-term headwinds to Wendy's royalty revenue stream.

◆ KR Kroger Other

Kroger is dropping Red Bull from its stores as the grocery giant pushes back against supplier price hikes. This reflects broader grocery industry pressure on consumer staples suppliers and underscores retailer leverage in the current pricing environment.

◆ NVO Novo Nordisk Activist Entry

Novo Nordisk is facing shareholder pressure to diversify away from its heavy GLP-1 exposure through M&A activity. The pressure signals growing investor concern about concentration risk in the company's pipeline and revenue base.

▲ BA Boeing Contract Win

Boeing is in talks with NASA for its Starliner spacecraft to be used on ten or more new flights into low-Earth orbit, a potentially meaningful contract win that could help rehabilitate the program after its troubled history. This represents a direct competitive development against SpaceX.

▲ ACN Accenture Partnership

Accenture will embed a team of evaluators to monitor safety and alignment at Anthropic, a modest but notable partnership in the AI safety space. The arrangement signals growing enterprise involvement in AI governance frameworks.

▼ VW Volkswagen Guidance Cut

Volkswagen issued a large downside preannouncement for 2026 operating margins on Friday, now guiding to up to 1% versus the Street's approximately 4.1% estimate and prior guidance of 4 to 5.5%. The miss was driven by a goodwill impairment charge at Porsche, a challenging auto market especially in China, and additional restructuring expenses, though adjusted margins of roughly 4% are broadly in line with consensus.

Macro · Rates

Weekend news flow was net negative, driven by escalating Middle East tensions and a cluster of negative AI industry headlines. The Houthis dramatically ratcheted up missile and drone attacks on major Saudi Arabian cities over the weekend, worsening an already deteriorating geopolitical landscape. Ukraine also launched a massive drone strike on Russia including Moscow, while the Pentagon appears stretched thin across multiple conflict fronts.

Global monetary policy remains pointed in a hawkish direction, with the BOJ, ECB, and FOMC all hiking this month. The BOJ's most recent hike was slightly more dovish than expected, but paradoxically sparked macro concern by pressuring the yen lower and rekindling fears about Japan potentially selling Treasuries to defend its currency. Kansas City Fed President Schmid made hawkish remarks Friday warning that elevated inflation is being driven by more than just oil, and markets are pricing greater than 50% odds of further hikes from multiple central banks including the FOMC on October 28.

Bull / Bear
BULL CASE

Even a small improvement in Middle East geopolitics would likely trigger an outsized drop in energy prices, with favorable knock-on effects including lower yields, reduced inflation, better margins, and improved consumer confidence. The Trump/Xi summit on Thursday could produce modest tariff cuts on roughly 30 billion dollars worth of goods, a constructive signal for risk assets. China and Pakistan are reportedly pressuring Iran to rein in the Houthis, suggesting growing international alarm that could push toward a negotiated solution. The UN General Assembly brings together officials from key Middle East countries including Iran, creating at least the possibility of diplomatic progress.

BEAR CASE

The geopolitical landscape is quite grim — Iran is tightening its grip on global energy supplies via Hormuz, aligned militias in Iraq, and the Houthis, who are now escalating attacks on Saudi Arabian cities. The AI industry faced a significant negative news dump over the weekend including a delayed Anthropic IPO, disappointing ARR projections, OpenAI's staggering negative 280 billion dollar free cash flow projection through 2030, and rising off-balance sheet AI liability exposure for Big Tech up roughly 300 billion dollars in the last year alone. Energy price spikes will begin working through the system and could trigger demand destruction, and hopes for Middle East diplomatic progress at the UN General Assembly may simply prove to be wishful thinking.

Market Outlook

Adam's leaning is cautiously bearish heading into the week, with the negative AI headlines and Middle East escalation creating headwinds. The key catalysts to watch are the Trump/Xi summit on Thursday September 24 — which could deliver modest tariff relief — and the UN General Assembly running September 22-28, where any diplomatic progress on Iran would be a major positive surprise for markets.
