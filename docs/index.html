Macro · Outlook · Key Names
Macro

US equity indices are mixed at midday with the SPX flat, Nasdaq up 38 basis points, R2K up 30 basis points, and the Dow down 43 basis points. Markets are in a holding pattern as investors watch UN diplomatic developments around Iran and the Middle East, with Brent crude off modestly near $100 on hopes for a Hormuz resolution and the partial resumption of Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline. Treasuries are pretty flat across the curve, while gold, silver, and bitcoin are all modestly lower.

Market Outlook

The market is in a wait-and-see mode, with the major indices treading water. The directional lean is neutral-to-cautiously bullish on AI and tech into a dense catalyst window over the next 10 days — Zuckerberg's Meta Connect keynote Wednesday night, OpenAI DevDay on September 29th, and Micron earnings on September 30th. Watch those AI events as the key near-term catalysts.

Sector Watch

Financials broadly underperforming — banks, brokers, insurers, and asset managers all lower. Lab testing names hit hard on CMS Medicare rate cuts steeper than expected. AI and semiconductor-linked tech names outperforming as the market looks ahead to Meta Connect, OpenAI DevDay, and Micron earnings. Housing names showing strength. Travel and leisure names also outperforming. Consumer staples catching a bid. Texas Governor Abbott's data center permit freeze adds a cloud over the AI infrastructure buildout narrative. Fertilizer stocks facing headwinds as Belarus downplays any potash deal with the White House.
